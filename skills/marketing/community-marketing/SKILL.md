## The rules

1. **Build around shared identity and member value first.** Define who members are and what they get from one another. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing]
2. **Seed activity before opening publicly:** recruit 20 to 50 founding members by hand and seed 5 to 10 posts. These are the source's launch choices. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing]
3. **Choose the model and role for the stage,** then maintain a weekly member loop, onboarding and rituals. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing]
4. **Disclose ambassador benefits and monitor the relationship** under the [existing disclosure rules](COMMUNITY.md#ambassadors). [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ, https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking]
5. **Read health weekly and act on the warning signs,** keeping book/vendor benchmarks attributed and platform-history limits explicit. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing; COMMUNITY.md]

## Prove it

The strategy names identity, model, stage, role and weekly loop; launch has founding members and seeded posts; ambassadors receive disclosure instructions and monitoring; support history is retained appropriately; and the five health metrics and warning signs are reviewed. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing; COMMUNITY.md#prove-it]
