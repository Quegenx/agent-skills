# Draft from the user's saved references

Build a human review queue from the user's own saved posts and established voice. Source: [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md and references]. Existing [draft-first publishing and collection rules](SKILL.md#the-rules) apply; this workflow does not authorize engagement automation or posting.

## Establish the account and voice evidence

- Use only the task's authorized read-only view, official API or user export. A logged-in account or a bookmark page is an input surface, not permission to post. If the approved view cannot be reached, report the access gap rather than bypassing it. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md, Start; existing social-media SKILL.md, The rules]
- Learn from the user's own writing, not from the quoted author's post or a generated draft. Select a representative recent mix of original posts, replies and quote-post commentary, recording the corpus date, source and coverage. The source targets 100 recent user-authored posts and asks for the actual shortage when fewer are accessible; the existing personal-voice method remains in [anti-slop-copy VOICE.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/VOICE.md#personal-voice). [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md; write-like-meng-on-x/SKILL.md, voice-corpus workflow]
- Separate a post's publication date from its bookmark/save date; an old published post can have been saved recently. Do not manufacture the unavailable date. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md, Collect Bookmarks]

## Select the angle before drafting

- Read the complete source post and enough context to know what it claims. Keep its URL, author, available dates and media/context evidence. Expand truncated text when available rather than treating a clipped preview as complete. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md, Collect Bookmarks; Draft]
- Choose a distinct useful angle: an observed implication, specific addition, supported disagreement, practical application or connection to the user's actual work. Do not merely paraphrase the original or invent the user's experiences, metrics, endorsements or personal relationships. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md, Draft]
- The source's working preset scans roughly 30 days of source-post publication dates in the bookmark feed, selects 12 to 20 candidates and drafts 7 to 10. Treat these as adjustable queue sizes, not proof of exhaustive account coverage. Preserve the requested count and disclose shortages or uninspected saves. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md, Collect Bookmarks; Report]
- Match the user's observed format, sentence habits, specificity and tone. Avoid repeated artificial hooks, forced jokes, generic praise and engagement bait. A named creator's biography, commercial claims and distinctive persona are not reusable sample identity. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md; write-like-meng-on-x/SKILL.md and references/voice-profile.md; repository identity boundary]

## Return an actionable draft queue

For each kept item, give the source URL, angle, actual quote-post draft and a concise reason it fits now and the next specificity gap or objection to hone. Put the strongest recommended draft in the reply as well as any requested saved queue; a list of possible themes alone does not satisfy a drafting request. Mark uncertainties for the user rather than quietly filling missing facts. Scheduling and publishing remain separately authorized actions. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md, Report; existing social-media SKILL.md, Drafts first]

## Prove it

Every draft has an inspected source URL and an original contribution; the voice corpus distinguishes user-authored commentary from quotations; dates and coverage are honest; no invented personal claim or real-brand sample identity was added; platform limits are checked for the chosen publishing mode; and the queue stays in draft form. [MengTo/Skills, x-bookmark-quote-posts/SKILL.md, Verify; existing social-media PLATFORM-LIMITS.md]
