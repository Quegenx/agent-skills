## Specs

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

| Platform | Length | Aspect ratio | Key difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 15 to 60 s | 9:16 | Trending sounds, raw feel |
| Reels | 15 to 30 s | 9:16 | Polished content, rewards saves and shares |
| Shorts | 30 to 60 s | 9:16 | YouTube search applies; searchable titles |

App-growth's paid spec is "9:16 vertical, 15 to 30 s" `[appeeky]`; lightreel's organic sample found 40 to 75 s talking-head videos the most repeatable ([../creator-marketing/UGC-FORMATS.md](../creator-marketing/UGC-FORMATS.md#talking-head)). Paid and organic differ; test length on your own account.

## The 3-second rule

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]` You have 3 seconds to stop the scroll. Every video needs three simultaneous hooks, all in the first second:

```
[VISUAL HOOK] + [VERBAL HOOK] + [TEXT OVERLAY]
```

## Structures

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

**Problem-Solution (15 to 30 s):**
```
[0-3s]   Hook: State the problem
[3-10s]  Agitate: Why it matters
[10-25s] Solution: Your method/product/tip
[25-30s] CTA: What to do next
```

**List (30 to 60 s):**
```
[0-3s]   Hook: "X things that [outcome]"
[3-50s]  Items: One every 5-8 seconds
[50-60s] CTA
```

**Tutorial (30 to 60 s):**
```
[0-3s]   Hook: Show the end result first
[3-8s]   Overview: "Here's how..."
[8-50s]  Steps: Quick, clear instructions
[50-60s] Result + CTA
```

**Story arc (45 to 60 s):** personal stories, case studies, testimonials.
```
[0-3s]   Hook: Tease the outcome
[3-15s]  Setup: Context and stakes
[15-45s] Journey: What happened
[45-55s] Resolution: The result
[55-60s] Lesson/CTA
```

**POV or skit (15 to 30 s):** relatable content, humor, niche communities.
```
[0-3s]   Setup: Text overlay sets the scene
[3-25s]  Performance: Act out the relatable scenario
[25-30s] Punchline or twist
```

Ideas by business type: SaaS (feature demos with the outcome first, before/after, "Watch me do X in Y seconds"); ecommerce (unboxing, comparisons, how it's made, customer reviews); services (process reveals, client transformations, myth-busting); personal brand (lessons learned, controversial takes, day in the life).

## Captions

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]` Most social video is watched without sound. The source says "Captions increase watch time by 25-40%" (its own figure, unsourced). On-screen captions:

- **At most 2 lines** on screen at once, **3 to 5 words per line**.
- Bold sans-serif with a black outline; highlight key words in a different color.
- Timing matches speech exactly.
- Tools: CapCut, Descript, Captions.ai, Premiere Pro.

## Hook library

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]` Fill each with a specific fact or story; empty superlatives fail the AI-tells check ([POSTS.md](POSTS.md#no-ai-tells)).

- **Curiosity (engagement):** "The secret to [outcome] that nobody talks about" · "I found a feature in [product/platform] that saves me [specific time/result]" · "I can't believe this actually works..." · "I tried [thing] for 30 days and I was NOT expecting this" · "This completely changed how I think about [topic]" · "Nobody talks about this, but..." · "Why does nobody talk about this?" · "Am I the only one who didn't know this?" · "The reason [common thing] doesn't work is..."
- **Value (saves):** "How to [achieve outcome] in [specific timeframe]" · "[Number] [things] that will [benefit]" · "[Topic] in 60 seconds: the [N] things that matter" · "[Outcome] hack that actually works" · "The [adjective] way to [outcome]" · "If you're struggling with [problem], watch this" · "Stop doing [common practice]. It cost me [specific result]." · "[Number] mistakes that are killing your [results]" · "Why [thing you think is good] is actually hurting you"
- **Story (watch time):** "3 months ago, I [bad state]. Today, I [good state]." · "Here's how I went from [before] to [after]" · "I used to think [old belief]. Then [specific event] happened." · "I made a huge mistake with [topic]" · "Here's why I stopped [common practice]" · "I lost [something significant] because of this mistake" · "So this just happened..." · "This cut my [task] from [X] to [Y]" · "Let me tell you about the time I [interesting situation]"
- **Controversial (comments):** "Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]" · "[Common advice] is actually wrong" · "I'm going to get hate for this, but..." · "Most [audience] get this completely wrong"
- **Contrast (education and decisions):** "Should you [option A] or [option B]?" · "We used to [old way]. Now everyone [new way]. Which one actually works?" · "Most [audience] do [X]. The ones who [result] do [Y]." · "You can cancel [thing they think they need]." · "You need [common belief]. Or do you?" Both sides must be real options the viewer weighs, and the video has to pick one.

## Script template

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

```markdown
## Video: [Working Title]

**Platform:** TikTok / Reels / Shorts
**Length:** XX seconds
**Format:** [Talking head / Slideshow / Demo / Screen recording]

### Hook (0-3 sec)
- Visual: [What viewer sees]
- Audio: [What they hear]
- Text overlay: [On-screen text]

### Body (3-X sec)
- [Timestamp] - [What happens/what you say]
- [Timestamp] - [Next beat]

### CTA (final 3-5 sec)
- Verbal: [What you say]
- Text: [On-screen text]
- Action: [Follow, comment, link in bio, etc.]

### Production Notes
- Music/sound: [Trending sound or music choice]
- B-roll needed: [List any clips needed]
- Graphics: [Any text animations or overlays]
```

## Visual patterns

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

- **Talking head:** good lighting (ring light or window light), eye contact with the camera, hand gestures for emphasis, an interesting background.
- **Slideshow video:** a strong visual on each slide (2 to 4 seconds each), text overlays with key points, a consistent style, voiceover or a trending sound.
- **Screen recording:** zoom in on important areas, cursor highlights or click animations, smooth deliberate movement, optionally your face in a corner.
- **B-roll heavy:** show, don't tell; quick cuts (1 to 3 seconds per shot) matched to voiceover beats; mix wide, medium and close-up.

## Audio

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

- **Trending sounds** for entertainment and lifestyle content where the sound fits and the trend is still rising; not when it distracts or is already declining.
- **Original audio** for education, storytimes, product demos and tutorials, and to build a recognizable brand voice.
- **Voiceover:** slightly faster than conversation, varied tone, pauses for emphasis, a quiet room with noise removal; AI voices work for faceless content.
- **Music:** match the energy to the content; avoid copyrighted music on Reels and Shorts and use platform music libraries; duck the music under voiceover.

## Posting

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]` (test against your own audience)

| Platform | Best times (local) |
|---|---|
| TikTok | 7 to 9 AM, 12 to 3 PM, 7 to 11 PM |
| Reels | 9 AM, 12 PM, 7 to 9 PM |
| Shorts | 12 to 3 PM, 7 to 9 PM |

| Goal | Minimum | Optimal |
|---|---|---|
| Growing | 1 a day | 2 to 4 a day |
| Maintaining | 3 a week | 1 a day |
| Testing | 2 a week | 5 a week |

**Batching:** ideate 10 to 20 concepts (30 min); script 5 to 10 videos (1 hour); batch-film the talking-head content (2 hours); edit and caption (2 to 3 hours); schedule (30 min).

## Analytics and iteration

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Watch time % | Is the content engaging throughout? |
| Completion rate | Did hook and content deliver? |
| Saves | Is it valuable enough to revisit? |
| Shares | Is it worth spreading? |
| Comments | Did it spark conversation? |
| Follows | Did the viewer want more from you? |

**Test:** hooks (same content, different opening), length (15 vs 30 vs 60 s), format (talking head vs slideshow vs demo), time of day, CTA.

**When to pivot:** 5+ videos under 1% completion, change the hooks; high views but low follows, check the CTA and content-audience fit; high saves but low shares, the content is valuable but not social; many negative comments, the source suggests leaning into the controversy or adjusting tone (keep within [POSTS.md](POSTS.md#no-ai-tells): no rage bait).

## Common mistakes

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

1. **Slow hooks:** don't build up to the point.
2. **No text overlay:** many watch without sound.
3. **Poor audio:** bad audio kills retention instantly.
4. **Too long:** if it can be shorter, make it shorter.
5. **No CTA:** tell viewers what to do.
6. **Ignoring comments:** engagement in the first hour matters.

## Prove it

Every script fills the template with a visual, verbal and on-screen hook in the first second and a structure from this file; the video is a 9:16 composition, not a cropped horizontal cut; captions show at most 2 lines of 3 to 5 words timed to speech; music is from the platform library or licensed; results are read on completion, saves, shares and follows; and after 5 videos under 1% completion the hooks change.
