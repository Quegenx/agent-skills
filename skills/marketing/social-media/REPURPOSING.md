# Repurposing long-form into social

Turn one piece of long-form content into many posts. "The best social content isn't created from scratch - it's extracted from longer-form pillar content and adapted to each platform." Text is from coreyhaines31/marketingskills `social` SKILL.md (MIT). Which flagship pieces to make, and the per-flagship atomization checklist, are in [content-strategy SKILL.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/content-strategy/SKILL.md).

## Podcast or video to social

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]` Extract "content atoms", self-contained moments that work on their own:

| Atom | What to look for | Best platform |
|---|---|---|
| Quotable moment | A bold claim, hot take, or memorable line (15 to 60 s) | X, LinkedIn, TikTok |
| Story arc | A complete mini-story with setup, conflict, resolution (60 to 90 s) | Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts |
| Tactical tip | A specific how-to or framework explained clearly (30 to 60 s) | LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts |
| Controversial take | A contrarian opinion that sparks debate | X, LinkedIn |
| Data or stat callout | A surprising number or research finding | LinkedIn carousel, X |
| Behind the scenes | Authentic, unpolished moments | Instagram Stories, TikTok |

**Podcast workflow:**

1. **Get the transcript:** Whisper, Descript, or your podcast host's transcription.
2. **Mark timestamps:** flag the 5 to 10 best moments while listening or scanning the transcript.
3. **Extract clips** for each moment (Descript, Opus Clip, or by hand).
4. **Write standalone captions:** each clip needs context; don't assume the viewer heard the rest.
5. **Add subtitles:** most social video is watched without sound.
6. **Schedule across 1 to 2 weeks:** spread one episode across many posts.

**Per episode, aim for:** 3 to 5 short clips or audiograms (15 to 60 s) for Reels, TikTok and Shorts; 1 to 2 LinkedIn text posts from key insights; 1 X thread of takeaways; 1 carousel of the main framework or list; 1 newsletter section or blog post from the best segment.

Clip editing (start inside the conflict, result before the workflow, one CTA) is in [creator-marketing UGC-FORMATS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/creator-marketing/UGC-FORMATS.md#clips).

## The workflow

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, social]`

1. **Create pillar content** (blog, video, podcast, webinar, newsletter).
2. **Extract content atoms** (5 to 10 per piece: quotes, stories, tips, data).
3. **Adapt to each platform** (format, length and tone; limits in [PLATFORM-LIMITS.md](PLATFORM-LIMITS.md)).
4. **Write standalone captions:** each post must work without context.
5. **Schedule across the week:** spread distribution, don't dump everything at once.
6. **Update and reshare:** evergreen content can repeat every 3 to 6 months. On X, seen/served filters can remove candidates before ranking; the source's repeat interval is a publishing heuristic ([X-RANKING.md](X-RANKING.md#structure-and-predicted-outcomes)).

Horizontal video recut for TikTok is on the source's "What doesn't" list ("Repurposed horizontal video"); each vertical cut is its own composition.

## Prove it

Each long-form piece yields 5 to 10 named atoms, each mapped to a platform and format; every caption makes sense to someone who never saw the source; every clip has subtitles; one episode's posts are spread across 1 to 2 weeks; and no evergreen rerun repeats within 3 months.
