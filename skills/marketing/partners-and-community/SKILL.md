# Partners and community

Both plays borrow trust: a partner lends an audience that already listens to it, and a community lets customers vouch for you to each other. "The best partners share your audience but don't compete for the same budget"; the strongest communities form around who members are, not around the product. ## The rules

1. **Pick partners who share the buyer, not the budget:** same persona with a different problem, adjacent in the workflow (before, after or alongside you), similar stage and customer size, complementary not competitive. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, co-marketing]
2. **Route the request to the right partnership type** before defaulting to joint content ([PARTNERSHIP-TYPES.md](PARTNERSHIP-TYPES.md)). [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, co-marketing]
3. **Partner outreach is commercial email:** a physical postal address and a working opt-out in every message, with "no exception for business-to-business email" under CAN-SPAM; in Canada, consent first ([PARTNER-MARKETING.md](PARTNER-MARKETING.md#outreach)). [https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business]
4. **Agree lead ownership in writing before launch,** and tell registrants at signup who receives their details ([PARTNER-MARKETING.md](PARTNER-MARKETING.md#the-agreement)). [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, co-marketing]
5. **Build the community around a shared identity, and let value flow to members first.** "Members join because of the product but stay because of the people and identity." [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing]
6. **Don't open a community publicly before there is baseline activity:** recruit 20 to 50 founding members by hand and seed 5 to 10 posts first. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing]
7. **Anyone you give something to (ambassadors, giveaway entrants, partners' promoters) discloses it clearly,** in words like "#XYZ_Ambassador" or "Gifted by XYZ", and you monitor them ([COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md#ambassadors)). [https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking]
8. **Read community health weekly** and act on the warning signs, not on member count ([COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md#health-metrics)). [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, community-marketing]

## Prove it

Each shortlisted partner has a score on all six criteria and a named overlap; every outreach email carries a postal address and an opt-out and went to a Canadian contact only with consent; the agreement covers all eight parts including lead handling; the community has a written identity, a weekly core loop and seeded posts before opening; every ambassador and contest brief includes the disclosure wording; the weekly health read has all five metrics; and each file's own Prove it checks pass for the parts you used.
