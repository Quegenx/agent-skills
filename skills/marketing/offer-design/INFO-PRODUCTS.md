# Research, validate and sell a paid knowledge product

An ordered paid-product guide from [more skills, info-product-playbook.md]: discover a problem, validate it, build the smallest complete solution, distribute complete useful answers and invite an opted-in audience to return. This is a paid guide, template pack, system or course workflow. A free chapter is an acquisition option, not a reason to move the paid-product job into lead-magnets. Read the shared [.agents/product-context.md contract](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/CONTEXT.md) first.

## Choose the entry path

[more skills, info-product-playbook.md, prerequisite and running this skill]

- **No topic:** start with a niche the user knows through real experience. Research and rank candidate problems before choosing one.
- **Topic selected:** run validation before building. Be willing to reject the topic and return to the next candidate.
- **Product exists:** inspect its actual distribution, missing channels and permission-based email audience before proposing another product.

Return concrete artifacts as the work progresses: research file, ranked candidates, validation drafts, product outline, channel-by-channel content calendar and email sequence. Use the user's actual knowledge, figures and stories.

## Find repeated problems with evidence of spend

[more skills, info-product-playbook.md, phase 1]

Keep one research record per finding: problem, exact audience wording, source link and evidence of money being spent. Group related findings and rank the clusters on three separate questions: does the problem recur across people, do people pay for an alternative, and do those alternatives attract repeated specific complaints? A crowded category or visible price alone does not establish profit.

Use the existing [source playbooks](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/customer-research/SOURCES.md) for collection and evidence handling. The source proposes these topic-discovery exercises when the channel fits:

| Input | Look for |
|---|---|
| Relevant community questions | Repeated questions, explicit willingness-to-pay/course requests and dissatisfaction with current products |
| Unanswered questions | A specific recurring problem whose existing answers leave a gap |
| Permitted manual group observation | Author exercise: observe three to five relevant groups over a few weeks and record repeated questions |
| Book reviews | Five-star descriptions of the desired transformation, alongside three-star descriptions of missing coverage |
| Comparable paid products | Actual spend evidence and recurring complaints about the closest alternatives |
| Tutorial questions | Follow-up requests and recurring confusion about particular steps |
| Buyer-search language | Natural problem wording to carry into useful content and titles |

**Access correction:** the source suggested unrestricted RSS/third-party collection. Reddit requires approved API access and explicit written approval for commercial use. Its claimed no-key route and fixed request allowance are not adopted. Use permitted inputs under the existing research owner, rather than treating a public endpoint as permission. [https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/42728983564564-Responsible-Builder-Policy]

## Validate before production

[more skills, info-product-playbook.md, phase 2]

The author's gate is at least two of the following four signals before building. It is a practitioner decision rule, not statistical proof of demand. Keep each artifact and the actual observations separately:

1. **Existing payment:** document competing products, services or tools people actually pay for. Distinguish a visible price or review count from an observed purchase.
2. **A complete useful post:** draft and, only with publication approval, share an executable answer where the audience gathers. Record reactions, actual questions and requested conversations. Interest and an inquiry are not a completed sale.
3. **A one-page presell:** describe the proposed product and offer a buy or waitlist action. Record signups separately from paid preorders. The source claimed refusal to leave an email proves refusal to pay; keep those observations distinct.
4. **Competitor-gap exercise:** read the available reviews of the two closest products and check whether the plan addresses the top three repeated complaints. Record the source's exercise scope and any unavailable evidence rather than implying an exhaustive review.

If the gate fails, choose the next researched cluster. Do not turn an unvalidated topic into a long production project merely because an outline is already drafted.

## Build the smallest complete solution

[more skills, info-product-playbook.md, phase 3]

Choose the format by what the buyer needs to do: a guide for an ordered knowledge/system task; templates, swipes or spreadsheets for a usable artifact; footage when the task genuinely needs a visual walkthrough. The source favors a complete small first version over an unfinished large course, without establishing a universal time or price advantage.

Open with the actual outcome and audience. Put the actions in execution order, with enough detail to perform them. Include common mistakes and the branches for when a step fails. End with the author's five-action quick start. The user verifies every drafted claim, number and step against real expertise and supplies their own stories and data; an agent draft is not that verification.

Use existing [value-based pricing](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/pricing-strategy/SKILL.md) and [offer components](OFFER-ANATOMY.md). The source's optional ladder is a complete core product, a higher tier with usable tools/templates/extras, then later access or updates. Price research comes from the actual alternatives and buyer conversations, not page count. Existing guarantee, discount and renewal rules remain in their canonical owners.

Start with an existing checkout suited to the actual product rather than building custom checkout for an unvalidated offer. Confirm the selected delivery mechanism as well as payment: a payment link alone does not establish that a buyer receives the digital asset. The source's named-provider capabilities are not verified by this guide. Checkout mechanisms remain in [the Stripe payment owner](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/stripe/PAYMENTS.md). [more skills, info-product-playbook.md]

## Distribute complete answers

[more skills, info-product-playbook.md, phase 4]

The free answer should work on its own. The paid product offers organization, completeness and ready-made artifacts for a person who wants the shortcut. Reuse the actual questions found in research rather than inventing a new content calendar disconnected from demand.

- **Community posts and inbound replies:** use the complete-method and conversation workflow in [social-media REDDIT.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/social-media/REDDIT.md). Keep human publication approval and community rules.
- **Question sites:** answer the actual research questions completely over time. On Quora, disclose relevant affiliations, make the answer understandable without leaving the site, and do not use affiliate links. The source's relevant end-link suggestion is conditional on these rules, not a platform exemption. [https://help.quora.com/hc/en-us/articles/9456583756180-Question-and-Answer-Policies]
- **Groups:** give useful permitted answers, respecting the individual group's link and promotion rules; a private group is not automatically a permissible collection source.
- **Search wording:** use natural buyer terms when they fit the answer and channel. The source promised durable rankings and sales; those outcomes are not guaranteed. [https://developers.google.com/search/help/crawling-index-faq]
- **Requested conversations:** answer the person's situation fully before offering the relevant product as an optional short route. Keep the proposal brief and low-pressure; this is not authorization for unsolicited messaging.
- **Proof file:** retain real attributable buyer results, screenshots and thanks with their actual context and permission. Route proof claims to the existing copy/research rules; do not turn inquiries into revenue or create sample testimonials.

## Invite the audience back

[more skills, info-product-playbook.md, phase 5]

Invite buyers and interested correspondents to a clearly described newsletter, optionally with a chapter, template or promised update. The source says the incentive makes the ask natural; it does not establish automatic enrollment. Follow the existing [capture and consent owner](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/email-practices/LISTS-AND-CAPTURE.md) for permission, confirmation and suppression.

Plan useful niche emails with relevant product/tier invitations within what subscribers agreed to receive. The source's recurring earnings and easier-second-launch anecdotes are not a subscription contract, forecast or guaranteed result.

## Prove it

[more skills, info-product-playbook.md, failure modes and running this skill]

- The research file preserves actual wording, URLs and spend evidence, and the chosen cluster has an explicit repetition/spend/gap rationale.
- Validation shows which of the author's four signals were observed, with signups, inquiries and purchases separate; a failed gate returns to research.
- The outline is executable in order, includes failure paths and the five-action quick start, and the user has checked the specific claims and stories.
- The content calendar gives complete useful answers, the product shortcut is optional, and the proof file contains actual attributable evidence.
- Delivery is confirmed separately from checkout; the email sequence respects the actual opt-in promise. Report unrun delivery, launch or billing work as unverified.
