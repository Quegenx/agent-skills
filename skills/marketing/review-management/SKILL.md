## The rules

1. **Read `.agents/product-context.md` and choose the request, response, triage or reuse mode** before drafting. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, referrals; REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#the-task-and-the-mode]
2. **Request reviews by an actual experience milestone, never sentiment:** NPS and CSAT do not decide who gets an invitation. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, referrals; REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#requesting-reviews]
3. **Check the actual platform's rules.** No incentives on Google Maps or Trustpilot; any proposed G2/Capterra program needs current eligibility, disclosure and cap verification. [REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#platform-rules, named official policies and source-only qualifications]
4. **Keep public replies factual and private records private;** assign an owner and follow through. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, referrals; REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#replying-to-a-review]
5. **Moderate only on actual policy evidence,** and reuse a quote only with its rights, approved attribution and disclosures recorded. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, referrals; REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#moderation; REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#reusing-reviews-as-evidence]
6. **Drafts are not sends or publications.** Apply the user's authorization before outward actions. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, referrals]

## Prove it

Review invitations use a neutral milestone; no positive sentiment is bought or criticism suppressed; replies expose no private data or invented resolution; reused quotes have permission records and preserve meaning; and the selected mode passes [the playbook's checks](REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#prove-it).
