# Marketing skills

Marketing, SEO and GEO for web and SaaS products: getting found in Google (`seo`, `keyword-research`, `search-console`) and in AI answers (`ai-search-visibility`), auditing and structuring a site (`site-audit`, `site-architecture`), content and research (`content-strategy`, `comparison-pages`, `customer-research`), conversion and measurement (`cro`, `ab-testing`, `marketing-attribution`), paid and social reach (`paid-ads`, `ad-creative`, `creator-marketing`, `social-media`), outbound and sales (`cold-outreach`, `prospecting`, `sales-enablement`, `revops`), launches and earned attention (`product-launch`, `public-relations`, `partners-and-community`, `event-marketing`), leads and loops (`lead-magnets`, `referral-programs`), money (`pricing-strategy`, `offer-design`, `churn-prevention`, `onboarding-and-upgrades`), channels and media (`email-marketing`, `sms-marketing`, `marketing-images`, `marketing-video`), planning (`marketing-plan`, `marketing-loops`, `marketing-council`, `growth-ideas`), and complete client-service playbooks (`service-businesses`). Every skill reads the shared product context at `.agents/product-context.md`, defined in anti-slop-copy's CONTEXT.md. Mobile app growth stays in the mobile set's `app-growth` and `aso`; Google Analytics is in the monitoring set and cookie consent in the frontend set. Details in the [main README](../../README.md#marketing).
