# Format tiers

A prioritized S to F map of about 51 Meta ad formats, built to answer "which format do I make next", not as an encyclopedia. From [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, ad-creative, meta-creative-formats.md] (MIT), adapted from Dara Denney's "I Ranked 51 Meta Ad Creative Types (Tier List)" with craft from Oren John. It is one practitioner's point-in-time ranking: Meta's delivery and format fatigue shift, so re-check against your own account data. How to build each format: [STATIC-TEMPLATES.md](STATIC-TEMPLATES.md), [SHORT-FORM-VIDEO.md](SHORT-FORM-VIDEO.md), [MOTION-VIDEO.md](MOTION-VIDEO.md), [NATIVE-REVEAL-VIDEO.md](NATIVE-REVEAL-VIDEO.md).

## The one question

For any format: **is it a unicorn scaler or a supporting cast member?**

- **Unicorn scaler:** reaches cold, net-new audiences and holds up as spend scales. Rare; worth disproportionate investment.
- **Supporting cast:** converts people already mid or low funnel. Necessary, but it won't open new audiences however much you spend.

A format is "bad" only when you expect it to scale cold and it structurally can't. Build a portfolio: a few scalers doing the reaching, a bench converting.

**Why creator-fronted formats top the list.** The source's explanation, practitioner and not in Meta's docs: Meta's Andromeda delivery is persona-based, and a creator reaches a persona natively through people who already follow and trust them; a partnership ad's seed audience starts from the creator's own. Watch rolling month-over-month reach: when it falls, deploy creator-fronted formats, especially partnership ads ([paid-ads META-WEB-ADS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/paid-ads/META-WEB-ADS.md#rolling-reach)).

**Production complexity:** Low = copy and one asset, makeable today (statics, founder's letter); Med = needs a creator, shoot, script or edit (yapper, green screen, VSL script); High = multi-party, rights or heavy production (celebrity, warehouse shoot, AI animation, press).

## S-tier: unicorn scalers

| Format | Role | Complexity | When to use or skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Founder content** | Cold scaler | Low to Med | The reliable first winner at any production level: the story of why you built it connects with same-problem buyers. Use early, before you have proven creative. |
| **Partnership ads** | Cold scaler | Med | The source's top investment priority and best path to net-new reach. Use always; deploy when rolling reach drops. Skip only if you can't source creators. Setup and disclosure: [creator-marketing](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/creator-marketing/SKILL.md). |
| **VSL (video sales letter)** | Cold scaler | Med to High | For anything that needs education first (health, wellness, fitness, complex mechanisms). Skip for impulse products. The script is the ad. |

When you contract creators for partnership ads, also have each shoot a few low-fi statics (how they'd post a Story for the brand): a mini-funnel per creator at near-zero extra cost.

## A-tier: scales up well

| Format | Role | Complexity | When to use or skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amateur investigation** | Cold scaler | Med | A creator investigates your product or niche (visiting competitors, testing claims). For categories where skepticism is the barrier. |
| **Yapper** | Cold scaler | Med | A creator talks to camera with a personal story. High ceiling, hard to nail (creator + script + setting); skip if you can't cast well. |
| **David & Goliath** | Cold-capable | Low to Med | The brand as underdog against an incumbent or broken norm. Needs a real villain. |
| **Grid statics** | Cold-capable | Low | A multi-product, SKU or bundle grid. Easy, and the source's lowest-hanging test when you have several products. |
| **Authority** | Cold scaler | Med | A real doctor, dermatologist or expert fronts it, for trust-gated niches; claims substantiated and legally reviewed. |
| **Green-screen commentary** | Cold-capable | Med | A creator over the content they comment on, with an educational angle; strong in apparel. |
| **Catalog / DPA** | Cold-capable | Low to Med | Dynamic product ads can prospect cold, not only retarget; needs a real catalog. |

## B-tier: solid supporting cast

Converts mid-funnel reliably; occasionally joins the top rotation with great messaging. Mostly Low complexity: TikTok love letter · real short (top-of-funnel support, Med) · callout ads · before/after (mid-funnel; claims are regulated in health, finance and beauty) · progression · tweet or Reddit statics (strong as a first frame; real posts only) · headline ads (need excellent messaging, pair with callouts) · us vs them (often in a brand's top 8) · lifestyle Story-style statics (mirror selfies, flat lays) · creator low-fi statics · objection handling (works fast) · founder's letter static (strong during sales) · conversation ads (Med, hard to execute) · educational infographics (read as content; under-used) · mood board (apparel) · comment reply · challenging your beliefs (Med; needs known persona beliefs) · ugly, handwritten or post-it (strong during sales periods).

## C-tier: situational

Can win in narrow conditions, cost more than they return for most: AI animation (High; reaches new pockets at first, rarely holds) · statistics ads (luxury and retail on awareness or traffic objectives, not DTC ROI) · celebrity (High) · AI avatar (the source says it scaled with legal disclaimers but is being phased out for real creators; disclosure rules in [MOTION-VIDEO.md](MOTION-VIDEO.md#compliance)) · warehouse (High) · street interview (see the note below) · duet, reaction or stitch (needs the original creator's rights) · ASMR (needs ASMR creators) · regular UGC (works, with general fatigue on manufactured problem-solution voiceover over b-roll).

## D-tier: rarely moves the needle

Breaking-news ads · AI billboard (overdone) · GRWM and day-in-my-life (organic-native; scales on paid only for morning-routine products).

## E-tier: mostly skip

Text-only (usually bland AI copy; except a founder's letter during sales) · testimonial statics (only with a genuinely specific, verbatim golden-nugget review) · listicles (the source says dead lately) · carousels (the source says rarely worth the extra assets).

## F-tier: don't bother

- **Press ads:** a rights and permissions problem; outlets pursue unlicensed logo use. If you have licensed coverage, one quote inside another format is safer than a logo wall (the source's Press Mention template).
- **Podcast ads:** a waste unless the founder is on a genuinely well-known show; a rented studio or AI-generated "podcast" clip doesn't pay.
- **Notes-app and UX fake-native ads:** familiar UI stops everyone, so it fails to qualify the right people; the source says they "do not convert". The source's own [NATIVE-REVEAL-VIDEO.md](NATIVE-REVEAL-VIDEO.md) material recommends an Apple Notes reveal; that is an internal conflict in the source, left for your own account data to settle.

## Cross-cutting principles

- **Portfolio, not silver bullet.** In this ranking only founder, partnership, authority, investigation, VSL and grid statics reliably scale cold; staff both roles.
- **Recreated scenes are dramatizations.** The source says street interviews and duets are "often better to fake/recreate". Recreate them only as clearly staged scenes with actors, never presented as candid passers-by or real customers: the FTC's rule prohibits testimonials by "someone who does not exist ... or who did not have actual experience" (16 CFR Part 465, https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials).
- **Fatigue is real** on over-taught formats (manufactured UGC, notes-app, AI billboards); a novel but honest format beats a saturated best practice.

## Prove it

For a DTC supplement account with flat reach that can make anything: the answer uses the scaler vs supporting-cast lens, ties falling reach to partnership ads, picks S-tier formats (founder, partnership, VSL) and a fitting A-tier one (authority, grid statics) with the health-claims review caveat, explicitly drops press, podcast and notes-app formats, and frames it as a portfolio [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, ad-creative, evals #14, #15].
