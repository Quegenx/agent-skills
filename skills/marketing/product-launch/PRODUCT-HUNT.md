# Product Hunt

The one place in this repo that defines how to launch on Product Hunt. Every fact here is from Product Hunt's own launch guide and help center unless tagged otherwise; where coreyhaines31/marketingskills `launch` or the app-growth guide [eronred] says something different, the official form is used and the difference is listed in [Where other guides differ](#where-other-guides-differ). Product Hunt changes its rules and forms; check the live submit form before trusting a limit.

## Is it a fit

[https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works] Products "can be anything from mobile apps to innovative showerheads". A good fit is:

- **New,** or a substantive update to an existing product.
- **A product,** not a blog post or a list.
- **Usable:** "Closed beta launches are usually not a good fit."
- **From a trustworthy maker,** with unique value.

The source skill's view [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, launch]: pros are an early-adopter audience, a credibility bump and possible press and backlinks; cons are heavy competition, short-lived traffic spikes and significant preparation.

## Accounts and hunting

- **Personal accounts only:** "Company accounts cannot hunt or post products" [https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product], and Product Hunt doesn't "allow company or brand accounts to post products or comment" [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works].
- **New accounts "need to wait 1 week before posting a product"**, and must complete onboarding first; Product Hunt "highly recommend[s] joining the platform well ahead of your launch (three months or more)". [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/before-launch; https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product]
- **Co-makers need their own accounts** (usernames) before launch day to be credited and to comment. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch]
- **Self-hunt.** "Do I need someone specific to hunt my product? ... Nope. Hunting is just posting." Product Hunt reports that "79% of featured posts were by makers who self-hunted" and "60% of #1 Product of the Day winners were self-hunted". [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/before-launch] The help center: "We recommend posting your own product. It's also not necessary to have a Hunter." [https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product]
- **Notifications follow the maker, not the hunter:** "The Hunter's followers do not receive e-mail notifications of the post ... The Maker's followers will receive an e-mail notification if the post ends up on the homepage." [https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product]

## Goals

[https://www.producthunt.com/launch/before-launch] "You should have measurable goals." Product Hunt's menu: leaderboard position, upvotes, comments, followers, buzz, investors, traffic, sales or leads, feedback, hiring, brand. Product of the Day is "determined via a confidential algorithm ... using upvotes, time since posting, and a number of other factors", and "many products that do not achieve this rank" had successful launches, so don't make it the only goal. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works]

## Day, time and scheduling

- **Day:** "there is no golden-ticket answer". Larger companies tend to launch on weekdays; "Weekends ... can be great for smaller teams, side projects, and personal apps", and Product Hunt reports that "products launched on the weekend get 15% more 'Visit' button clicks than those on weekdays". [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch]
- **Time:** the "rule of thumb is to schedule your launch for 12:01am PST", because "The homepage runs on a 24-hour cycle based on Pacific Standard Time". Exceptions: match a press embargo (embargo Friday 8am PT, launch Friday 8am PT), a European, Asian or African startup may benefit from launching earlier in the day, or launch when the team is online to respond together. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch]
- **Schedule ahead:** "You can now schedule your launch up to 1 month in advance so that you can tease it, drive traffic, and collect followers well before your big day." Without a date yet, use "Create draft"; drafts auto-save. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch]

## The listing

[https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch; https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product]

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| URL | The direct product page (an App Store or GitHub link is allowed). "Shortened links (e.g. bit.ly) and track links (e.g. Google UTMs) are not accepted." |
| Name | The name only: no description, no emojis |
| Tagline | At most 60 characters, no gimmicks ("NOT allowed: 'The most ameozing app in the app store'"); write it with [anti-slop-copy SKILL.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/SKILL.md) |
| Links | App Store, Google Play and others, optional; the X handle is the product's |
| Description | The launch guide says "max 500 characters"; the help center says "within 260 characters". The two official pages disagree; the launch guide reads as the newer page. Check the live form; 260 fits both |
| Launch tags | Up to 3 |
| Thumbnail | Required, square, 240x240 recommended, under 3MB. A GIF animates only on hover, so its first frame must work alone; no strobing or quick cuts. "Less than a third of Product of the Day products used an animated GIF" |
| Gallery | At least 2 images, 1270x760 recommended |
| Video | Optional; YouTube links only, not private, the full URL. "About 53% of products that reached Product of the Day since 2021 include a video" |
| Interactive demo | Product Hunt names Arcade, Storylane, Hexus, Supademo, Layerpath and ScreenSpace and says "all are free for Product Hunt launches" |
| Shoutouts | 3 per launch |
| Pricing | Free, paid, or paid with a free trial or plan |
| Status | Mark beta or not available yet |
| Promo | Offer, code and expiration date are all required |

Gallery and thumbnail images can be made with [marketing-images SKILL.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/SKILL.md).

## The first comment

[https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch] "70% of products who achieved Product of the Day, Week, or Month had a first comment by the maker." It can be written at submission. Include:

- Key features and who it's for.
- Your story and your goals.
- Pricing or the launch offer.
- "Ask for feedback (NOT upvotes)".

Keep it humble, with no marketing-speak, and reply to comments using the product itself where you can (Product Hunt's example is a Loom reply).

## What gets a launch removed

- **Paying anyone to hunt or send traffic** "goes against our guidelines and will result in us taking action to remove your product, and you could be permanently banned". [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works]
- **Asking for upvotes.** "Don't ask for upvotes. Ask for feedback, comments, opinions, help spreading the word"; asking specifically for upvotes "is against our policies". [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch]
- **Contests or giveaways that reward upvotes:** "we may unfeature your product". [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch]
- **Mass messaging and unsolicited email:** don't send the same tweet to many people, and don't scrape addresses and send unsolicited emails [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch]. Lawful email rules: [email-practices SKILL.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/email-practices/SKILL.md).
- **Spam in votes or comments:** the leaderboard changes "based on the number of upvotes, comments, time since submission, and other factors" [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works], and a product ranking "dramatically lower than it should be" may have had spam detected in its upvotes or comments. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/launch-day-questions]

## Pre-launch mistakes

[https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch] Product Hunt's list, verbatim:

- "Asking your community to join only on the day of your launch and not before."
- "Not preparing your website or content for launch"
- "Not using all the tools at your disposal to tell your community about your launch early."
- "Failing to set measurable goals, or assuming the only goal that matters is Product of the Day."
- "Scheduling or posting their product too late in the day if you don't have limitations."
- "Paying a hunter or assuming that you need one."

## Launch day

- **One landing page, one call to action,** optionally with a Product Hunt-specific offer (Product Hunt's example: "20% off for 6 months"). Product Hunt badges and embeds come from the Embed link on the Launch Page. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch]
- **Watch the Launch Day dashboard** (position, upvotes, comments, reviews) and respond to comments in real time; split the day into shifts. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/launch-day-duties]
- **Email is "one of your strongest tools"** for telling your own audience. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch]
- **Only post in communities where you have already been active:** "Launch day is not a time to go spamming Reddit and Indie Hackers if you haven't already been posting there". [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch]
- **The Daily Digest** goes to "more than 350,000 readers" every weekday, with the previous day's top 10 most upvoted products plus editorial picks. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/launch-day-duties]
- From the source skill [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, launch]: treat it as an all-day event, answer every comment, and direct traffic back to your site to capture signups (an owned channel).

## After launch day

- **Claim the Product Page** ("Claim this page"); upvoters become its followers. The Launch Page can be promoted for two weeks, then the Product Page; Product of the Week and Product of the Month are still open after the day. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/launch-day-duties; https://www.producthunt.com/launch/days-after-launch]
- **Follow up** with commenters and the emails you captured, remind them of the offer, and collect Product Hunt reviews; review notifications can go to email or Slack. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/days-after-launch; https://www.producthunt.com/launch/two-weeks-post-launch]
- **Product Hunt ads** (a "momentum campaign") are separate from the leaderboard. [https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works]
- From the source skill [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, launch]: convert Product Hunt traffic into email signups and keep momentum with post-launch content ([LAUNCH-PLAN.md](LAUNCH-PLAN.md#after-the-launch)).

## Relaunching

[https://www.producthunt.com/launch/two-weeks-post-launch] Posting again within 6 months of the last launch brings up a pop-up asking whether it is a major update. A game-changing update can be resubmitted through the request form, with the reasons.

## Where other guides differ

- **"launch Tuesday to Thursday"** [eronred, the playbook behind ../../mobile/app-growth/LAUNCH-AND-MARKETING.md, which now points here]. Official: no golden-ticket day; weekends suit smaller teams and get 15% more Visit clicks ([Day, time and scheduling](#day-time-and-scheduling)).
- **"find a hunter 1 week before"** [eronred]. Official: self-hunt; no hunter is needed, and paying one breaks the rules ([Accounts and hunting](#accounts-and-hunting)). The source skill's eval for a first Product Hunt launch also expected "hunter network"; drop that expectation.
- **"coming-soon page 2 weeks before"** [eronred]. The current guide no longer describes a coming-soon page; schedule the launch up to 1 month ahead and share the scheduled page.
- **"ship at 12:01am PST"** [eronred]. Correct as the default; the official exceptions are a press embargo time, a non-US team, or when the team is online.
- **"Productivity, developer and design tools"** as the fit [eronred]. Narrower than official: any new, usable product fits ([Is it a fit](#is-it-a-fit)).
- **"Encourage your existing audience to engage"** [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, launch]. Compatible only as an ask for feedback and comments, never upvotes.

## Prove it

- The posting account is personal, at least a week old and onboarded; every co-maker has an account.
- The listing passes the table: product URL with no shortener or UTM, name without description or emoji, tagline of at most 60 characters, description within the live form's limit, up to 3 tags, a square thumbnail under 3MB whose first frame works alone, at least 2 gallery images, a public YouTube video if any, and a promo with offer, code and expiry if any.
- The maker's first comment is drafted and asks for feedback, not upvotes.
- No launch email, post, DM or giveaway asks for upvotes or rewards them, and no one was paid to hunt or send traffic.
- Measurable goals are written down beyond Product of the Day, and the post-launch follow-up has an owner.
