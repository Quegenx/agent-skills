# SEO

Search engines can only rank a page they can reach, read and index, and Google chooses among pages by what they say and how other pages link to them, not by character counts or a "dofollow" flag. Most SEO advice in tools and skill packs mixes Google's documented rules with the tool's own heuristics; this skill keeps them apart and quotes [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs) wherever a rule is Google's. ## The rules

1. **Check the crawl before the findings.** A blocked, rate-limited or JavaScript-shell page reports no title and zero words; record it as a coverage gap, never as thin content, and ask the owner to allowlist the crawler instead of evading the block ([TECHNICAL-AUDIT.md](TECHNICAL-AUDIT.md#check-the-crawl-first)).
2. **A tool's threshold is not Google's rule.** Google sets no limit on title or meta description length, no word count, no heading count or order for ranking; state such numbers as the tool's heuristic, and use web.dev's TTFB bands for speed ([TECHNICAL-AUDIT.md](TECHNICAL-AUDIT.md#tool-thresholds-are-not-google-rules)).
3. **Recommend selectively.** Lead with one to three changes that capture more qualified demand or stop a real loss; never rewrite a page that already ranks near the top; size benefits by mechanism and cluster, label any position-one figure hypothetical, and never invent a conversion rate or revenue [open-seo, seo-audit].
4. **Every live SERP ranking claim has a dated observation** written "#N (page P)" with query, country and listings returned from a licensed data source; keep Search Console average positions separate ([TECHNICAL-AUDIT.md](TECHNICAL-AUDIT.md#ranking-evidence)). Scraping Google for rank checks is against its spam policies.
5. **Canonical is a hint; `noindex` is removal.** Consolidate duplicates with `rel=canonical` in one place, keep `noindex` for pages that should not be in Search, and never use `noindex` or robots.txt as crawl control on a site below Google's crawl-budget sizes ([PAGES-AT-SCALE.md](PAGES-AT-SCALE.md#indexation-and-crawl-budget)).
6. **Each page at scale earns its place with data no sibling has.** Pages that only swap a city or keyword into identical content are the source's "thin content" mistake and match Google's doorway examples; launch the strongest 20 to 50 first and scale only once they index ([PAGES-AT-SCALE.md](PAGES-AT-SCALE.md#what-google-calls-spam)).
7. **Earn links, don't manufacture them.** No bought, swapped, badge-required or automated links unless qualified `sponsored` or `nofollow`; no low-quality directories, bookmarks, forum signatures or optimized-anchor press-release links; DR is a third-party metric, not a Google signal ([LINKS-AND-OUTREACH.md](LINKS-AND-OUTREACH.md#what-google-calls-link-spam)).
8. **Outreach stays lawful and personal.** Contact details only from public business-role sources with their URL, never invented or scraped into lists; every email meets [email-practices COMPLIANCE.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/email-practices/COMPLIANCE.md); a human approves every account, form and send.
9. **Verify a backlink from the anchor itself:** the `<a href>`, its `rel` tokens (spaces and commas), page-level `nofollow` and `X-Robots-Tag`, recorded with a timestamp; `sponsored` is the correct value on a paid tier ([LINKS-AND-OUTREACH.md](LINKS-AND-OUTREACH.md#verify-the-actual-backlink)).
10. **Local: category and claim problems outrank posting,** businesses are matched by `cid` or `place_id`, and no plan gates, buys or filters reviews or stuffs the business name ([LOCAL.md](LOCAL.md)).
11. **Refresh for readers, not for a fresh date.** Keep the URL and ranking anchors, redirect with 301 or 308 for at least a year when a URL must change, and move the visible date and `dateModified` only after a substantial change ([CONTENT-REFRESH.md](CONTENT-REFRESH.md)).
12. **Severity is P0 to P3** as in [code-review REVIEWER.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/code-review/REVIEWER.md); a tool's critical, warning and info map to P1, P2 and P3, and anything keeping a page that should rank out of Search is P0.

## Prove it

The crawl's blocked and shell pages are reported as gaps; no length or word-count number is presented as Google's; the audit leads with at most three sized, evidenced recommendations and a reason for every rejected candidate; each ranking has its query, country, date and "#N (page P)" form and none came from scraping Google; duplicates use one canonical method; every page in a scaled set differs from its siblings by more than the variable; no link in the plan is bought, swapped, badge-required or unqualified when paid; every live listing has an `Observed rel` and `Checked at`; every outreach contact has a public source URL; refreshed pages keep their URL or a lasting permanent redirect; and every file's own Prove it checks pass for the parts you touched.
