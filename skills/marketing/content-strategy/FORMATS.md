# Content formats

Which types fit the searchable and shareable jobs, which formats earn links, and the production standard for each format. From coreyhaines31/marketingskills `content-strategy` (MIT). Vs and alternative pages have their own skill, [comparison-pages](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/comparison-pages/SKILL.md); free tools and calculators are [lead-magnets](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/lead-magnets/SKILL.md); programmatic pages at scale are in [seo](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/seo/SKILL.md).

## Searchable types

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, content-strategy]`
- **Use-case content:** the formula "[persona] + [use-case]", for long-tail keywords ("Project management for designers", "Task tracking for developers", "Client collaboration for freelancers").
- **Hub and spoke:** use the [linking pattern](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/site-architecture/URLS-AND-LINKS.md#hub-and-spoke). "Create hub first, then build spokes. Interlink strategically." The source's URL example is `/topic` with `/topic/subtopic-1`; most content works under `/blog/post-title`, with a dedicated hub URL only for major topics with layered depth (the source's example is Atlassian's `/agile` guide).
- **Template libraries:** high-intent searches ("marketing plan template") that give standalone value and show how the product improves the template.

## Shareable types

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, content-strategy]`
- **Thought leadership:** name concepts everyone feels but hasn't named; challenge conventional wisdom with evidence; share honest experiences.
- **Data-driven:** product data (anonymized), public data, original research (run experiments, share results).
- **Expert roundups:** "15-30 experts answering one specific question", with distribution built in.
- **Case studies:** Challenge, Solution, Results, Key learnings. Testimonials and results in them follow the FTC rules in [comparison-pages TEMPLATES.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/comparison-pages/TEMPLATES.md#ftc-rules-for-quotes-and-results).
- **Meta content:** behind-the-scenes transparency ("How We Got Our First $5k MRR", "Why We Chose Debt Over VC").

## Link-earning formats

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, content-strategy]` The source cites Foundation Inc.'s B2B Backlink Intelligence Report (March 2026), "a single vendor study of B2B SaaS sites, so treat as directional". Each format's share of backlinks relative to its share of pages:

| Format | Backlinks vs page share |
|---|---|
| Statistics / data roundups | 4.25x |
| Glossary / definition pages | 1.47x |
| Interactive tools / calculators | 1.38x |
| How-to / tutorials | 1.36x |
| Original research / reports | 0.80x |
| Ultimate guides | 0.77x |
| Thought leadership | 0.74x |
| Templates / frameworks | 0.68x |

The source's reading: "curating statistics earns ~5x the links of producing original research" (4.25 over 0.80 is about 5.3). Its implications: publish a stats page for your category and keep it fresh (citable one-line stats are also what AI answers lift; see [ai-search-visibility](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/ai-search-visibility/SKILL.md)); when you run original research, pair it with your own stat roundup of citable one-liners. The formats at the bottom still earn rankings, conversions and brand: judge each piece by its job.

## Per-format discipline

`[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, content-strategy]`
- **Blog post:** write 10 title options before drafting and pick the strongest; plan about 5 editing passes (structure, clarity, evidence, line edit, headline and SEO). This is a drafting habit; anti-slop-copy's two or three delivered alternatives for the headline are a different step ([anti-slop-copy](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/SKILL.md)).
- **Long-form guide:** the flagship of a pillar, with a table of contents and links to its spokes; build the hub before the spokes.
- **Video:** script the hook first, front-load the payoff, plan short clips at creation (video production itself is [marketing-video](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/videos/marketing-video/SKILL.md)).
- **Podcast:** one interview yields a transcript, quote graphics, short clips and a written recap; design the episode to be atomized.
- **Email:** one idea per send; the subject line is the title, so write several and pick. Sending rules are [email-practices](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/email-practices/SKILL.md).

## Prove it

Each planned piece names its type and its job (rankings, links, conversion, brand); any link-earning claim carries the Foundation Inc. vendor label; a blog post's draft file shows the 10 title options; a case study's results trace to the customer's own numbers.
