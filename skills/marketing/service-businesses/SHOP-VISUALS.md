## Offer ladder and real buyer

[more skills, 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md]

- Frontage opener: one free image, optionally printed A4 on decent paper and given to the owner. The author excludes this opener from an invoice.
- Photo-set job: four frontage/interior images, fixed price once, for agreed listing/door/flyer/other authorized uses.
- Lighting proposal: sign company, electrician or shopfitter buys a preview for its installation quote. The source proposes per-quote work under a monthly trade-client arrangement; the buyer is distinct from the depicted shop.
- Product work: per item in batches, with weekly new-stock work as the recurring option.

No money price, contract minimum, cancellation term or pilot duration is supplied. Repeated demand and superior sales results are hypotheses, not guarantees. Keep operator review before each delivery.

## Bounded local research

[more skills, 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md]

Choose one street or quarter and independent categories such as hardware, butchers, florists, bookshops, barbers or haberdashery. The author's optional filter is rating at least 4.0, fewer than about 600 reviews and evidence of independence. A family name or trade label is a clue, not proof of ownership. Cross-check any no-website claim under [prospecting LOCAL-SMB.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/LOCAL-SMB.md#website-status).

Find a specific visible presentation issue, obtain a rights-cleared current actual photo, choose the honest change envelope, make the selected deliverable, compare it with the original, approve and use a separately authorized handoff. The source's everyone-qualifies statement conflicts with its own later rating/review filter; choose a targeting policy before building a list.

## Production and paper review

[more skills, 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md]

Select the matching [frontage, lighting or object branch](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md#shopfronts-lighting-proposals-and-stock). Preserve the shop's actual age, sign and neighboring context. A proposed lighting installation must be presented as proposed. Product images are checked against the actual object, not merely a visually pleasing output.

For a paper handoff, inspect an actual print and compare lettering and neighbors before delivery. Verify that the current business is still trading at that frontage, especially when the input is old. Choose a quiet appropriate time rather than assuming walk-ins are universally welcome; the author's preference excludes lunchtime. Delivery/channel choice follows recipient context and authorization.

The source downloaded Street View imagery for editing/printing. Google's rules do not permit that general workflow; use an independently captured or rights-cleared photograph instead. [Shared official correction](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md#start-with-permission-and-the-original)

## Handoff copy

[more skills, 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md]

Inputs are shop name/type, what actually changed, publicly stated tenure if known and printed/Instagram channel. Use fewer than 45 words overall, fewer than 30 for print, warm plain language and optional lowercase. Explain that the image is theirs to keep free, state that the sign was retained only after checking, and end with one simple other-angle question. Do not criticize rating, review count or missing website, or pitch a package, call or site build in this first note. Apply [anti-slop-copy](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/SKILL.md) for generic copy review.

Describe actual edits. The source's only-light-changed wording is false when the recipe also crops/straightens/cleans; its printed example is 34 words and fails its own under-30 rule. Do not copy it or invent an alternative case study. Literal pixel-identical signs and the conflicting night-brightness instructions also remain unresolved in the [shared fidelity contract](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md#shopfronts-lighting-proposals-and-stock).

## Prove it

[more skills, 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md]

The buyer is the shop for existing-photo/stock work and the quoting trade for installation previews; each job has the right one-off/per-item/per-quote unit; the free opener stays free; actual photo rights and current trading identity are established; signs, stock and neighboring businesses pass full-size/paper comparison; proposed lights are not claimed to exist; and handoff copy states only real changes within its word limit. The author reports a search and frontage edit, but no real lighting-trade job, product-set sale or walk-in; comparative revenue/channel claims are not verified.
