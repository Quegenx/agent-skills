# Demo-first client acquisition

The shared acquisition and handoff method from [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md], with the distinct approval packets in guides 02, 03, 05 to 13. Individual service files own their offer, demonstration, client intake, recurring work and billing. This file owns the common candidate-to-demo-to-mail sequence.

## Choose and inspect one market

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

1. Choose one niche and city or service area. Compare niches using sourced business facts and the value of the actual service, rather than assuming every local business needs the same product.
2. Assemble an inspectable candidate sheet. The common fields are business name, address, published phone, website status and source evidence; individual recipes add their own qualification fields. Keep the initial candidates separate from the chosen send list.
3. Inspect operational status, identity, the actual service gap and local buying authority. Show the filtered count and first 20 rows where the recipe requests them. Stop for recipient selection before building a batch.
4. Retain the campaign's selected city, niche, source list, provider accounts, sender identity and spending allowance. A prompt containing a dollar ceiling does not establish that a provider enforces it.

**Source differences:** guide 01's prose says rating above 4.0 while its prompts include exactly 4.0; choose the intended boundary explicitly. Its 10 to 200 reviews and rating filter are author choices, not proof of demand. Guides that say to exclude every chain/franchise differ from [prospecting LOCAL-SMB.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/LOCAL-SMB.md#common-mistakes), which checks the franchisee's local marketing authority first. Keep that existing authority check. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; coreyhaines31/marketingskills, prospecting]

Use [local discovery and website verification](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/LOCAL-SMB.md) and [list compliance](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/LIST-COMPLIANCE.md). The guides proposed retained Places lead exports; [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) restrict storage outside permitted exceptions and exempt place IDs. Preserve useful field schemas with permitted independent provenance. A missing website field is not a finding that no website exists. Review/photo display also needs the applicable attribution and rights. [Google Places policies]

## Approve one template before batching

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

Build one niche-specific example, inspect it on a phone and refine it before copying. Preserve the approved layout while replacing each recipient's verified data. Keep recipient, source assets and preview link associated in the sheet. Service files specify the content of each preview and any extra review gates.

Identify the actual builder and contact channel. A proposed site or automation is a demonstration until the owner accepts it. Do not invent customer events, reviews, revenue or financial records under a real business's identity, even with a mock label. Use source-provided fixtures without real-brand sample data. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; owner standing rules]

The source wants a finished-looking demonstration; production design still follows the applicable [landing-page-ui](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/web-ui/landing-page-ui/SKILL.md), [app-ui](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/web-ui/app-ui/SKILL.md) or [agent-chat-ui](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/ai-agents/agent-chat-ui/SKILL.md). Its page-content brief does not replace those design rules. Actual-photo inputs and output review go to [marketing-images REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md). ## Prepare the mail packet

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

The author's postcard proposal is 6 by 9, subject to the selected supplier's supported units, template, address area and artwork requirements. Its front shows the actual demonstration, often a phone screenshot. Its back carries one truthful recipient-specific proposal, the QR for that exact preview and the real sender's name and callback number. Each service supplies its own hook.

Show three finished samples when the recipe requests three. Some recipes require three demo pages as well; demand alerts require every card to be shown. Keep those stronger service-specific gates. Artwork approval precedes approval of the named mail batch and its cost. An approved design alone is not an order or send authorization.

Use the approved provider account and log the actual result for each recipient: business, destination address, preview URL and send date; guide 01 also requests expected delivery date. Expected arrival is not confirmed delivery. The source's quoted postcard prices are not current supplier quotes.

## Handle observed interest

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

Notify the operator through the chosen destination when measured preview activity occurs. Retain the campaign association and actual observed event. A URL request does not identify the business owner, prove a physical QR scan or establish dwell time without real telemetry.

The author's optional cadence is a same-day human conversation and, after three days without a response, one short follow-up draft per business, reviewed before sending. Use the service's actual message purpose: view the site, try the demo, or discuss the pilot. Follow [cold-outreach](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/cold-outreach/SKILL.md) and [SMS compliance](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/sms-marketing/COMPLIANCE.md); a scan, public phone number or operator approval is not SMS permission. The source proposed a post-scan text; Twilio requires consent appropriate to sender, subject and message type. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/messaging-policy]

## Accept, hand off and retain

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

After a real acceptance, carry out only the agreed service handoff. Each recipe names client access, owned assets, pilot, recurring deliverables and billing evidence. For direct websites, use [WEBSITE-OFFERS.md](WEBSITE-OFFERS.md); an affiliate claim has a different payer and transfer process.

Establish the individual stages before saving a reusable workflow. The handoff preserves niche/city inputs, selected list, template, asset-to-preview mapping, approval checkpoints, provider and spend scope, send log, accepted-client scope and recurring report. Scheduler state, credentials and confirmed side effects stay in [agent-harness SCHEDULED-AGENTS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/ai-agents/agent-harness/SCHEDULED-AGENTS.md), rather than being redefined by a sales prompt.

## Prove it

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

Show the approved recipient list, approved template and correctly matched previews; the recipe's required sample packet; the exact batch approval and actual send records; evidence behind every factual hook; measured activity without inferred visitor identity; and an accepted client handoff naming the actual ongoing work. Missing permissions, vendor details and commercial terms remain visible rather than being filled with assumptions.
