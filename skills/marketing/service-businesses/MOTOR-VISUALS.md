## Select the commercial motion

[more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md]

| Buyer | Job and proposed charging unit |
|---|---|
| Independent dealer | Reviewed stock images, per car within an agreed monthly volume; author proposes reducing unit fee with greater volume. First three images free, then quote the stock list. |
| Wrap, tint or detail shop | Preview of the customer's exact car and selected treatment; per render/enquiry, without a subscription. |
| Van/fleet operator | Signage preview for an actual vehicle; one-off per vehicle or fleet. |

The author suggests beginning with wrap shops. No selling price, pilot duration or demonstrated close rate is supplied. Forty cars or a six-vehicle fleet are source illustrations, not required deal sizes or market evidence.

## Qualify one area

[more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md]

Research independent dealers, detailing/valeting and wrap/tint businesses. Dealer heuristics: rating at least 4.3 plus fewer than 8 actual photos or visibly crowded multi-car shots. Wrap/detail heuristics: rating at least 4.7, fewer than 200 reviews and fewer than 10 actual photos. Do not infer work quality or complete photo inventory from these proxies. The [food guide's photo-sample correction](FOOD-VISUALS.md#qualification-and-routing) also applies to capped Places arrays.

The source rejects manufacturer franchises outright. [more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md] Existing local SMB guidance instead checks whether a franchisee has local marketing authority; keep that rule. ## Listing stills before motion

[more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md]

The repeatable actual-stock shot set is three-quarter front, three-quarter rear, interior from the driver's door and dashboard with actual mileage visible. Each needs evidence of the actual vehicle/view; one exterior photograph cannot establish unseen condition or mileage. Accept the still before reusing it as a treatment-preview base.

The author's optional reel is three or four real same-car stills with a slow push on each, assembled to about 15 seconds. Stills come first. A cut of still photographs is separate from generated specific-product footage, which remains prohibited by the [existing video rule](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/videos/marketing-video/SKILL.md#the-rules). The source's photo-edit proposals also retain the [generic product/prompt/text conflicts](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md#conflicting-source-methods).

## Preview inputs and acceptance

[more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md]

For each request, identify the actual vehicle and desired output: listing angle, explicit wrap color/finish, tint level, paint-correction preview or supplied van graphics. Use the matching [canonical branch](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md#vehicles-and-treatment-previews); do not conflate a sales listing with a proposed treatment that deliberately changes one surface.

Before delivery, compare at full size for erased damage, changed wheels, invented badges and surviving plates/reflections. A color preview includes the physical swatch and permanent indicative-color warning; tint requires the applicable local rules. Treat plate blanking as the author's operating policy, not universal legal advice. Use only actually supplied business name, phone and authorized accreditation marks on van concepts, with exact text/logos handled under the existing image policy.

## First message and ongoing delivery

[more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md]

Closed inputs are business name/type, actual deliverable, checked listing information and chosen permitted channel. Draft fewer than 50 words in plain direct lowercase. Open with their own vehicle, state once that no damage was removed after verifying that statement, use one real listing number or none, and finish with one easy question. Do not pitch a call, package, monthly plan, strategy or quick chat in the first message. Use [anti-slop-copy](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/SKILL.md) for generic phrase checks. An operator account identifies its actual operator/business; the source's personal-looking-account advice does not permit impersonation.

Agree the real monthly stock count, per-enquiry or per-fleet scope before production; keep the three billing motions separate. The author proposes learning model failures through at least 15 inspected car edits before outreach. Every paid output still receives individual human review, not a sample-only check.

## Prove it

[more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md]

The selected buyer/unit is explicit; authorized actual input supports each view and mileage claim; all vehicle/treatment/van branch checks pass against the original; damage remains visible, badges/wheels match and plates/reflections meet the chosen policy; physical color confirmation is required; first-message claims are true; and recurring car volume is distinct from per-enquiry previews or one-off fleets. The author reports search and one forecourt edit, but no reel, actual customer wrap or outbound send; no revenue result is established.
