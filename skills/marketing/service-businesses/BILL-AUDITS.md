# Recurring business-bill audits

The audit-and-recovery service from [more skills, 10-business-bill-audit.md]: compare bills with real agreements and fee schedules, draft supported provider requests, track actual credits and bill an agreed savings share. The source's classifier is one stage inside that complete client service.

## Offer and intake

[more skills, 10-business-bill-audit.md]

The author proposes a free first real-bill audit and a share of actual savings instead of an upfront audit charge. Target card-processing, phone/internet or waste bills where they fit the business. The source supplies no share percentage, attribution period, future-savings treatment or termination terms; these remain owner decisions. Generic pay-on-results design stays in [offer-design GUARANTEES.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/offer-design/GUARANTEES.md).

Candidate niches include HVAC, plumbers, restaurants, auto shops and retail. Optional source settings are rating at least 4.0, at least ten reviews, phone and website. Use the shared [permitted sourcing and candidate approval](DEMO-FIRST.md#choose-and-inspect-one-market), with name, address, phone, site and relevant permitted rating/review evidence in a niche/city audit worksheet.

For an actual client, obtain the bill, provider/account identity and applicable contract early. Request the preceding three months of the same bill when available and locate the provider's applicable published fee schedule. Evidence limited to three months does not establish when an older recurring fee began. A large-looking line is not necessarily an overcharge.

## Comparison rulebook and decisions

[more skills, 10-business-bill-audit.md]

Keep a rulebook and contract context per client/provider. The six source anomaly classes are:

| Candidate issue | Evidence to compare |
|---|---|
| Fee absent from the contract | Actual agreement and applicable schedule |
| Rate above the contracted rate | Contracted rate and billed basis |
| Service no longer used | Actual service/cancellation terms and charge |
| Duplicate | The actual repeated billed item |
| Increase without written notice | Prior bills and applicable notice record |
| Minimum despite volume above the minimum | Actual agreed minimum, volume and billed amount |

These are candidate checks, not universal refund entitlements. Non-use is not automatically contractual cancellation. Compare every line with agreement, history and applicable fee table; retain correct lines as not-an-overcharge rather than forcing every item into an anomaly category.

The source proposes one decision call per bill, separate per-line overcharge yes/no and type, plus whole-bill dispute-worthiness and savings range. Follow [Jev's independent-question and exact-fact rules](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/ai-agents/jev-decisions/SKILL.md#the-rules): interpretation can be judged, but amounts, rates, comparisons and totals supported by exact records are computed in code. Integration form is linked from [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

The author's flagging threshold is strictly above 0.8, with lower values sent to review. Unlike the invoice guide, this guide leaves exact equality undefined. Specify equality and the named output metric before using the proposal; apply existing [calibration](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/ai-agents/jev-decisions/PRODUCTION.md), not a claim that the threshold proves recoverability.

**Unresolved source precedence:** its full prompt says never flag a line matching either the contract or the published fee table. A public fee table could permit more than the negotiated contract. Keep that source wording visible and ask which applicable terms govern conflicting evidence; do not silently make one override the other. Preserve suspected amount, requested remedy and actual credit as separate facts.

## Demonstration and provider letter

[more skills, 10-business-bill-audit.md]

The optional connection exercise is eight synthetic statement lines, two planted errors and six correct lines, using the source's illustrative 2.6% plus $0.10 rate. Compare returned classifications with the planted labels and show the chosen metric; this is not independent model validation or a market rate.

The sales demonstration is a separate trade-matched synthetic bill with ten to fifteen lines and three planted anomaly types. Show every line, highlighted candidates and types, computed monthly/yearly illustration and the first paragraph of a proposed provider letter. Label it synthetic without creating financial records under a real prospect's brand. Offer to assess one actual bill free. Annualizing a monthly candidate does not establish twelve recurring recoverable months.

For an actual supported finding, draft a short polite letter identifying business, provider, account number and statement date. For every challenged line include its name, amount, evidenced months charged and applicable contract/schedule comparison. Request supported historic credit and future correction/removal. Retain the letter with the bill and show it before every send; the owner name/signature needs actual authority. The source does not grant autonomous negotiation, settlement or acceptance of changed terms.

Use [DEMO-FIRST.md](DEMO-FIRST.md#prepare-the-mail-packet) with this source's three demo-page samples and three postcards. The front shows the synthetic finding summary; the back truthfully identifies it as an illustration of a comparable bill, with correct QR and actual callback. Do not imply the prospect's real bills have already been inspected. Optional interest handling follows the shared observed-event and permitted-channel rules.

## Ongoing service and billing evidence

[more skills, 10-business-bill-audit.md]

After actual acceptance, select a forwarding address or authorize access to the inbox already receiving bills. For every new bill, select that client's provider contract, schedule and policy, prepare findings/letter and obtain approval before contact. A shared inbox is the author's operating proposal, not evidence that client separation or every provider adapter already works.

Track later statements, provider responses, credits and refunds against the actual finding. At month end, operator and owner receive overcharges found, actual credits/refunds received and unresolved requests. Bill the agreed share from that actual reconciliation, using the agreed treatment of future reduced bills. Do not charge on the mock total, a requested remedy or the model's savings prediction.

The source suggests bundling this with [invoice collection](INVOICE-COLLECTION.md) and [dispute recovery](DISPUTE-RECOVERY.md). Keep their separate customer/account contexts, pilot promises and fee bases rather than treating every recovered-money workflow as interchangeable.

## Prove it

[more skills, 10-business-bill-audit.md]

Show the actual bill/contract/history/schedule packet, justified anomaly classes and correctly computed candidate sums. Equality and conflicting-term precedence are resolved or explicitly held. Every provider letter matches available evidence and approval. The monthly statement distinguishes findings, requested remedies, credits received and open work; its fee follows agreed terms rather than assumed recoveries.
