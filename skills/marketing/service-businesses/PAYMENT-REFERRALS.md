# Payment referrals and merchant migration

The provider-funded introduction and hands-on migration from [more skills, 12-whop-payment-referrals.md]. It has separate local and online research branches, an unpublished mobile demonstration, owner-account handoff and post-launch processing checks. Generic commission definitions and disclosure stay in [referral-programs AFFILIATES.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/referral-programs/AFFILIATES.md).

## Offer and accepted program terms

[more skills, 12-whop-payment-referrals.md]

The source proposes no separate service invoice to the owner: the provider pays the introducer from the applicable referral program. Read the accepted current program terms before quoting commissions, attribution, eligibility or owner costs. The relationship and comparison must be explained truthfully near the referral CTA.

The source combines lifetime earnings, a six-month minimum and volume-based extensions. Whop's [Partners article](https://whop.com/blog/whop-partners/) describes up to 30% of provider gross profit and ongoing lifetime earnings; its separately named [Affiliate terms](https://whop.com/earnings-terms/) stop seller-referral commission after six months. These may be different programs. Obtain the applicable accepted terms rather than merging them or claiming one universally overrides the other. The extension rule is unresolved. [Whop Partners; Whop Affiliate terms]

The source's example monthly earnings are not a fee calculator. Commission is not automatically a percentage of the whole processing fee. The [current fee schedule](https://docs.whop.com/payments-and-billing/fees/fees) includes domestic-card 2.7% plus $0.30, additional international/conversion charges and optional services. It differs from the source's approximate 3% and incomplete earnings arithmetic. Actual fee/profit basis, transaction count, payment mix and program agreement are necessary for a comparison. [Whop fee schedule]

## Qualify one pocket

[more skills, 12-whop-payment-referrals.md]

Start with one kind of business. Look for an offer already selling, repeated payment activity, visible sales evidence, a specific payment problem and an accessible owner.

| Branch | Evidence worksheet |
|---|---|
| Local | Business, address, published phone, website, permitted rating/review evidence, observed payment flow and evidence of friction such as cash-only acceptance, failed card facilities, missing online checkout or a booking flow without deposits |
| Online | Seller, current platform, its public fee page, visible audience count only if shown, actual product price, public business contact and clearly identified assumptions in any revenue/fee estimate |

Rank by supportable friction or savings, show the author's proposed top 30 local candidates, then wait for selection under [DEMO-FIRST.md](DEMO-FIRST.md). A public member count is not a paid-subscriber count; a cash-only review does not quantify lost sales. Leave unknown revenue, transactions and losses unknown.

## Unpublished demonstration and call brief

[more skills, 12-whop-payment-referrals.md]

Using accurate authorized assets and current offer facts, prepare an unpublished store proposal with actual products/services, prices and tiers. Local proposals illustrate supported counter checkout/deposits; online proposals preserve existing tiers. Show storefront and checkout at phone size and review the first three before preparing the rest. Keep drafts unpublished; the operator is not the merchant accepting real payments for a prospect.

The claim preview has the business-specific screenshots, actual operator contact and the approved referral CTA. The source proposes prefilled business/industry parameters, but their exact support and draft-transfer procedure remain unverified. Do not fabricate working checkout, deposit or transfer functionality merely to match a screenshot.

Use the [shared postcard packet](DEMO-FIRST.md#prepare-the-mail-packet) with phone checkout on the front, a truthful unpublished-demo invitation and QR on the back, three proofs and approved named-batch sending. The source's exclusive claim path does not replace the [nearby commission disclosure](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/referral-programs/AFFILIATES.md#disclosure). Whop's [earnings terms](https://whop.com/earnings-terms/) require disclosure near the affiliate link and prohibit representing the promoter as the seller. [Whop Affiliate terms]

Before a call, prepare one plain-language page: what the business sells, actual current payment path, supported estimated volume, verified present costs, comparable provider costs and three concrete day-one changes. The operator offers to handle the move. Keep the old payment path available until the new one actually works; deposits do not guarantee no-shows stop.

## Owner invitation and migration

Whop's [referral guide](https://docs.whop.com/refer-businesses-to-whop) documents business name, optional website/estimated annual revenue, owner email and Create & invite. It creates the business and invites the owner to take ownership. The source omits the annual basis and owner-email input; this route does not prove every proposed cross-account draft transfer works. [Whop referral docs]

[more skills, 12-whop-payment-referrals.md]

After an actual accepted engagement, prepare the supported owner-account handoff, let the owner connect payouts and reconcile every catalog item/price. For a local merchant, set up supported QR/contactless acceptance on the owner's device. For an online seller, prepare the member-access continuity plan for review and choose the migration date with the owner.

The source's acceptance step is the first authorized real payment end to end, followed by daily processing checks for a week and an alert if processing stops. These are proposed future operational actions, not permission to run a live payment while documenting the recipe.

Whop's [Tap to Pay documentation](https://docs.whop.com/payments-and-billing/tap-to-pay) requires a supported country, eligible iPhone/software and connectivity, and supports one-time payments only. The source's repeat-payment prospect criterion does not mean contactless can start subscriptions. Verify the selected merchant/device eligibility; do not infer universal availability. [Whop Tap to Pay docs]

## Ongoing ledger and referrals

[more skills, 12-whop-payment-referrals.md]

Maintain referred business, niche, actual processing-start date, monthly volume and actual monthly commission. Monthly review flags processing drops/stops; two weeks after the first payment, remind the operator to ask the owner about peers with the former payment problem. This reminder is not an automatic outbound message.

The source says work is done once processing starts, but its own first-week and monthly checks remain service responsibilities. Preserve those explicit duties. Track actual platform commission state using the existing [reconciliation owner](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/referral-programs/AFFILIATES.md#reconciliation-and-payouts), rather than assuming an introduction guarantees attribution or payment.

## Prove it

[more skills, 12-whop-payment-referrals.md; official Whop pages above]

Show the applicable accepted program, fee comparison with explicit known inputs, qualified evidence and truthful unpublished demonstration. Show actual owner-account/payout handoff, member-access plan where needed, selected feature eligibility and authorized first-payment acceptance when performed. The first-week check log and monthly volume/commission ledger substantiate continuing responsibilities; no unsupported lifetime or earnings calculation is promised.
