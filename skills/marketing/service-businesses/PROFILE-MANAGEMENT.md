The diagnosis and priority rules remain in [local SEO](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/seo/LOCAL.md); this file owns the commercial delivery workflow.

## Offer and maintenance-gap packet

[more skills, 13-google-maps-local-seo.md]

The author proposes weekly Profile maintenance for a monthly fee, with no numerical price, trial, term or cancellation rule. Optional qualification is rating at least 4.0 and 20 reviews. Present the top 30 maintenance candidates with reasons rather than treating these thresholds as proof of buying intent.

The proposed intake fields are name, address, phone, website, rating/review count, place ID, hours, services, observed photo count/recency, available questions/answers and replies on the last ten available reviews. Seven proposed gap signals are no posts, fewer than ten photos, customer-only photos, unanswered questions, missing replies, missing services and missing hours. The author requests a score out of ten but supplies no weights; resolve the rubric rather than inventing arithmetic.

Use [shared permitted sourcing](DEMO-FIRST.md#choose-and-inspect-one-market). The source treats Places as if it provided every requested administrative field and chronological review set; that coverage is unverified. Record unavailable data as unknown and keep the actual evidence surface/time with the comparison.

For the real query and area, compare three observed local-pack businesses: review/photo counts, posting/reply activity and service/hour completeness. List three concrete observed gaps per prospect. The source's activity-heavy pitch does not override the existing priority: category/claim issues outrank posting cadence, and posts follow competitive profile basics. ## Prepare the first month

[more skills, 13-google-maps-local-seo.md]

For each approved business, prepare:

1. Four weekly posts based on real services and the actual season.
2. Proposed answers to available unanswered questions, only on a verified available surface.
3. Replies to the last five available reviews, including negative ones, in the authorized business voice.
4. Accurate revised services and description using supported customer vocabulary.
5. Three example images, each visibly labelled Example, never presented as actual jobs or premises.

Show the first business's complete month before batching. Real owner job photos replace examples after onboarding. Existing image rules and [review response/privacy rules](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/review-management/REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#replying-to-a-review) still apply.

Follow [DEMO-FIRST.md](DEMO-FIRST.md#prepare-the-mail-packet), retaining this source's three demo pages and three postcard samples.

## Manager onboarding and first week

Use separate Google Accounts and delegated Manager access, not the owner's password. Google's current invitation sequence is Business Profile, More, Business Profile settings, People and access, Add, operator email, Manager, Invite. Confirm acceptance before acting; the source's quick-access wording is not a delivery guarantee. Managers can edit information, reply to reviews and manage posts. [https://support.google.com/business/answer/3403100?hl=en]

[more skills, 13-google-maps-local-seo.md]

Send the owner short phone-readable instructions with the exact email to invite. Publish nothing while waiting for access. After confirmed access and operator approval, the source backlog order is services/description, hours, available Q&A, review replies and first post, subject to the local SEO priorities above. Tell the owner the actual changes each day in the first week; do not promise Google will visibly publish every edit within a few days.

## Weekly work and exceptions

[more skills, 13-google-maps-local-seo.md]

On the chosen weekday, draft the seasonal post and new-review replies, handle questions only where available, file/add owner-supplied real photos and check services, regular hours and upcoming holiday hours. Give the owner one easy photo-submission number. Show the operator the complete package before publication.

Separately, a new review below four stars triggers same-day operator notification rather than waiting for the weekly queue. Apply the existing [reputation response rules](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/review-management/REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md), not automatic admissions, invented resolutions or disclosure of private account facts.

## Monthly report and second-offer review

[more skills, 13-google-maps-local-seo.md]

At month start, prepare applicable searches, views, call-button interactions, directions and website clicks beside the previous month and the pre-service baseline month. Include what was actually published. Operator reviews first; the owner receives a short metrics-first summary. Label delayed/unavailable values instead of fabricating a complete month-start report.

Google's [performance definitions](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9918094?hl=en) say Calls counts call-button clicks, not completed calls; searches may take up to five days to appear; available metrics vary; and performance can include both organic and Ads interactions. The source called these actual calls and implied the service caused improvement. Report the applicable observations without claiming causation. [Google Business Profile performance help]

The source also promises ranking/call gains from the work. Google's [ranking help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en) identifies relevance, distance and prominence and describes complete information, reviews and photos; it supplies no guarantee that this service increases rank or calls. Keep the maintenance deliverables and observed report, not the guarantee. [Google local ranking help]

For existing website/review-card clients, optionally apply the same maintenance rubric and list three missing items for each proposed expansion. Contact nobody from that report. A prior purchase does not establish current neglect, Profile access or publication permission.
