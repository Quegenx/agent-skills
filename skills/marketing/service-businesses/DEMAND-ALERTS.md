# Local demand alerts and recommendation-led offers

The two-stage business from [more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md]: use permission-cleared local service requests to select a useful demonstration, then offer subscribed trade/area alerts. It supplies a research and client-service workflow, not permission to automate access to Facebook groups.

## Source and request records

[more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md]

Start with one locality and a selected source list. The author's discovery worksheet records source name/link, membership count if available and approximate observed daily posting activity; a minimum membership/activity filter is configurable. The user handles any permitted membership action. Membership does not establish automated collection or reuse permission.

Keep observation read-only: no likes, comments, posts, reactions or messages from the watcher. A later business-service demonstration and its separately approved outreach are distinct steps, not permission to contact the people requesting recommendations.

Keep the existing [public-source boundaries](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/FIRST-CUSTOMERS.md) and [list compliance](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/LIST-COMPLIANCE.md). The source proposes logged-in group collection; private/gated community collection remains excluded, and current Meta automation permission is unresolved. No bot-detection bypass or simulated-human evasion. [more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md; coreyhaines31/marketingskills, prospecting]

For permitted observations, keep two linked views:

| View | Fields and counting unit |
|---|---|
| Requests | Original link, necessary request evidence, service requested, stated neighborhood, date, and urgency labelled as an interpretation: today, this week or just looking |
| Recommended businesses | Supplier name, recommendation evidence and one row per business; count different recommending posts, not raw comments or presumed customers |

The source asks for exact comments and every named person; the existing public-signal rule is minimal quotation and paraphrase by default. Retain the evidence link and only necessary permitted quotation. A personal service request is not contact consent or permission to resell personal data. [more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md; coreyhaines31/marketingskills, prospecting/FIRST-CUSTOMERS.md]

## Verify and select the offer

[more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md]

Match business name and locality, recording permitted address/phone, relevant rating/review evidence, place ID where used and website status. An uncertain match stays uncertain. The source labels an unconfident match no listing; keep the existing [confidence and website cross-check](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/LOCAL-SMB.md) instead.

The proposed gap labels are no website, fewer than 30 reviews, storefront and phone-based booking. The last two describe product fit rather than necessarily a defect. The source routes the biggest gap to:

| Signal | Demonstration |
|---|---|
| Confirmed website gap | [Website offer](WEBSITE-OFFERS.md) |
| Good rating and low review count | [Review-request automation](REVIEW-AUTOMATION.md) |
| Counter/storefront | [Physical review card](PHYSICAL-REVIEW-CARDS.md) |
| Phone-heavy operation | [Hospitality/phone intake](HOSPITALITY.md) where its scope fits |

The guide gives no multi-gap scoring weights or tie-breaker. Ask the operator to choose rather than inventing one. The shorter combined prompt omits the good-rating qualification; retain that source difference when selecting the review branch.

## Selection digest and contextual demonstration

[more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md]

The main proposal is a weekly digest; Monday at 9 a.m. is the example, and the combined prompt also allows daily delivery. Its example packet contains the five most-requested services and fifteen most-recommended businesses ranked by distinct-post mentions, plus mention count, review count/rating where permitted, gaps and a genuine minimal recommendation quote.

Build nothing until the operator selects a business. Show each selected demonstration. Use [DEMO-FIRST.md](DEMO-FIRST.md#prepare-the-mail-packet) for production and send mechanics, with these additional artifact constraints: actual demo screenshot on the front; factual source, service, date window and recommendation context on the back; correct QR and real operator contact. Verify any last-week wording against the actual date. Show every card before approved sending and retain the send result.

## Sell the subscribed alert service

[more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md]

Onboard business name, trade, served area, explicit request-alert subscription and approved owner destination/channel. Match permitted requests to that trade in or near the chosen area; the source provides no radius. Alert fields are source name, trade, short job summary and original post link. Do not send the same post twice to the same subscriber. Give the operator the weekly alert count.

The source promises alerts within minutes while inspecting only a few times daily, and separately proposes 15 minutes after flagging. Those do not establish a publication-to-alert SLA. Define check cadence separately from detection-to-send targets; use the existing [marketing-loop state and cadence](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-loops/SKILL.md). No source selling price, billing interval or cancellation term is supplied.

Twilio's [Messaging Policy](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/messaging-policy) requires consent even for informational notifications, scoped to the agreed sender and subject. The source's signed-up alert customer is useful onboarding evidence; purchasing a different service alone is not the alert subscription. Channel implementation stays with [SMS](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/sms-marketing/COMPLIANCE.md). [Twilio Messaging Policy; more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md]

## Prove it

[more skills, 05-facebook-group-service-requests.md]

Show permitted source access, the linked request/business records, distinct-post counts, uncertainty on unresolved matches, selection before building and a truthful contextual artifact. For subscribers, show agreed trade/area/channel, original-link alerts, per-recipient duplicate suppression and weekly actual counts. No unverified latency, guaranteed job win or near-zero operating cost is part of the offer.
