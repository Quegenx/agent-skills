# Retail content service

The sample-first retail content offer from [more skills, 06-clothing-store-content.md]: separate stores with observed ads from stores needing an organic reel, prepare a reviewed sample, then deliver an agreed monthly quantity from new-arrival photos. This recipe preserves the service process while keeping existing restrictions on generated depictions of real products.

## Two candidate branches

[more skills, 06-clothing-store-content.md]

Choose one niche/city among clothing, boutiques, furniture, jewelry or decor. Record permitted business name, address, phone, website, sourced Instagram if available, rating and permitted photo evidence. Independent stores with usable product-photo sources are the author's target. Apply the [shared discovery and approval flow](DEMO-FIRST.md#choose-and-inspect-one-market).

Match each store by actual identity/location before Ad Library research. Record observed ad status, saved current creative, original ad text and observed running information. The source proposes ad runners and no ads tabs, then operator selection from both. Existing [Ad Library research](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/ad-creative/CREATIVE-ROADMAP.md) keeps unverified status unknown; the source's forced yes/no does not establish absence of ads.

Route observed ad runners to a revised-ad proposal using their current offer. Route stores with no observed ads and suitable products to an organic reel proposal. A store's ad status is a routing signal, not proof it will buy or that the new creative performs better.

## Product and continuity brief

[more skills, 06-clothing-store-content.md]

The author selects five or six clear single-product shots, preferring plain backgrounds and pieces whose movement would be useful. Its source order is website, Instagram, listing images; applicable rights and platform permissions precede reuse. Keep a folder per store and review the selections for the first three stores. Shared permitted-input and fidelity review belongs in [marketing-images REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md).

Record the actual garment's color, pattern and cut, or furniture/decor identity. The proposed creative brief uses five stills, a consistent adult model/customer style, person, lighting and setting; the furniture/decor alternative uses a styled room. The optional street-style example is full-length framing on a sunny sidewalk with natural light and the product in focus. Five outputs from six inputs needs a production selection; the guide gives no sixth-product exclusion rule.

**Generation conflict:** the source proposes generating the same model wearing the exact merchandise, then generating motion with the model approaching the camera, turning and the fabric moving naturally. Existing [marketing-images](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/SKILL.md) prohibits drawing the product, and [marketing-video](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/videos/marketing-video/SKILL.md#the-rules) prohibits generated footage of specific products and real locations. Keep those existing rules. This source's generated-model branch remains a documented alternative requiring a specific exception; approval of the service recipe alone does not grant it. The remaining selection, edit brief, approval and recurring-client process still apply to permitted real assets. ## Sample delivery and monthly fulfillment

[more skills, 06-clothing-store-content.md]

Prepare a store-specific viewing page with its name, the approved reel, muted playback and a sound-on control, plus actual operator name/number. The source proposes autoplay; [MDN autoplay guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Media/Guides/Autoplay) says browser settings and policy can block playback, so the experience needs an available manual-play route. The source treated autoplay as certain. [MDN]

Use [DEMO-FIRST.md](DEMO-FIRST.md#prepare-the-mail-packet) for the three-card sample and batch approval. The front uses an actual approved reel still; the back identifies the already-made reel and correct viewing QR. Review every creative before delivery, not merely the three mail samples. Export the same approved video file with a short proposed DM for the human sender, subject to [outreach rules](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/cold-outreach/SKILL.md). Do not automate Instagram contact from this guide.

For an accepted client, agree `[x]` reels per month. The owner supplies actual new-arrival photos through the agreed email or WhatsApp intake. Use the approved template and permitted production method, send outputs to the operator first, then to the owner with a suggested caption for approval. The owner posts. Keep the monthly made/approved log and acquisition log of store, asset, link and actual sent date.

No selling price, setup fee or measured production cost is supplied. Do not promise virality, a professional-shoot equivalent or nearly free subsequent reels. Model costs and the number of monthly assets remain actual agreement inputs.
