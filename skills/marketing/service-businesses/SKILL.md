## The rules

1. **Name the offer before the automation.** Select the buyer, concrete artifact/service, proposed trial or pilot, fee unit, actual ongoing work and who owns the delivered account or asset. Leave missing selling prices, percentages, SLAs and attribution periods unresolved rather than supplying a standard package. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; 08-overdue-invoice-collection.md; 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md; 16-guest-house-systems.md]
2. **Start with a qualified, evidenced prospect and an inspectable artifact.** Use the shared [demo flow](DEMO-FIRST.md), keeping its service-specific approval exceptions. A missing field, a public photograph or a demo visit does not establish the business facts or permissions that were not observed. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; 03-nfc-review-cards.md; 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md; owner safety rule]
3. **Separate review from permission to send or operate.** Recipient/list approval, sample approval, a final artifact review, a mail batch and a live client connection are distinct decisions. Preserve the selected guide's actual gates; do not infer indefinite permission from the first sample. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; 02-review-automation-sms.md; 09-chargeback-recovery.md; 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md]
4. **Keep generic methods in one place.** [Prospecting](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/prospecting/SKILL.md) owns evidence and qualification; [cold outreach](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/cold-outreach/SKILL.md) and [SMS](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/sms-marketing/SKILL.md) own permitted contact; [real-photo editing](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md) owns fidelity/QA. Their existing constraints remain when a supplied technique conflicts. Owner app, landing-page and agent-chat designs remain unchanged. [repository standing rules 5, 6, 9 and 11]
5. **Use a decision model only for judgment.** For the results-based service guides, keep the source's proposed menus and review branches, while exact amounts, dates and equality checks remain deterministic. Source thresholds are not calibrated defaults; use the existing [Jev gates and production owner](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/ai-agents/jev-decisions/SKILL.md). The missed-call fixed-template service need not use a model. [more skills, 08-overdue-invoice-collection.md; 09-chargeback-recovery.md; 10-business-bill-audit.md; 11-trades-instant-quotes-winback.md; 20-trades-lead-systems.md]
6. **Report actual events and money.** Keep observed scans/clicks, leads, requests, confirmed bookings, collected payments, recovered funds and credited savings distinct. Invoice or commission statements follow the selected service's actual evidence and agreed fee basis, never a model's predicted result. [more skills, 03-nfc-review-cards.md; 08-overdue-invoice-collection.md; 09-chargeback-recovery.md; 10-business-bill-audit.md; 12-whop-payment-referrals.md; 13-google-maps-local-seo.md]
7. **Name ongoing responsibilities.** A monthly fee corresponds to the specified recurring intake, production, review, monitoring or report. A one-time client-owned page, physical item or room-image job does not silently become a subscription. The owner still approves actual customer-facing changes under the service contract. [more skills, 02-review-automation-sms.md; 03-nfc-review-cards.md; 06-clothing-store-content.md; 13-google-maps-local-seo.md; 16-guest-house-systems.md]
8. **Use the existing durable execution and security contracts.** Scheduled work, retries, per-client access and side-effect controls belong to [background-jobs](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/background-jobs/SKILL.md), [Supabase security](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/supabase/SECURITY.md) and [third-party connection ownership](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/third-party-integrations/SKILL.md). A source prompt or proposed schema is not proof a service was built or isolated. [more skills, 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md; 16-guest-house-systems.md; 20-trades-lead-systems.md]

## A shared integration correction

Jev through AI Gateway uses typed decision APIs; it is not available through Chat Completions or Responses. The source guides 08 to 11 said to call it like an ordinary chat model. Use the current [official decision reference](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/modalities/decision) with the existing Jev question/gate definitions; do not infer integration code from those source prompts. The official page retains earlier evaluation names as deprecated aliases, so a naming change alone does not prove an existing compatible integration is broken.

## Prove it

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md through 20-trades-lead-systems.md]

- The selected service has an evidenced buyer, actual inputs, a concrete demo/deliverable and its source-specific approval gates.
- The pilot and fee units are explicit; missing commercial terms and undefined classifier branches remain visible decisions rather than invented defaults.
- The client receives the promised approved work and an actual-event/outcome record; attribution and unrun production behavior are not overstated.
- Recurring work, account ownership, support and handoff match the selected offer, and each relevant topic's Prove it contract is satisfied or its remaining gap is reported.
