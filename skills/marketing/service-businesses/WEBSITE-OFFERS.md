# Website offers and client ownership

Two distinct website businesses from [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md] and [more skills, 07-free-website-affiliate.md]: sell the build and agreed maintenance to the client, or give away the build and earn a qualifying platform referral. Use [DEMO-FIRST.md](DEMO-FIRST.md) for the shared candidate, preview, postcard and approval sequence.

## Direct build and maintenance

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

Choose one niche and city. The author's candidate settings are no listed website, 10 to 200 reviews and rating 4.0 or higher in the prompts; the prose instead says above 4.0. Resolve that boundary and cross-check real website status before selection. Exclude closed businesses and apply the existing local-authority check to chains/franchisees.

The source's one-page content brief, in order:

1. Business name and a one-line description of what it actually does.
2. Prominent call action using its verified phone.
3. Services, checked against actual business evidence.
4. Permissioned reviews; the author's proposed count is six, not a reason to fabricate missing reviews.
5. Actual permitted business photos.
6. Verified service area.
7. Contact form.

Add a slim proposal banner identifying the actual builder and callback number. Approve the first niche template after phone inspection, then keep its layout fixed while changing each business's data. The source suggests five random batch previews, especially inspecting sparse-photo cases; five is a sample, not proof every site is correct.

**Image and review differences:** the source substitutes niche stock imagery when fewer than three business photos exist and displays reviewer first names. Stock cannot be presented as that business's actual people, work or premises; keep the existing [real-image rules](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/REAL-PHOTO-EDITING.md). Google requires applicable review author/source attribution, not merely first names, under its [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies). [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; Google Places policies]

After acceptance, move the approved site to the client's authorized domain, configure SSL and update the Profile website link only with actual access and approval. If a domain must be purchased, the source proposes buying it in the business's name; purchase still requires the agreed authority and spend. A Profile edit does not guarantee Google will index or show the site: [Google recrawl guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl). The source said the edit would make the site appear when people search. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; Google Search Central]

For agreed monthly maintenance, update actual services, permitted new reviews and owner photos, address requested fixes and send a one-line summary of what really changed. The guide supplies no build price, maintenance fee, minimum term or cancellation rule. Resolve those terms; do not assume every client needs equal work. [more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md]

## Affiliate-funded free build

[more skills, 07-free-website-affiliate.md]

The operator charges nothing for the build in this model. The owner pays any required platform plan and domain costs; the operator earns only an eligible referral commission. This guide supplies neither recurring maintenance scope nor recurring affiliate income. Generic program economics, disclosure and reconciliation stay in [referral-programs AFFILIATES.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/referral-programs/AFFILIATES.md).

1. Apply to the selected program and read accepted terms before building: eligible customers, allowed promotion channels, attribution window, refunds, payout requirements and ownership transfer. Keep the approved referral link available to the workflow.
2. Select the permitted niche/city list. The author proposes no site, 10 to 200 reviews and strictly above 4.0. Get list approval.
3. Record a platform choice and one reason per business. The source proposes Wix for a brochure/contact site and Base44 for an actual booking, quoting or ordering need; its booking illustration selects service, day and time before the booking action. These are source alternatives, not a replacement for the user's stack or proof that every business in an occupation needs an app.
4. Approve one niche template with identity, purpose, call action, services, six available permissioned reviews, photos and service area. Add the selected app flow where needed. Keep business-specific copies unpublished in the operator account and retain each draft link.
5. Show desktop and phone screenshots on the claim preview, name the actual operator and provide the real callback number. The source's single claim CTA uses the approved referral link and avoids competing platform signup links. Explain required costs and the commission relationship beside that offer.
6. Follow the shared mail packet. After a confirmed signup, match the available identity to the business row, notify the operator and prepare transfer to the exact owner account for approval. Do not assume dashboard identity fields or every transfer route exist.
7. After confirmed transfer, prepare the applicable upgrade/domain/publication guidance. Track signup, ownership transfer, eligible paid conversion, refund/hold state and commission separately.

The author proposes checking dashboards every few hours and transferring within a day. Those are optional service commitments; verify transfer eligibility before offering them. The source equated claims with commissions and treated an upgrade as locked payment. The [Wix FAQ](https://www.wix.com/about/affiliates/faq) instead conditions the qualifying referral on the Premium user passing its 14-day trial without cancellation, with first-click attribution, a 30-day cookie, a $300 payout threshold and invoicing. Its flat $100 rate is not established by that FAQ. [more skills, 07-free-website-affiliate.md; Wix Affiliate FAQ]

The [Base44 affiliate page](https://base44.com/affiliates) advertises $100, a 30-day cookie, monthly processing and a $300 threshold, but describes paid conversion relative to its lock-in day. The source anchored a month to signup. Use the accepted current program terms rather than resetting an attribution clock at signup or transfer. Cross-account draft transfer and this campaign's eligibility remain unresolved implementation inputs. [more skills, 07-free-website-affiliate.md; Base44 affiliates]

For US offers, state the conditions of receiving and keeping a free item clearly at the outset under the [FTC Free guide](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/guide-concerning-use-word-free-similar-representations), and follow the existing [commission disclosure rule](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/referral-programs/AFFILIATES.md#disclosure). The source's free headline and claim button omitted those nearby conditions and disclosure. [FTC Free guide; FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ]

## Prove it

[more skills, 01-website-sales-postcard-pipeline.md; more skills, 07-free-website-affiliate.md]

Identify the payer and distinguish build, hosting, domain, maintenance and referral terms. Show the approved niche template, truthful content and preview ownership. For direct work, show the authorized domain/Profile handoff and actual monthly changes. For the affiliate branch, show accepted eligibility/attribution terms, nearby disclosures, exact recipient transfer approval and actual conversion/commission states. No signup is counted as paid commission and no undefined support obligation is promised.
