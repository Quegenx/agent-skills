# Review-request automation service

The recurring post-job review service from [more skills, 02-review-automation-sms.md]. Its distinct deliverables are a live inbound-code demonstration, completed-job intake, a bounded request/reminder flow and a four-field monthly owner report. Shared acquisition is [DEMO-FIRST.md](DEMO-FIRST.md); neutral reviewer eligibility and platform rules are [review-management REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/review-management/REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md).

## Prospect and comparison packet

[more skills, 02-review-automation-sms.md]

Choose a niche/city where completed work creates a genuine reviewable experience. The source suggests plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, auto repair, dentistry and landscaping, with rating at least 4.0, fewer than 50 reviews and a published business phone. Those select prospective businesses, not happy-only customer reviewers.

The proposed worksheet contains name, address, phone, rating, review count, place ID and an evidenced owner name if available. Name it for niche/city/reviews and show the count. Use the shared sourcing rules. Add three high-review peers in the same niche/city and, for each prospect, the nearest relevant peer within the chosen radius, its name/count and an accurate comparison. The source gives no fallback when no relevant competitor exists; leave the comparison unresolved rather than inventing one. Approve recipients before production.

## Signed-client intake and delivery

[more skills, 02-review-automation-sms.md]

1. Start with an owner form or authorized owner message recording customer name/phone after a completed job. The source later proposes connecting the client's existing completion event from Jobber, Housecall Pro or Square; it supplies no verified adapter contract.
2. Apply the neutral experience-based cohort and authorization rules in [review-management REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/review-management/REVIEW-PROGRAMS.md#requesting-reviews) and [SMS compliance](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/sms-marketing/COMPLIANCE.md). Owner possession of a customer number does not establish permission for this messaging program.
3. The author's example cadence waits three hours after completion, sends a named-business honest-review request with the actual review link, then allows one reminder after three days only for an evidenced eligible nonresponder.
4. Cap this source flow at two messages per customer. The guide supplies no reset period for later jobs; resolve that scope before reenrolling someone. It also does not establish reliable individual review attribution, so do not equate no matching public review with proof of nonresponse.
5. Log observed review count daily and retain actual request outcomes. Shared sender identity, consent, opt-out and suppression rules govern every send.

Twilio's [Messaging Policy](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/messaging-policy) requires consent scoped to sender and subject and downstream written consent for an application serving customers. An inbound demo starts that conversation, not unlimited recurring promotion. The source's operator approval and completed-job phone intake omit that consent evidence. [Twilio Messaging Policy]

## Monthly client report and commercial scope

[more skills, 02-review-automation-sms.md]

On the first of the month, prepare the previous month's requests sent, observed new reviews, current total and current gap to the original postcard competitor. Show the operator the first two months before owner delivery; later automation needs the actual agreed grant. A review-count change is not proof the program caused every new review or improved rank.

This is an ongoing monthly service, but the source supplies no selling price, onboarding fee, term, cancellation rule or SLA. Set those with the owner. Do not substitute the source's postcard cost estimate for the service fee or promise more calls from a larger review count.
