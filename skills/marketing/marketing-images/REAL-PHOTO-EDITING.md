# Editing an actual subject

Reference-photo preservation and subject-specific acceptance contracts, paraphrased from [more skills, 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md], [more skills, 15-restaurant-food-visuals.md], [more skills, 16-guest-house-systems.md], [more skills, 17-estate-agent-listings.md], [more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md], [more skills, 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md] and [more skills, 20-trades-lead-systems.md]. These guides propose editing real supplied photographs, rather than inventing a similar subject. Their generation methods remain subject to the existing policies below. Service packaging and sales belong to [service-businesses](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/service-businesses/SKILL.md).

## Start with permission and the original

Use an authorized photograph of the exact subject, compare the output against that original, and reject unintended changes before delivery. Keep the comparison available within the photograph's permitted retention. Public visibility, free delivery and depicting a business do not establish editing or redistribution permission. [more skills, 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md; 15-restaurant-food-visuals.md; 17-estate-agent-listings.md]

- The photographer is generally the initial copyright owner in the US, subject to defined work-made-for-hire exceptions. Paying for photography alone does not establish ownership. The property guide instead assumed the commissioning agent owned it. [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/photographers/)
- UK guidance likewise normally gives the creator ownership, with employment and contractual exceptions; copyright covers copying, adaptation and distribution, including free copies. The food guide instead asserted a universal right to edit a public customer's photo and give it to the business. [UK ownership guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ownership-of-copyright-works), [copyright overview](https://www.gov.uk/copyright)
- Street View imagery may not be printed or downloaded for separate use; permitted digital advertising uses are constrained. The shop guide instead downloaded a frame for editing and an A4 handout. Use independently captured or rights-cleared input for that workflow. [Google geo guidelines](https://about.google/intl/eng/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/)

## Define what may change

[more skills, 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md; 15-restaurant-food-visuals.md; 16-guest-house-systems.md; 17-estate-agent-listings.md; 18-motor-trade-visuals.md; 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md; 20-trades-lead-systems.md]

Before editing, identify the subject, the permitted changes and the details that must remain. Supply observed materials and visible regions rather than letting a model invent them. Inspect the full-size original and result side by side, specifically looking for removed defects, changed proportions and altered lettering. A detailed prompt is a constraint, not proof that the output complied. Save the exact model and prompt under [SKILL.md's evidence requirement](SKILL.md#prove-it).

Choose the matching branch below. A proposed installation or treatment must read as a proposal, while an image representing existing stock, premises or completed work must preserve its actual condition. Do not call an edited illustration an unchanged photograph.

## Solar roof illustrations

[more skills, 14-solar-rooftop-renders.md]

- Inputs: exact property photograph, supported panel count and dimensions, observed roof material, visible roof description and explicit areas to leave clear. Translate independently known compass orientation into an image-relative region; do not expect a direction word alone to identify the intended plane.
- Residential concept: dark all-black panels in landscape rows, following the actual roof pitch, perspective and vanishing lines. The source proposes low black rails about 10 cm above the roof. Preserve clear roof edges and obstacles, with no overhang or panels covering chimneys, skylights or vents. These are illustration instructions, not certified installation dimensions.
- Match the existing sky reflections and shadow direction. Keep walls, doors, windows, garden, boundary, vehicles, sky, exposure, grain and framing unchanged. Compare a chimney shadow with the new array's shadows. Reject a uniform blue slab, conflicting light, impossible overhang or covered obstacle.
- Commercial concept: use an authorized overhead photograph and describe the visible building and roof bays. Align dark panel blocks with the existing long axis and bays, with separate maintenance gaps and visible rooflight/vent/plant exclusions. The source proposes approximately 1 m gangways and 1.5 m perimeter/obstacle gaps; neither is a verified engineering requirement.
- Match overhead angle, resolution, image softness, color and light; vary reflections rather than painting one flat rectangle. Preserve the yard, vehicles, materials, roads, trees and neighboring buildings. Visually inspect for glass areas and already installed panels before approving.
- Count the visible panels against the caption; an illustration cannot claim the count, capacity or production of a different configuration. Show the measurement source/date and an installation-design disclaimer as specified by [service-businesses SOLAR-PREVIEWS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/service-businesses/SOLAR-PREVIEWS.md).

## Food and cafe premises

[more skills, 15-restaurant-food-visuals.md]

- Dish inputs: actual photograph and dish identity. Preserve every ingredient, portion, arrangement, untidy detail, plate, cup, cutlery and table. No added garnish, herbs, sauce, steam, props or extra saturation. The proposed changes are light, viewpoint and crop only.
- The source's food direction is soft one-sided light, gentle falloff, a lower/closer view, less empty frame, soft background and level horizon. Reject a studio restyling. Acceptance question: can this kitchen serve the exact plate shown today?
- Cafe premises: retain building, sign, windows, door, furniture, fittings, neighboring shops and actual seating. No invented plants, furniture, people or lights. Proposed changes are warm late-afternoon light, straight verticals, level horizon, tighter crop, restrained shadow lift and pavement-litter cleanup. Someone who knows the premises must recognize them.
- A four-image set and exact menu transcription have different acceptance contracts. Their ordered deliverables, menu uncertainty marker and proofreading/signoff gate are in [service-businesses FOOD-VISUALS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/service-businesses/FOOD-VISUALS.md).

## Guest bedrooms

[more skills, 16-guest-house-systems.md]

Preserve walls, ceiling, windows, beams/slopes, bed position, headboard, furniture, fittings and floor. Do not repaint, replace carpet, move or replace furniture, enlarge the room or add artwork, plants, throws, cushions or other props. Permitted preparation is making the existing bed, squaring duvet/pillows, removing personal clutter, switching existing bedside/table lamps on, soft warm window daylight and landscape framing from a corner at chest height with straight verticals. A previous guest must recognize the same room, rather than a renovation. The [hospitality offer](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/service-businesses/HOSPITALITY.md) owns delivery and room-film decisions.

## Empty-property staging

[more skills, 17-estate-agent-listings.md]

Use the permitted original empty-room photograph plus an explicit furniture list. Keep wall positions/proportions, ceiling height, windows, door, radiator, light fitting, skirting, floor/color, sockets, marks and defects. Do not widen, repaint, recarpet, straighten actual defects or hide damp/damage. Only add the specified furniture, sized and priced to suit the property, with ample visible floor. Check floor contact, scale against doors/windows, shadows and reflections; preserve original camera/framing and only relight enough to integrate the furniture.

Keep a legible virtual-staging disclosure inside the frame far enough to survive cropping. Do not remove it at a client's request. It does not replace applicable disclosure and original-image requirements: California Business and Professions Code 10140.8 requires reasonably conspicuous alteration disclosure, access to clearly identified originals and specified internet-posting treatment. The source instead claimed that a two-word label everywhere removed any need to know local rules. [California AB 723](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB723)

## Vehicles and treatment previews

[more skills, 18-motor-trade-visuals.md]

- Listing identity: preserve model, proportions, paint/color/finish, exact wheels/tyres, trim, badges and ride height. Keep kerbing, scuffs, scratches, chips, dents, rust, cracked lenses and mismatched paint at their original visibility. No body kit, lowering, changed wheels or unsolicited tint.
- The proposed listing treatment isolates one actual car on clean level ground with a neutral soft background, warm low front-left light, a long body highlight, wheel-hub-height view and level horizon. Do not invent unseen condition or mileage when changing the view. Reject added reflections, altered badges/model lettering, changed wheel spokes or repaired damage.
- Wrap: change painted panels only, using the supplied color and finish. Preserve glass, black trim, grille, lights, unwrapped mirror caps and wheels; follow body lines and panel gaps. The source asks satin to show soft reflection gradients and gloss to show crisp reflections.
- Tint changes only the selected glass; detailing changes gloss/depth/reflection in the existing color while retaining every visible scratch, chip and blemish. The source proposes soft overcast light to make the finish readable.
- A color preview travels with the actual physical swatch reference and a permanent indicative-color/physical-sample warning, set under the existing text policy below. A render is not color approval for manufacture.
- Blank plates, including background vehicles and reflections in windows or wet ground, is the author's chosen operating policy. Its claim that this is a universal legal requirement is not established.
- Tint needs the applicable local specification. In the UK, first-use date matters: from 1 April 1985, front windscreen transmission is at least 75% and front-side at least 70%; before then both are at least 70%. The source's lighter-windscreen-than-sides instruction was not a legal specification. [GOV.UK tint rules](https://www.gov.uk/tinted-vehicle-window-rules)
- Van preview: preserve the actual van's make/model/size/color, wheels, stance, dirt/dents, position, camera and background. Proposed graphics occupy the side panel only: a lower-third color band following the body, large trade name, smaller business name, one number and a supplied authorized accreditation mark. Plain heavy sans-serif, left aligned, with most panel space left empty; no invented website, social icons, stock people, gradients, swooshes, glow or decorative fill. Match curves, gaps, perspective, light and sheen. Keep original van color; the source's instruction to leave a van white cannot override a differently colored input.

## Shopfronts, lighting proposals and stock

[more skills, 19-shop-storefront-visuals.md]

- Existing shopfront: retain fascia, exact lettering/typeface/colors, every displayed product, door, windows, stall risers, worn paint, materials, neighbors and age. No modernization, repainting, display restyling, removed products or invented plants, seating, awnings, people or installed lighting.
- Proposed presentation edits: warm late-afternoon street light, straight verticals, level horizon, tighter crop, slight window-shadow lift and removal of specified litter/dead wiring/grime outside the shop's character. Inspect lettering and neighboring frontage side by side, and inspect an actual print. A claim that only light changed is false if cropping or cleanup also changed.
- Night installation proposal: same building and existing sign, blue hour, restrained warm fascia downlights, window-interior light and one soft doorway-ground pool. No new sign/logo, neon, colored wash, uplighting, flare or illumination without a plausible fitting. Label/present the work as proposed, rather than an existing installation. The source's never-brighter-than-the-street rule conflicts internally with its slightly-brightest-shop prompt; choose the intended brightness before production.
- Actual stock object: preserve shape, scale, material, color, finish, wear, patina, manufacturing marks and imperfections. No cleaning, polish, repair, straightening or substitution. Proposed surrounding changes are a selected seamless background, one soft upper-left light/lower-right shadow, centered eye-level framing with space, and true color. No props, hands, packaging, studio reflections or additional products. Compare the image with the actual object.
- The source requests literal identical sign pixels despite relighting/reframing. Preserve lettering and design as an acceptance requirement; do not promise pixel identity that has not been demonstrated.

## Finished trade work

[more skills, 20-trades-lead-systems.md]

Use actual finished-work photographs and, when available, actual pre-work photographs for a matched pair. Preserve materials, tiles, grout, fitting brands, fixture positions, paint, floor, silicone, edges, joints and existing defects. Keep room size/proportions, window, door and ceiling. An uneven tile or grout line stays uneven. Permitted edits remove the trade's temporary tools, offcuts, packaging, dust sheets, cleaning products and personal clutter; replace flash with soft light from the existing window; correct camera verticals/horizon; and reframe honestly from a corner at chest height. Correcting camera perspective must not straighten faulty workmanship. Both the customer and trade must recognize the actual job, with no improvement to the workmanship itself.

## Prove it

The input rights and exact subject are established; the selected branch lists permitted changes and invariants; full-size comparison shows no changed product, geometry, defect, workmanship or lettering outside that contract; proposed work and staging are disclosed; required originals remain accessible under applicable rules; exact model/prompt are saved; print deliverables were inspected on paper; and no conflicting method was treated as an approved generic-policy change.
