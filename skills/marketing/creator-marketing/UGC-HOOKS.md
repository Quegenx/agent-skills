# UGC hooks

Openings for creator and UGC short-form video on TikTok and Instagram Reels. Everything here is `[lightreel]`: lightreel.ai's UGC playbook (April 2026) and its weekly October 2026 reports on talking-head, trending, consumer-app, skincare, K-beauty, fitness and food videos, paraphrased with short quoted hook lines. The reports are one-week observational samples that measure likes and views, not sales; the site itself calls them directional, so treat every pattern as a hypothesis to test. "The formula creates eligibility, not guaranteed virality." Hooks for your own posts and organic video scripts are in [social-media POSTS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/social-media/POSTS.md) and [social-media SHORT-VIDEO.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/social-media/SHORT-VIDEO.md); paid-ad hook rules are in [app-growth TIKTOK-ADS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/app-growth/TIKTOK-ADS.md#hooks).

## The spine

Every video: a hook in the first 3 seconds whose only job is to stop the scroll, then the problem, then the solution presented naturally, not as an ad. App-growth and the video skills say the same about the opening ("a hook on frame 0", [make-video SKILL.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/videos/make-video/SKILL.md)); app-growth's TikTok formats put "0 to 2 s hook text" first `[appeeky]`.

## Hook types

- **Visual:** a striking image.
- **Verbal tick:** "HUH?", "OMG?", "Bro what?".
- **Qualifier:** "If you're a teacher, stop scrolling" (app-growth's Format 3 uses "Stop scrolling if you [persona trait]" `[appeeky]`).
- **Story opener:** "I was at my mother-in-law's house when I saw the CRAZIEST thing".
- **Editing hook:** splice the juiciest moment into the first 3 seconds as a teaser.

## Talking-head openers

The first sentence contains one of: conflict, consequence, audience, credential, or an unexpected method. Seven openers observed:

1. Stitch and reject ("No, you don't. And I actually think that we need to sit and unpack…").
2. A precise audience, then a pivot.
3. A debatable thesis with its logic explained.
4. A costly consequence ("Storytime of how I lost customers because…").
5. A verifiable credential first.
6. An unexpected mechanism, then the demo.
7. A source clip, then the creator becomes the guide.

**Founder stories** run decision, measurable pain, reason, evidence of rebuilding, present outcome; replace "as a founder" with the actual event.

## Phrase families and formulas

- **Discovery:** "Why did nobody tell me about this sooner?", "How am I only just finding out about this?"
- **Disbelief:** "I thought this was a joke until…"
- **Reveal:** "Not me finding out this way."
- **Identity escalation:** "I fear I may have taken this too far."
- **Formulas:** "Why is nobody talking about this?" (an under-recognized thing, evidence, interpretation); "I didn't expect this to work" (a failed expectation, demo, result); "Watch this before you buy it" (purchase risk, overlooked evidence, verdict).
- **Structures:** criticism answered by a visual rebuttal; the finished payoff, then the method; one specific annoyance, one fix; a skill shown in an uncomfortable context. Use "POV" only when a concrete situation follows.

## Instagram hooks

POV plus a social consequence; a familiar question with an absurd response; "Remember when this was free?"; an impossible-looking claim plus proof; numbered utility ("Five one-pan dinners…"); an authority reacting to a provocative question; an identity or fandom transformation; a hyper-specific character label.

## Category hooks

Seen in beauty, fitness and food reports:

- **Decision:** "which one is for you?"
- **Preventive regret:** "3 habits I wish I started in my 20s"
- **Contrarian guardrail:** "Before you add another exfoliant…, watch this"
- **Quantified curiosity:** "400 drops in one bottle?!"
- **Insider access:** "Come with me to…"
- **Disqualifier:** "If you don't want hydrated skin, keep scrolling"
- **Condition first:** "Dark spots + uneven skin tone?"
- **Skeptical:** "Overhyped"
- **Decision rule:** "When to use hot and when to use cold"
- **One regret:** "If I could go back and train for my first marathon and change one thing…"
- **Mild social jeopardy:** "pls like this so we don't get in trouble" (a like request; [social-media POSTS.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/social-media/POSTS.md#no-ai-tells) counts asks like this as engagement bait for your own posts)

## App hooks

Experiential novelty ("We have events on Tinder now"); the aspirational result first; relationship projection ("US?"); interactive prediction (guess the calories before the reveal); concrete proof numbers; a cultural remix left unexplained. Make the CTA match activation and where the screen goes in [UGC-FORMATS.md](UGC-FORMATS.md#app-videos).

## Captions and proof-first openings

- Write narrower captions: name the exact private behavior viewers recognize but rarely say out loud.
- Open on the result, the receipt, the stress test or the interface outcome; "honest review" positioning is weaker than showing the test.

## Prove it

Each video's first three seconds carry one named hook type that a viewer could repeat; the hook leads into a stated problem before the product appears as the solution; variant openings on one body are different reasons to keep watching, not synonyms; any number or result in a hook is real and substantiated ([DISCLOSURE.md](DISCLOSURE.md#honest-endorsements)); and hook patterns are logged with results so the next brief starts from what held attention.
