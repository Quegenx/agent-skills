## Ebooks and guides

**Best for:** building authority, deep education, awareness-stage leads.

Structure:

1. Title page with professional design.
2. Table of contents.
3. Introduction: frame the problem, set expectations.
4. 3-7 chapters, one key concept per chapter.
5. Summary: recap key takeaways.
6. CTA: next step toward your product.

Guidelines: "Ideal length: 10-25 pages (shorter is fine if valuable)"; include visuals (charts, diagrams, screenshots); use callout boxes for key stats or quotes; end each chapter with a quick takeaway; "Don't pad - density beats length". Tools the source names: Canva, Google Docs to PDF, Notion export, Designrr, Beacon.by.

## Checklists

**Best for:** process-oriented tasks, quick wins, implementation help.

- Title: "[Number]-Point [Topic] Checklist".
- Numbered or checkbox items; group into logical sections if 10+ items; a brief explanation per item (1-2 sentences).
- Keep to 1-2 pages; actionable language ("Verify X", "Set up Y", "Remove Z"); order by workflow sequence or priority.
- Make it printable (clean layout, generous spacing) with a "done" checkbox for each item.

What works: step-by-step processes, audit criteria, launch checklists, setup guides.

## Cheat sheets

**Best for:** reference material, shortcuts, quick-lookup information.

- One page (two pages max), organized by category or workflow, dense but scannable, visual hierarchy with headers and grouping.
- "Optimize for quick reference, not reading": tables, grids or columns; formulas, shortcuts or code snippets; designed for printing or saving as a desktop reference; bold the most important items.

What works: keyboard shortcuts, formula references, terminology glossaries, decision matrices.

## Templates and spreadsheets

**Best for:** repeatable processes, planning, tracking.

- **Spreadsheets (Google Sheets, Excel):** a "How to Use" tab with instructions; example data pre-filled; data validation for dropdown fields; conditional formatting for visual cues; "Lock formula cells, leave input cells editable"; a "Make a Copy" link for Google Sheets.
- **Notion:** a duplicate link; a getting-started guide; example content; Notion's database features (views, filters, relations); "Keep it simple - don't over-engineer".
- **Documents:** multiple formats (Google Doc, Word, PDF); placeholder text in [BRACKETS]; inline instructions in a different color; immediately usable with minimal editing.

"Templates should be usable within 5 minutes of downloading."

## Swipe files

**Best for:** inspiration, examples, learning from others.

- A curated collection of 15-50 examples, organized by category, type or use case.
- Each example: the example itself (screenshot, text, link); why it works (2-3 bullet annotations); how to adapt it (1-2 sentences).
- Quality over quantity; add your analysis, don't just collect; organize for browsing; update periodically; credit original sources.

What works: email subject lines, landing pages, ad copy, CTAs, onboarding flows, pricing pages.

## Mini-courses

- **Email:** 3-5 emails over 5-7 days; one lesson per email, one concept per lesson; each email "teach → example → exercise"; progressive difficulty; the final email is a summary plus a CTA.
- **Video:** 3-5 videos, 5-15 minutes each; hosted on unlisted YouTube, Loom or a course platform; links delivered by email drip; worksheets or exercises per lesson.
- **Cadence:** every 1-2 days. "Each lesson should deliver standalone value. If someone only watches lesson 2, they should still learn something useful."

The signup form states this schedule: Yahoo's rule, as this repo records it, is to "honor the frequency the list promised" ([email-practices LISTS-AND-CAPTURE.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/backend/email-practices/LISTS-AND-CAPTURE.md#marketing-emails)).

## Quizzes and assessments

**Best for:** engagement, segmentation, personalized results.

- **Questions:** 5-10 ("sweet spot: 7"); multiple choice only, no open-ended; insightful, not obvious; a progress indicator ("Question 3 of 7").
- **Results:** 3-5 result categories, each with a name, description and personalized recommendations; tailor follow-up emails by result type; a share-worthy result format ("I got: Growth Stage Marketer!").
- **Gating:** "Gate results behind email capture. The quiz itself is ungated." For EU users the result may not depend on marketing consent ([GATING-AND-DELIVERY.md](GATING-AND-DELIVERY.md#consent-and-double-opt-in)).

Building the quiz as an interactive tool is in [FREE-TOOLS.md](FREE-TOOLS.md).

## Webinars and workshops

- **Live:** "30-45 minutes teaching + 15 minutes Q&A"; structure "Hook → Teach (3 key points) → Demo/example → CTA"; promote 1-2 weeks ahead; "Send 3 reminder emails (confirmation, day before, 1 hour before)"; record for replay.
- **Evergreen:** pre-recorded, on demand; the same structure with tighter editing; gated with email registration; an automated follow-up sequence.
- **Follow-up:** "Send replay link + summary + CTA within 24 hours", then the nurture sequence.

"Teach something genuinely useful. A webinar that's just a sales pitch will damage trust." Event promotion, registration and attendance work is in [event-marketing](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/event-marketing/SKILL.md).

## Prove it

Each magnet follows its format's limits (a checklist within two pages, a cheat sheet on one, a quiz with 5-10 multiple-choice questions and 3-5 results, a template usable in five minutes); a swipe file credits every example's source; an email course's schedule matches what the form promised; and a webinar sends its three reminders and the replay within 24 hours.
