# Planning a lead magnet

What to ask, what makes a magnet work, which format fits which stage, and what to hand back. All from [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets] unless tagged otherwise; read `.agents/product-context.md` first ([anti-slop-copy CONTEXT.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/CONTEXT.md)) and ask only what it leaves open.

## Before planning

Gather (ask if not provided) [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets]:

- **Business:** what the company does, who the ideal customer is, what problems the product solves.
- **Current lead generation:** how leads are captured now, which magnets or offers exist, the current conversion rate on email capture.
- **Content assets:** existing content to repurpose (blog posts, guides, data), expertise you can package, templates or tools used internally.
- **Goals:** list growth, lead quality or product education; the target stage (awareness, consideration, decision); timeline and resources.

Task questions:

1. What existing content or expertise could you turn into a lead magnet?
2. Where does your audience spend time online?
3. What's the most common question prospects ask before buying?
4. Do you have an email nurture sequence set up for new leads?
5. What's your budget for design and promotion?

## The five principles

From [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets]:

1. **Solve a specific problem:** one clear pain point, not a broad topic. "How to write cold emails that get replies" > "Marketing guide".
2. **Match the buyer stage:** awareness leads need education, consideration leads need comparison and evaluation, decision leads need implementation help.
3. **High perceived value, low time investment:** "Should look like it's worth paying for"; "Consumable in under 30 minutes (ideally under 10)"; an immediate, actionable takeaway.
4. **Natural path to product:** it solves a problem your product also solves, shows a gap the product fills, and demonstrates expertise in the space.
5. **Easy to consume:** "One clear format (don't mix ebook + video + spreadsheet)", works on mobile, no special software.

## Formats

Effort and time are the source's estimates [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets]:

| Type | Best for | Effort | Time to create |
|---|---|---|---|
| Checklist | Quick wins, process steps | Low | 1-2 hours |
| Cheat sheet | Reference material, shortcuts | Low | 2-4 hours |
| Template (doc, spreadsheet, Notion) | Repeatable processes, workflows | Low-Med | 2-8 hours |
| Swipe file | Inspiration, examples | Medium | 4-8 hours |
| Ebook or guide | Deep education, authority | High | 1-3 weeks |
| Mini-course (email) | Education and nurture | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
| Mini-course (video) | Education and personality | High | 2-4 weeks |
| Quiz or assessment | Segmentation, engagement | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
| Webinar | Authority, live engagement | Medium | 1 week prep |
| Resource library | Ongoing value, return visits | High | Ongoing |
| Free trial or community access | Product experience | Varies | Varies |

How to build each one is in [FORMATS.md](FORMATS.md). An interactive tool (calculator, grader) is in [FREE-TOOLS.md](FREE-TOOLS.md); a content archive packaged as an agent skill is in [skill-authoring CONTENT-TO-SKILL.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/agent-workflows/skill-authoring/CONTENT-TO-SKILL.md).

**A static magnet or a free tool:** the source's eval expects the answer to separate static content from an interactive tool, compare the effort to build, compare SEO and shareability, and choose on speed against investment [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets evals].

## Matching the format to the stage

The example titles are the source's [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets]:

| Stage and goal | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness: educate on the problem, reach people who don't know you | Checklist | "10-Point Website Audit Checklist" |
| | Cheat sheet | "SEO Cheat Sheet for Beginners" |
| | Ebook or guide | "The Complete Guide to Email Marketing" |
| | Quiz | "What Type of Marketer Are You?" |
| Consideration: help evaluate solutions, build trust | Comparison template | "CRM Comparison Spreadsheet" |
| | Assessment | "Marketing Maturity Assessment" |
| | Case study collection | "5 Companies That 3x'd Their Pipeline" |
| | Webinar | "How to Choose the Right Analytics Tool" |
| Decision: help implement, remove friction to purchase | Template | "Ready-to-Use Sales Email Templates" |
| | Free trial | "14-Day Free Trial" |
| | Implementation guide | "Migration Checklist: Switch in 30 Minutes" |
| | ROI calculator | "Calculate Your Savings" ([FREE-TOOLS.md](FREE-TOOLS.md)) |

Many downloads and few trials: the source's eval diagnoses a lead quality or buyer stage mismatch, checks the lead quality signals (including ICP fit), reviews the nurture sequence, and may suggest a consideration- or decision-stage magnet [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets evals]. A long magnet that converts poorly (its example: a 50-page ebook at 4%) points first at a length and effort mismatch: test a shorter format, break it into smaller pieces, review the page, and ask whether the traffic is cold or warm.

## What to hand back

The strategy has five sections [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, lead-magnets]:

1. **Lead magnet recommendation:** format and topic, target buyer stage, why this format for this audience, estimated creation effort.
2. **Content outline:** key sections or components, length and scope, what makes it unique or valuable.
3. **Gating and capture plan:** what to gate and how, form fields, landing page structure ([GATING-AND-DELIVERY.md](GATING-AND-DELIVERY.md)).
4. **Distribution plan:** promotion channels, content upgrade opportunities, paid amplification if any ([PROMOTION-AND-MEASUREMENT.md](PROMOTION-AND-MEASUREMENT.md)).
5. **Measurement plan:** KPIs and targets, what to A/B test first.

## Prove it

The plan names one problem and one stage; the format fits that stage; the effort estimate is labelled as an estimate; every example title or case it proposes is real for this product; and all five output sections are present.
