# One-pagers and leave-behinds

Templates for the one-pager by use case, with layout guidance and copy prompts. From coreyhaines31/marketingskills, skill `sales-enablement` (SKILL.md and references/one-pager-templates.md, MIT). The deliverable is full copy with layout guidance (visual hierarchy, sections).

## When and how

[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, sales-enablement]

- **Post-meeting recap:** reinforce what you discussed, keep momentum.
- **Champion internal selling:** arm your champion to sell for you.
- **Trade show handout:** a quick intro that drives follow-up.

Structure: (1) problem statement, the pain in one sentence; (2) your solution, what you do and how; (3) 3 differentiators, why you vs. alternatives; (4) proof point, one strong metric or customer quote; (5) CTA, a clear next step with contact info.

Design principles:

- One page, literally. Front only, or front and back maximum.
- Scannable in 30 seconds. Bold headers, short bullets, whitespace.
- Include your logo, website, and a specific contact (not info@).
- Match your brand but keep it clean: "this is a sales tool, not a brand piece."

Quotes and metrics are real ones the customer agreed to show ([SALES-ASSETS.md](SALES-ASSETS.md#case-study-briefs-sales-format)); a template slot you can't fill stays a visible placeholder or comes out.

## Product overview one-pager

[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, sales-enablement] The default one-pager. Introduces your product to someone who knows nothing about you.

```
[Logo]                                              [Tagline]

HEADLINE: One sentence describing what you do and who it's for.

THE PROBLEM
2-3 sentences describing the pain your buyer faces.

THE SOLUTION
2-3 sentences describing how your product solves it.

WHY [YOUR PRODUCT]
• Differentiator 1 - One sentence explaining the benefit
• Differentiator 2 - One sentence explaining the benefit
• Differentiator 3 - One sentence explaining the benefit

PROOF
"Customer quote with specific result." - Name, Title, Company
[Optional: 2-3 metric callouts: "X% improvement", "Y hours saved"]

[CTA Button/Link]                    [Contact: name@company.com]
```

Copy prompts:

- Headline: "What do you do, in one sentence, that makes someone say 'tell me more'?"
- Problem: "What is your buyer struggling with before they find you?"
- Differentiators: "If you could only tell them 3 things, what would make them choose you?"

## Use-case one-pager

[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, sales-enablement] Tailored to a specific workflow, vertical, or problem. More targeted than the product overview.

```
[Logo]                                    [Use Case: e.g., "For Sales Teams"]

HEADLINE: How [your product] helps [persona] [achieve outcome].

THE CHALLENGE
When [persona] needs to [task], they face [specific pain].
This leads to [consequence]: [time wasted / money lost / risk].

HOW IT WORKS
1. [Step 1] - What happens and why it matters
2. [Step 2] - What happens and why it matters
3. [Step 3] - What happens and why it matters

RESULTS
• [Metric 1]: Before → After
• [Metric 2]: Before → After
• [Metric 3]: Before → After

CUSTOMER SPOTLIGHT
"Quote about this specific use case." - Name, Title, Company

[CTA: "See it in action" or "Start a pilot"]       [Contact info]
```

Use it when different buyer personas need different one-pagers, for industry versions (healthcare, fintech, e-commerce) and for use-case versions (reporting, onboarding, security).

## Post-meeting leave-behind

[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, sales-enablement] Reinforces a conversation that already happened: summarizes what you discussed and proposes next steps.

```
[Logo]                                            [Date of Meeting]

MEETING RECAP: [Company Name]

WHAT WE DISCUSSED
• [Pain point 1 they mentioned]
• [Pain point 2 they mentioned]
• [Goal they're trying to achieve]

HOW [YOUR PRODUCT] HELPS
• [Solution to pain 1] - [Specific capability or workflow]
• [Solution to pain 2] - [Specific capability or workflow]
• [How you help them reach their goal]

RELEVANT PROOF
"Quote from a similar customer." - Name, Title, Company
[1-2 metrics from a similar customer]

PROPOSED NEXT STEPS
1. [Next step with date]
2. [Follow-up action]
3. [Decision timeline]

[Your name]  |  [Your title]  |  [Email]  |  [Phone]
```

- Send within 24 hours of the meeting.
- Reference specific things they said (shows you listened).
- Keep proposed next steps concrete and time-bound.
- "This is the asset your champion forwards to their boss."

## Champion enablement one-pager

[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, sales-enablement] For your internal champion to share with their team and leadership. Written to make them look smart.

```
[Logo]

WHY WE'RE EVALUATING [YOUR PRODUCT]

THE SITUATION
[2-3 sentences about the internal challenge, written as if the champion
is explaining it to their team. Use "we" and "our" language.]

WHAT [YOUR PRODUCT] DOES
[1-2 sentences. Plain language, no jargon.]

WHY THIS SOLUTION
• [Reason 1] - How it solves our specific problem
• [Reason 2] - How it compares to what we do today
• [Reason 3] - How it compares to alternatives we evaluated

EXPECTED IMPACT
• [Metric]: Current state → Expected state
• [Metric]: Current state → Expected state
• [Time to value]: Live within [X weeks]

WHO ELSE USES IT
[2-3 recognizable company names in their industry]
"Relevant customer quote." - Name, Title, Company

NEXT STEPS
• [What we're doing next]
• [What we need from the team]
• [Decision timeline]

Questions? Talk to [Champion name] or [Your name at email].
```

Why it works: written in the champion's voice, not yours; answers the questions their boss will ask; includes peer proof from companies they respect; a clear ask and timeline drive internal momentum.

## Layout

[coreyhaines31/marketingskills, sales-enablement] These are print sizes; screen type rules are in [anti-slop-ui SKILL.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-ui/SKILL.md).

Visual hierarchy: (1) headline, largest text, top of page, immediately communicates value; (2) section headers, bold and clear, scannable anchors; (3) body text, short sentences, bullets preferred over paragraphs; (4) proof elements, metrics and quotes stand out (larger font, color, or a callout box); (5) CTA, prominent, bottom of page or bottom-right.

Whitespace: margins at least 0.75" on all sides; enough space between sections to separate them visually. If it feels crowded, cut content. Never shrink font below 9pt.

| Element | Suggested size |
|---|---|
| Headline | 18-24pt |
| Section headers | 12-14pt bold |
| Body text | 10-11pt |
| Fine print / footer | 9pt minimum |

The source's footer table said 8-9pt, conflicting with its no-text-below-9pt rule and Prove it check. Keep the existing 9pt floor; these remain source print conventions. [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, sales-enablement]

Color: brand colors for headers and accents; body text dark (black or near-black) on white; 1-2 accent colors at most; use color to draw attention to metrics and CTAs.

File format: PDF for email attachments and leave-behinds; Google Slides or PowerPoint for editable versions reps can customize. Always include both: reps will customize, prospects want clean PDFs.

## Prove it

The page fits one side (two at most) and reads in 30 seconds; it names a person to contact, not info@; it carries exactly one proof point or the template's metric slots, each real; the leave-behind quotes what the buyer said and goes out within 24 hours; the champion version is in "we" and "our"; no text is below 9pt and margins are at least 0.75"; a PDF and an editable file are delivered together.
