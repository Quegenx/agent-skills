# Pages with function

## Contact form

- **The card:** at most 39.25rem, padding 3rem (1.5rem under 37.5rem), gap 2.25rem, `xlarge` radius, a subtle border, its description at most 32rem and centred, in a section at least `100svh` tall with `overflow: hidden` (the tile pattern behind it is in [MOTION.md](MOTION.md)).
- **Fields:** the control-subtle fill, padding `.625rem 1rem`, line height 1.5rem, a resting `inset 0 0 0 1px transparent` that becomes an inset 2px focus ring (`box-shadow`, 200ms); stacks .375rem apart with the label row inset .5rem; a 6px pill accent dot .3125rem after each required label; the textarea at least 8.75rem tall with `resize: none`. `autoComplete` per field, spellcheck off for email. Email inputs have no selection API, so render them as `type="text" inputMode="email"` when a feature needs the caret position. While sending and after success, fields are muted: the control-selected fill, .64 opacity, `pointer-events: none`, no focus ring.
- **Validation:** the form is `noValidate` and every field carries `required`, `aria-invalid` and its client minimum as `minLength` (name 2, message 10). Trim values before validating and before sending. Validate on submit; after the first submit, validate as the user types.
- **Errors under the field, absolutely positioned** (end-aligned, no layout shift), fading and rising 4px, keyed by their text, `role="alert"`, linked with `aria-describedby`.
- **A clear button** inside text inputs: a small round tinted `×` (a 24px visual in a 2rem hit area, 14px icon at stroke 1.8, its tint over 180ms; the field reserves 2.875rem at the end) that fades in from a 4px blur once there is text, clears and keeps focus in the field, and hides while the form is muted.
- **Cmd/Ctrl+Enter submits** from the message field; the submit button is at least 2.75rem tall with `.5rem 1.5rem` padding.
- **Idempotent sends:** the form carries its own id (a fresh UUID after each success) and ignores a send while sending, vanishing or already sent. The server decodes with a strict schema that rejects unknown keys, using the same limits as the database's checks (name 2 to 120, email at most 254 and matching `^[^@\s]+@[^@\s.]+(?:\.[^@\s.]+)+$`, message 10 to 5000, the language as a literal), then upserts ignoring duplicates through a server-only privileged client (visitors cannot write the table), so a double click or a retry lands on one row and still reports success. Typing again resets the success state.
- **Success** as in [MOTION.md](MOTION.md) (fields vanish, the button turns green, a success card unfolds: the recessed fill, `large` radius, padding 1.5rem, gap .75rem), with the status repeated in a visually hidden `aria-live` output. A failed send puts its error message in the same output.

## A caret that glides

For the site's showcase fields: hide the native caret (`caret-color: transparent`, restored on unmount) and draw a 2px accent bar, 1rem tall, that springs to the caret's position (stiffness 500, damping 30, mass .5). Measure the text before the caret in a hidden span that copies the field's font, font features, variation settings and letter spacing; for textareas use a mirror element that also copies `tab-size`, `word-break`, `text-align`, `box-sizing`, padding and the exact client size, with a marker span at the caret, and centre the bar in its line by `(lineHeight - 16) / 2` (line height falling back to 1.5 times the font size). Position from the right edge in right-to-left, and keep it inside the padding with 1px of tolerance. Jump instead of spring when the caret was hidden (first focus). Hide it, with no fade, while a range is selected (the browser draws the range), on blur (hide first, then let the field react), when disabled, and when it scrolls out of the box. The field's own handlers run before the caret moves. Re-place on `selectionchange`, on fonts loading (`document.fonts`), on resize, and a frame after wheel or touch scrolling; one pending animation frame at a time. Reduced motion: a spring so stiff it is instant (stiffness 10000, damping 100, mass .1).

## Help center

- **Header:** the animated title and a word-by-word description on one row aligned on `last baseline` (stacking under 48rem), with the theme's product blend mode. A search card (18.75rem tall, 15rem under 35rem, padding 3rem by 2rem, an accent title) with a big outlined button (2.5rem tall, a 2px accent border, a `0 2px 2px` shadow, inline padding 1rem and .5rem, background turning accent-soft on plain `:hover` over .14s, an 18px icon) that shows the shortcut tag (padding `.1875rem .625rem`, tracking -0.09px reset in right-to-left) whose tint deepens while the pointer is over the button (`pointerenter` and `pointerleave`). The tag reads `⌘K` on every platform, and either modifier works. The shortcut opens the search anywhere on the page: `metaKey || ctrlKey` with `key.toLocaleLowerCase() === "k"`, and `preventDefault`. The search mounts only while open.
- **Topic cards** in two columns, gap 1.5rem, each reveal wrapper `height: 100%` so cards stretch to the row: a backdrop blur of .25rem, gap .9375rem, padding 1.5rem (1.25rem on phones), the icon that tilts toward the pointer (in a 3.125rem slot), a title that doesn't wrap, a one-line description that ellipsizes on desktop and wraps on phones, and the arrow that fades in ([MOTION.md](MOTION.md)). Then common questions (a fixed set of four, shown whole with no "show more"; an answer with bullets renders as a paragraph plus a list with 1.5rem start padding and .5rem gaps), a card for asking a person, and the closing call. Guide sections pad 3rem by 2rem block and 3rem by 1.5rem inline.
- **Topic page:** a static title (at most 10em), a description at most 31rem, questions at most 50rem as accordions with `headingLevel={2}`, topped up with questions from other topics to at least six so a small topic never looks empty. The question named in the URL hash opens on arrival (read through `useSyncExternalStore` on `hashchange`); the clicked index (null at first) overrides the hash index, and a toggle seeds from the hash index and closes to -1, so an explicit close beats the hash.
- **Data:** topics (slug, title, description, meta description, icon, questions) and questions (id, title, answer, bullets, a link to the anchor on its topic page), all from translated messages. The route's loader validates the slug and returns only it; the page and the head re-resolve the translated topic from it, since the topic holds an icon component that can't be serialized. Check not-found in both.

## Help search (a command palette)

- **Mounting:** a native `<dialog open>` with `aria-modal`, portaled after hydration. The dialog layer is `pointer-events: none` with the panel `auto`, and the scrim is a separate `aria-hidden` sibling at z 9999998 under the dialog's 9999999. The panel sits 20% from the top, 96% wide up to 39.8125rem, `medium` radius, .625rem inline padding.
- **Opening and closing:** CSS keyframes over .2s ease for both: the scrim fades in and closes on press; the panel rises from `scale(.9)` and 1.25rem. Closing is a transform and opacity transition over .2s; the scrim takes `pointer-events: none` while it runs, and the panel unmounts on an opacity `transitionend` fired on the element itself (`target === currentTarget`), with a 260ms timer fallback. While open, body scrolling is locked (restore the previous `overflow`, don't clear it) and wheel and touch moves on the scrim are prevented; the input takes focus a frame after opening; the smooth-scroll library is told to leave the panel alone.
- **The input:** bottom border only, padding 1.25rem with 3rem at the end for the close button (the clear-button style with an 18px icon at stroke 1.8), `-webkit-text-fill-color` set for text and placeholder, tracking .1px reset in right-to-left, `autoComplete`, `autoCorrect` and `spellCheck` off, a visually hidden label and `aria-controls`. It is `outline-none`, and its secondary-colored caret is its focus cue.
- **Results** in two groups (questions, then guides), each heading with an id and its items in a `fieldset` with `aria-labelledby`. Trim and `toLocaleLowerCase` the query; an empty query lists everything. Questions match their title, answer and bullets; guides their title and description. The results are real links (Tab reaches them); in the input, arrows move a highlight that wraps around and resets to the first result on every keystroke, hover moves it too, Enter opens it with `location.assign`, and Escape (handled on the input) closes. The highlighted row carries `aria-current="true"` and an "Enter" hint (.34375rem radius, pushed to the end). Rows: the control-subtle highlight, `small` radius, `.375rem .75rem` padding, background over .15s, a single-line ellipsis, 32px icons (a book for a guide, a question mark for a question). Empty state: at least 15rem tall, `4rem 2rem` padding, faint centred text.
- **A preview pane** beside the list on desktop (list column 14.6875rem beside `1fr`; under 48rem the preview hides and the layout becomes a block; both at most 23rem tall with `overscroll-behavior: contain`): a start border, padding `1.625rem 1rem 1.625rem 1.625rem`, the result's kind and an outlined category tag whose border and text color come from a `data-category` attribute, its title as a link, its answer and bullets, with a 4rem fade at the bottom where it scrolls.

## Blog

- **Index:** the hero, the newest post as a featured card, then recent posts with a category select and numbered pagination (four a page). The category and the pages apply only to the posts after the featured one, which never filters out; changing the category resets to page one; paging scrolls back to the list's top. A hairline divider inset to the content box (`px-12` with `background-clip: content-box`, at most 67.625rem); the featured section's block padding drops to 3rem under 48rem.
- **Featured card:** the image (not lazy) at 52% beside the text at 48%, stacking under 61.25rem; "Featured" and category labels, a large title; padding 1.5rem (.75rem on phones).
- **Recent card:** a lazy image frame (12.25 by 23.375rem, full width under 61.25rem), title, a two-line clamp of the excerpt (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2`), a category chip; padding 1rem, gap 3rem; rows on desktop, stacked on phones; cards 4.5rem apart, the list 4.5rem below (3rem on phones). The empty state is centred with 4.5rem block padding.
- **Category select:** a split trigger (2.125rem tall, at least 8.125rem wide, a label half and a 2rem arrow half, each with .75rem logical outer corners and a 150ms background driven by a CSS variable, an `aria-label`), and a controlled popover (at least the trigger's width, at most 15rem tall and then scrolling) that scales in from `scale(.96)` at its trigger's origin in 100ms and leaves with opacity only in 100ms (no animation class with reduced motion). Options `.5rem .75rem`, 120ms transitions; the selected one takes the selected fill and medium weight. The chevron is a 10 by 6 stroke at .26 stroke opacity.
- **Pagination:** nothing at one page or fewer. A labelled `<nav>`, an elevated pill panel (padding .5rem, gap .5rem, page gap .25rem, 3rem above) with 2.75rem round previous and next buttons (← and → glyphs flipped with `scaleX(-1)` in right-to-left, .35 opacity when disabled) and page numbers, each with a "page N" `aria-label`, the current one accent with on-brand text and `aria-current="page"`. Background, color and opacity over 150ms; hover fills only under `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Post:** a back link, the title (at most 48rem, leading 1.16, semibold, balanced) with its computed accent part, then a details row (`justify-between`, gaps 7.5rem by .75rem, wrapping): a byline of a 24px round logo avatar (16px image, `alt=""`), an `aria-hidden` "•" and `<time dateTime>` with the raw ISO value, formatted by `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in the page's language with `dateStyle: "long"`, plus the category. Header bottom padding 4.5rem (3rem on phones). Then a `16.125rem 1fr` grid (gap 3rem, one column under 48rem, inner width 61.625rem) of an on-this-page rail (active on the first heading from the start) and the article: the hero image (high fetch priority, opens in the image preview, 3rem below), a divider (`0 0 3rem`), and blocks. The post section pads `0` at the top and the wide section token at the bottom (4.5rem on phones).
- **Article model:** blocks (paragraph, heading with id and level, list, quote, image with size and caption, table with a label) of rich inline pieces (text, strong, emphasis, code, internal or external link, line break). Key every piece by its content (type, copy and address joined), never its index. Internal links go through the localized address helper; external ones open with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Render each block with its own reveal; only `h2` headings get the word rise with a computed muted part, `h3` and `h4` are plain; headings report when they are in view for the rail; body images are lazy and open in the preview.
- **Prose values:** quote with a 2px start border, 1.5rem start padding, italic, tertiary; inline code at .375rem radius, `.1875rem .375rem`, leading 1.2, nudged up .03125rem, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; list items 1rem from the start, `.25rem` block padding, leading 1.5; captions centred, leading 1.45, .625rem above; headings leading 1.35, `h2` at 450, `h3` and `h4` medium.
- **Tables** scroll sideways inside a 1px box-shadow ring, the wrapper padded `1px 1px 1.5rem` so `overflow-x` doesn't clip the ring; the table has an `aria-label`, header cells `scope="col"`, cells `width: 1px` with `white-space: break-spaces`, `.5rem 1rem` padding, leading 1.25rem, column dividers as an inset -1px box-shadow (none on the last cell).
- **Data:** published articles per language, newest first, decoded by a schema at the boundary. Fetch the images for a whole list in one batched query (by translation group, ordered by position), never one query per article. Encode the slug; the single-article query takes one row or null. An article without its cover image is left out; the cover is a fixed size (here 1344 by 768) with its alt falling back to the title, and every alt text is optional. A section becomes blocks in order: an `h2`, its paragraphs, the image whose position matches the section's, then its bullets as a list. Errors carry distinctive literal prefixes (`BLOG_NOT_CONFIGURED` when the content source's settings are missing, `BLOG_REQUEST_FAILED: <status>` on a failed response). A missing slug is a real not-found thrown in the loader, so the page may assume its data exists and the head returns nothing without it.

## Legal documents

One component for every document: title (animated, at most 10em, bold), the updated date as the description (one shared "updated" message for every document; its word reveal starts at .5s), an intro (an array of paragraphs), sections (id, title, paragraphs, optional points) beside an on-this-page rail labelled with the document's title, its active section tracked by index. Section ids are stable English slugs in both languages, so anchors survive a language switch.

- **Layout:** a flex row (gap 3rem, at most 67.625rem, `align-items: start`); the article at about 42rem with a 1.65 line height, `overflow-wrap: break-word` and `pretty`; the section padded 4.5rem above and 6.25rem below (3 and 4rem on phones), 3rem inline; a top rule as an `aria-hidden` 1px span `calc(100% - 6rem)` wide (at most 67.625rem), centred with `left: 50%` and `translateX(-50%)`; points with 1.5rem start padding, .5rem gaps, 2rem below; section titles medium at leading 1.35, balanced.
- **Email addresses** in the text become `mailto:` links, matched with a bounded pattern (`[\w.+][\w.+-]{0,63}@[\w.-]{1,253}\.[A-Za-z]{2,63}`) so matching stays linear. The pattern is one global (`gu`) regex with a named capturing group: split the text on it, so the addresses stay in the list as their own pieces, and test each piece with the same regex to choose a link or plain text.
- **Reveals** are off by default; each document opts in, and the reveal rule (the hydration rule in [MOTION.md](MOTION.md)) is passed to the separator, the rail, the intro and every section.

## On-this-page rail

A sticky list at the anchor offset plus 1.5rem (hidden on phones), at most 18.75rem wide, with a start border, 1rem gaps and links padded .75rem at the start; the active item at full opacity and the rest at 50%, the indicator in [MOTION.md](MOTION.md), `aria-current="location"`. The active section is the one whose top crosses into the upper half of the viewport (`margin: -100px 0 -50% 0`, 30% visible). Headings get `scroll-margin-block-start` equal to the anchor offset.
