# Framed panel illustrations

Use this optional SVG direction for a friendly person-at-work scene framed by a violet inset on a pastel card. White UI fragments and foliage overlap that inset. Draw an original scene, then use [shared craft](ILLUSTRATION-CRAFT.md) for hands, contact, rendering and revisions. All dimensions below are drawing units at 480 pixels wide. ## Line hierarchy and surface details

[MengTo, illustration-framed-panel, SKILL.md Line and fill]

| Mark | Width at 480 |
|---|---|
| Panel perimeter | 1.35 |
| Body, clothing, furniture and bubbles | 1.25 |
| Hands, neck, footwear and bushes | 1.05 to 1.15 |
| Cloud and floor line | 1.2 |
| Clothing folds | .9 to 1.1 |
| Hatch pair and inner ear | .85 to .9 |
| Finger divisions | .75 to .8 |
| Outer brackets | 1.25, in the chosen pastel's mark color |

Use flat fills under navy contours with round joins and caps. Draw 2 to 4 hatch pairs per garment: each slash is 3 to 4 units long, its partner offset 1 right and 5 down, leaning up-right at width .9. Dark hair/trousers get lighter folds at 1 to 1.2. Shade with one unoutlined darker inset rather than a gradient.

Raised chips, toggles and buttons receive a darker duplicate 3 units below: red `#B8434A`, green `#3E8E5E`, white `#C9C5DE`. Add a short width-1 curved tick 5 to 7 units inside a white bubble's top corner. Use no more than three words, system sans at 10 to 16, weight 700 to 800, navy or white; illustrative placeholders are not product claims.

## Character record

[MengTo, illustration-framed-panel, SKILL.md Characters]

Use one seated, kneeling or crouching figure, 195 to 215 units from hair to floor. The 266-unit panel does not accommodate the source's roughly 5.5-head standing adult. Start from a new pose and keep these shared facial measurements rather than copying a shipped scene.

| Part | Construction |
|---|---|
| Head | Three-quarter view, width 36 and brow-to-chin height 46. Source face chin is near `(232,166.5)`. Near eye ellipse 1.8 by 2.1, far eye 1.5 by 1.9; upper lid/lash, width-1.2 arched brows, one far-side hooked nose, filled smile, tongue accent, ear curve and gold earring. Near blush ellipse 7.6 by 4.2 and a smaller far cheek. |
| Placement | Translate and rotate face, neck and hair together about `(232,166)`; lean 4 to 8 degrees into the action. Mirror for the other facing. Avoid scaling outlined head geometry without preserving the stroke measurements. |
| Hair | Draw back mass before face, fringe after. Add 3 to 5 highlight lines at 1.1 to 1.2 and 1 to 2 navy interior lines at .9 to 1. Bob, bun and curls are possible silhouettes. |
| Torso | Soft trapezoid 60 to 70 wide, overlapping separate sleeves. Neckline/hem second contour 3 to 4 inside; rib ticks every 6 to 7. Cardigan buttons radius 1.5; hoodie ties width 1.6 with small rectangular ends. |
| Hands | Length 22 to 26. Cuff band 5 to 6 wide with .8 center seam, skin forearm, narrow wrist, palm, knuckle contour and .75 to 1 finger curves. Thumb may be a separate shape; determine its side before placement. |
| Legs | Taper through the knee; darker far leg behind lighter near leg, one hem line and 2 to 3 units of sock/ankle. White sneaker with 3-unit red sole or a toes-tucked kneeling shoe. |

For typing, show the back of a flattened hand; for pointing, curl the other fingers under the thumb; for a handle grip, let the back-visible right fist close over the handle with its thumb toward the working end. A knee-resting far hand can be palm-down. The shared handedness table decides when a local-y mirror is required.

Let a seated body lean or twist, compress the cushion and overlap crossed legs clearly: far foot under the near knee, near shin in front, visible sock and sole. The source beanbag lean is about -5 degrees at the hips. Keep soles at roughly y 295 to 304 or on a reachable footrest, with contact ellipses.

## Panel, scenery and composition

[MengTo, illustration-framed-panel, SKILL.md Decor and props; Composition]

The background fills 480 by 360. Put the violet panel at x 110, y 46, width 262, height 266, with a 1.35 navy border: about 55% of card width and 74% of card height. Keep the overall art inside x 40 to 450, y 30 to 325.

1. Draw background, outer brackets/marks, panel fill, sky doodles, wall object, floor and wholly inset props.
2. Draw the panel outline, then overlapping bushes, furniture, figure, white breakouts and final accent ticks. Overlapping items must cover the frame, not disappear under it.

| Element | Placement and count |
|---|---|
| Sky | Two outline clouds, about 50 and 44 wide, each with a 3-unit dash at its right; optional 17-wide cloud and 2 to 4 small 9-wide arc ticks. Keep the head clear. At most one wall prop. |
| Floor | Lavender y 256 to 312, navy top line at 256; 6 to 7 dashes, lengths 10 to 26. Some add a 4-unit companion after a 5-unit gap. |
| Bushes | One or two on the floor at lower corners; at least one crosses a side. |
| White breakouts | Exactly two. Upper left roughly x 62 to 166, y 58 to 134; right roughly x 336 to 436, y 150 to 250. Extend 40 to 65 units beyond the frame; source measured 44 to 64. A 10 to 20-unit overlap is too slight for this recipe. |
| Bubble contents | Icon, small avatar or large glyph with 2 to 4 short placeholder lines; each gets its corner tick. Optional small red tag overlaps the upper-left corner. |
| Outer marks | Top-right and bottom-left L brackets; 2 to 3 parenthesis-like marks, double on one side and single opposite. At most 2 navy three-ray sparkle ticks by a breakout. |
| Main mass | Figure centerline x 205 to 235, left of panel center 241, head in upper half with 30 to 60 sky above it. Balance upper-left/right-middle breakouts against lower bushes. |
| Furniture | One lower mass 120 to 200 wide: seat, desk, board or seat/table group. Keep unused floor props out. |

Avoid a second person, confetti/stars, extra margin decoration, or a colored frame replacing navy. This specific composition is an optional preset, not a mandatory structure for all illustration.

## Reusable geometry and failure checks

[MengTo, illustration-framed-panel, SKILL.md Techniques and Failure modes; scripts/panel-kit.mjs]

The source helper is not installed here. Its reusable parts include scaffold, clouds, bushes, floor ellipses, speech bubbles, raised paper, placeholder lines, face/neck, shoes, several hand poses, cuffs and hatch marks. A source bubble has radius 8 and an 11-unit tail. Keep the torso and scene newly authored.

Draw an object before the fingers that wrap it. Rotate the fist with its forearm; the source permits a handle angle up to 45 degrees off the thumb axis. For a magnifier, redraw the actual underlying scene inside a lens clip at 1.6 scale about its center, then add .18 white lens fill, two glare strokes and a light `#C9C7DA` rim. A dark opaque disc loses the magnifier cue.

When moving path coordinates, preserve the listed stroke widths. The source scaler supports move/line/horizontal/vertical/cubic/smooth-cubic/quadratic/smooth-quadratic/close, including relative forms, and rejects arcs. This is its helper's limit, not an SVG limit.

Check a stool ring against leg reach; if it cannot carry the foot, plant the shoe behind the near shoe with its sole about 3 units higher. Draw a ring with two cubic ellipse halves and layer back/post/front separately. At tiny sizes, quote hooks can resemble digits; filled teardrop quotes need a 1x check. Preserve varied line weights, hatches and corner ticks so the result does not become a uniform UI kit.

## Prove it

[MengTo, illustration-framed-panel, SKILL.md Workflow and Verify]

- Record action, pose, wall prop, two breakouts, pastel choice and each hand's orientation. Block furniture/body/legs/head first and verify the 1x silhouette before adding detail.
- Compare the panel dimensions, floor, bracket colors, two frame-crossing white pieces and bush overlap against the recipe. Check facial sizes, light/dark separation, cuffs, thumb side, weight and reachable foot contact.
- Use the shared 2x whole-card/contact-sheet and 5 to 8x vector detail inspection. Look for missing thumbs, floating feet, weak cushion compression, oversimplified crossed legs, handle-over-finger mistakes and unreadable tiny glyphs.
- Verify allowed palette/opacity, line widths, at most three words, per-card IDs and deliberate tint exceptions. Report the actual renders and corrections; the source's example scores are not acceptance evidence for a new card.
