# Product illustrations that tell one story

Authored miniature interfaces for a feature card, docs figure or marketing hero, from the supplied Stageshow SKILL.md and references/craft.md, motion.md, choreography.md, rehearse.md and verify.md. These are illustrations, not browser automation or evidence that the depicted product performed an action. Real product claims and assets still need their real sources. [stageshow, SKILL.md; references/products.md; repository source and sample-data rules]

## Choose the picture before the motion

[stageshow, SKILL.md, Workflow; references/craft.md, Restraint and Density]

- Name the medium, destination, shown width, focal object, action and payoff. Compose at the host's real size; a narrow version is a second composition, not a squeezed desktop screen.
- Pick the story: how an interaction feels, a short flow through one app, or a system consequence that lands elsewhere. Three to five beats and a payoff are the source's recipe, not a required length for every product interaction.
- Simplify words without stripping the domain's recognizable structure. Put readable words and values where the eye lands; use placeholder lines for peripheral filler. A word budget is not an element quota, and empty panes are not a substitute for hierarchy.
- Prove the opening and payoff as stills before choreography. Choose a poster that explains the result alone, after rolling text, morphing labels and fading surfaces have settled.
- Use the project's own components, tokens and assets when supplied. Source illustration colors, crop preferences and timing do not replace the protected app, landing-page or agent-chat designs. Use fictional sample data, without copying the source's real-brand studies. [stageshow, SKILL.md, Workflow; references/products.md; repository owner-design and sample-data rules]

## Preserve the relationship through a change

[stageshow, references/choreography.md; references/motion.md, Changing text and values and Things that appear, travel and leave]

- Keep stable chrome, outer surfaces and action positions while dependent content changes inside. Retain old and new content in tracks rather than replacing the screen. Let related responses overlap in a readable order; the result still follows its cause.
- Keep a changing button mounted. Change its icon and label in the same slot, and move neighboring content with the measured width. Decide whether the control reserves its largest footprint or resizes before animating it.
- Mask the moving content slot locally; settled text belongs wholly inside the opaque region. Fade content while its space opens or closes so the moving edge never cuts readable glyphs. Do not blur the whole interface to hide bad geometry.
- A new row opens space continuously for its neighbors. A flying object takes one trip into its final measured slot; its handoff agrees in size, position and appearance. Check the actual landing element rather than its padded wrapper.
- Keep direct feedback prompt. Presentation travel can be slower, but delaying the product's response for storytelling makes the control feel unresponsive. A useful result hold needs no new pulse or repeated count.
- Reset with a visible cause, around retained context, or while hidden. Do not rewind data in view or run an entire navigation backward solely to make a loop. Inspect a full loop and the next start.

## Rehearse in the host

[stageshow, references/rehearse.md; references/verify.md]

- Inspect opening, payoff, busiest intermediate frame and every changed resting state at each shipping width and look. The demo's crop, ground and overlays must work inside the real destination page.
- Check glyph pixels as well as rectangles during the last moving frame and first settled frame. Correct geometry does not establish that text stayed visually still.
- Review pause, resume, replay, interruption, reduced motion and return from offscreen. A paused new scene must finish its measurements without advancing the story. A clean geometry scan is not visual approval; an empty, short or gapped recording is inconclusive.
- Keep the [gesture evidence](GESTURES.md#authored-cursors-and-drags) and [live pacing checks](PERFORMANCE.md#measuring) with their canonical owners. A deterministic export does not establish smooth live playback.

## Prove it

[stageshow, references/verify.md, Hand over; references/rehearse.md, The last pass]

Record the reference used, what changed, beats and payoff, captured widths, looks and states, observed faults and fixes, assumptions, unresolved issues and checks not performed. The poster tells the story alone, every action has a cause, text survives intermediate frames, and the loop's return is explained. Report actual captures and observations rather than claiming the source toolkit's gate was run when it was not.
