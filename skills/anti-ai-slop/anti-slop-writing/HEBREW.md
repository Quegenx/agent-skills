# Hebrew

AI Hebrew usually reads as English that was translated: English sentence shapes, English idioms rendered word for word, a formal register where Israelis expect a direct one, and the stock phrases of Israeli business websites. Everything in [TELLS.md](TELLS.md) applies too; this file adds what is specific to Hebrew. Rows tagged [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer] come from skills-il's `hebrew-content-writer` skill (MIT).

## 1. Hebrew tells

| Tell | Examples | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Era and context openers | בעולם של היום, בעידן הדיגיטלי, בעולם המשתנה במהירות, בתקופה שבה | Start with the subject or the reader's problem |
| Filler transitions | חשוב לציין ש, ראוי לציין, יש לזכור ש, כדאי לדעת ש, אין ספק ש, יחד עם זאת (as a reflex), בנוסף לכך, נכון להיום | Delete; the sentence stands without it |
| Contrast reveal | זה לא רק X, זה Y; לא X, אלא Y; זה לא עוד X | Say Y with its reason |
| Self-answered question, colon reveal | אז מה זה אומר בעצם? התשובה פשוטה: ...; הסוד: ...; השורה התחתונה: ... | A plain sentence |
| Calqued English idioms | בסוף היום, זה עושה שכל / סנס, לקחת את X לשלב הבא, לחשוב מחוץ לקופסה, משנה משחק, לעשות את ההבדל, לשחרר את הפוטנציאל | בסופו של דבר (or cut), זה הגיוני, the concrete change, the actual idea, what changes, by how much |
| Translated slop verbs | למנף, להעצים, לייעל תהליכים, לרתום, לאפשר לכם ל..., לגלות (as a CTA) | The real action: לחסוך שעתיים בשבוע, לשלוח חשבונית מהנייד |
| Empty adjectives | מהפכני, חדשני, פורץ דרך, ייחודי, איכותי, מתקדם, חכם, מקיף, הוליסטי, חלק / חוויה חלקה, מקצה לקצה, ברמה הגבוהה ביותר | The fact the adjective stands in for |
| The Israeli business-site trio | שירות אישי, מקצועיות ואמינות; צוות מקצועי ומנוסה; ניסיון של שנים רבות; הלקוחות שלנו בראש סדר העדיפויות; תחת קורת גג אחת; כל הפתרונות במקום אחד | What you actually do, since when, for whom, with one real example |
| Mission-statement filler | אנו מאמינים ש, החזון שלנו הוא, אנחנו כאן כדי, המטרה שלנו היא לספק | Say what you do and for whom |
| "Journey" and "world" | המסע של הלקוח, עולם התוכן, עולם הפיננסים, בלב העשייה | Name the steps, the field, the work |
| Upbeat closers | העתיד כבר כאן, ואנחנו רק בהתחלה, נשמח ללוות אתכם בדרך | End on the last fact or the next step |

## 2. Register: pick one and hold it

| Text | Register | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Legal, government, formal letters | Formal | Longer sentences and passive are expected |
| Business sites, B2B, reports | Clear business Hebrew | Plain, professional, not bureaucratic |
| Marketing, landing pages, ads | Direct, conversational | Short sentences, the reader as subject |
| Product UI, errors, buttons | Minimal | Two to five words, no politeness padding |
| Social posts, chat, community | Spoken | Slang and English words are fine if they're the brand's |

The most common failure is formal Hebrew in a casual context: הנכם מוזמנים, אנו שמחים להודיע, לרשותכם, נשמח לעמוד לשירותכם, בהתאם לצורך. Israeli readers expect directness (dugri). Direct is not rude: it keeps תודה and courtesy and drops the padding.

| Padded | Direct |
|---|---|
| נשמח אם תוכלו לשקול לעדכן את הפרטים | תעדכנו את הפרטים / יש לעדכן את הפרטים |
| ייתכן שכדאי לבדוק את החיבור לאינטרנט | בדקו את החיבור לאינטרנט |
| אנו מתנצלים על אי הנוחות שנגרמה | סליחה על העיכוב. זה תוקן |

## 3. Bureaucratic forms and common errors

| Avoid | Write | Why |
|---|---|---|
| במידה ו... | אם | "במידה ו" is a widespread error for a condition |
| על מנת, בכדי | כדי | Plain "כדי" unless the register is legal |
| לאור העובדה ש, מאחר ו | כי, מפני ש | Shorter, natural |
| ביצוע תשלום, ביצוע רכישה, מתן שירות | לשלם, לקנות, לתת שירות | Verbs over nominalizations |
| באופן משמעותי, באופן מיידי, באופן אישי (stacked) | הרבה / מיד / אישית, or the number | "באופן + adjective" on every line is a tell |
| ניתן ל... on every line | אפשר ל... | "ניתן" reads bureaucratic outside formal text |
| הבית הספר, הצוות המכירות | בית הספר, צוות המכירות | In smichut only the last noun takes ה |
| ראיתי הדוח | ראיתי את הדוח | "את" before a definite direct object |
| להתחבר את החשבון | לחבר את החשבון / להתחבר לחשבון | Wrong preposition after a reflexive verb [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer] |
| שלוש ימים, שלושה שנים | שלושה ימים, שלוש שנים | Numbers agree with the counted noun: שלושה is masculine, שלוש feminine. The source and old line mislabeled this as opposite gender. [Academy number decisions](https://hebrew-academy.org.il/topic/hahlatot/grammardecisions/terminology-ordinance/4-3-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A9-%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8/) |
| Mixed spelling (תכנית and תוכנית) | One spelling, full (ktiv maleh): תוכנית, שירות, תוכנה, קודם, פירוט | Consistency |

## 4. Gender

Pick one approach per text and keep it. If the brief doesn't name one, "Ask the user which approach they prefer if not specified" [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer]:
- **Plural address** (אתם, הירשמו, שלחו): natural in marketing and widely read as addressing everyone.
- **Infinitive or impersonal** (יש למלא את הטופס, אפשר לבחור): neutral, good for instructions and forms.
- **Verbal nouns for UI controls** (שמירה, שליחה, ביטול, הרשמה, המשך): the standard way to keep buttons gender-free.
- **Collective nouns** (קהילת המשתמשים מרוצה, not המשתמשים מרוצים): the noun's own gender carries the agreement [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer].
- **Slash forms** (משתמשים/ות, את/ה): only in forms or legal text where nothing else works. A paragraph full of slashes is unreadable. The other view: hebrew-content-writer offers slash notation for business and marketing text and, in its English version, "ניתן לבחור" as the impersonal form [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer]; this file keeps slashes out of marketing and prefers אפשר (as the skill's own Hebrew version, SKILL_HE.md, writes it: "אפשר לבחור").
- Never masculine singular imperative (לחץ, הירשם) by default in a product meant for everyone, and never switch approaches mid-text.

## 5. Punctuation and typography

- **Dashes:** use commas, periods or spaced hyphens in this repository. Em and en dashes are excluded by the owner, including long text. [Owner writing rule] The Hebrew maqaf (־, often typed as -) joins words with no spaces: ה-API, תל־אביב.
- **Gershayim and geresh** are not quotation marks: צה"ל, מנכ"ל, דו"ח for acronyms; ג'ירפה, צ'יפס, ד"ר, עמ' for sounds and abbreviations. Don't replace them with English quotes or strip them.
- **Exclamation marks:** Hebrew marketing overuses them. At most one, at a real peak.
- **Numbers and dates:** digits in body copy (3 ימים, תוך 24 שעות), DD/MM/YYYY, 24-hour time, ₪ placed consistently across the site. `Intl` does not produce those forms by default: in Node 26, `Intl.DateTimeFormat('he-IL')` gives `18.10.2026` (dots, so slashes need an explicit format), and `Intl.NumberFormat('he-IL', {style:'currency', currency:'ILS'})` gives `‏1,234.00 ‏₪`, with right-to-left marks and the ₪ after the number (measured in Node 26 during this repo's review; skills-il's test vector `₪1,234` does not match it).
- **Plurals in ICU messages:** Hebrew's plural categories are `one`, `two` and `other` (`new Intl.PluralRules("he").resolvedOptions().pluralCategories`; `select(2)` is `two`, `select(0)` is `other`), so a Hebrew message can carry a `two` branch beside `one` and `other`, and a "no items" string needs `=0`. better-writing's note that "a translator adds `few` or `many`" doesn't cover Hebrew's `two` ([anti-slop-copy MICROCOPY.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/anti-ai-slop/anti-slop-copy/MICROCOPY.md#build-strings-whole-for-every-language)).
- **Nikud:** only for children's text, poetry, or one ambiguous word or foreign name. Pointing body copy makes it read like a textbook.

## 6. English inside Hebrew

- **Keep in English** brand and product names and established tech terms (API, SaaS, deploy).
- **Transliterate** words that entered spoken Hebrew that way: אימייל, סטארטאפ, באג, פיצ'ר.
- **Translate** where a Hebrew word is what people say: משתמש, הורדה, עדכון, תוכנה. Forcing English there reads lazy.
- English nouns take Hebrew grammar naturally: באגים, לינקים, הדשבורד נטען (pick a gender for the noun and keep it; "usually masculine by default", so "הדשבורד נטען" not "הדשבורד נטענה" [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer]).
- On the web, wrap English runs that contain punctuation, codes, emails or URLs in `<bdi>` or `dir="ltr"` so the surrounding Hebrew punctuation doesn't jump. Check every mixed line in the browser. "Keep a space on both sides of the English run" [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer]. W3C: for a known direction, "tightly wrap every opposite-direction phrase in markup" with `dir`; "Without a `dir` attribute, the `bdi` element behaves as if `dir="auto"` had been applied"; `bdo` is not for normal bidi text, "only for special cases, mostly educational" ([Inline markup and bidirectional text in HTML](https://www.w3.org/International/articles/inline-bidi-markup/)). An English phrase inside Hebrew also needs `lang="en"` for screen-reader pronunciation (WCAG 3.1.2, [Language of Parts](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/language-of-parts.html)).

## 7. Search (Hebrew SEO)

Hebrew attaches prefixes (ה, ב, ל, ו, ש, מ) and inflects heavily, so one keyword appears in many forms: ביטוח, הביטוח, לביטוח, ביטוחים, מבוטח. Write naturally around the root instead of repeating one exact form. Israelis search tech topics in English too; cover both where it matters. A title with the topic early, one H1, a description that says what the page gives, and descriptive Hebrew alt text and link text. Don't stuff keywords: it's the same slop with a ranking excuse.

- **Length is not Google's rule.** This line used to say "Title around 55 to 60 characters". Google: "there's no limit on how long a `<title>` element can be"; "the title link is truncated in Google Search results as needed, typically to fit the device width" ([title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link)). The 55 to 60 range, and skills-il's "50-60 Hebrew characters" and "120-150 characters" for descriptions, are display conventions, not limits.
- **URLs.** Google allows Hebrew slugs: "Use words in your audience's language in the URL (and, if applicable, transliterated words)"; "characters in the non-ASCII range should be percent encoded"; hyphens, not underscores ([URL structure](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/url-structure)). skills-il suggests a transliterated ("bituach-briut") or English slug [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer]; both are within Google's line.
- **Keyword research:** "Research keywords in Hebrew using Google Keyword Planner (region: Israel)" [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer]; the research workflow is [keyword-research](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/keyword-research/SKILL.md).
- **Alt text** is written in the page's language, and localized routes are checked separately [freescan].
- **Other markets and AI answers:** build prompts and pages "from native demand, not translation", and audit local facts (price, currency, availability, legal, hours) per market [coreyhaines31/marketingskills, ai-seo] ([ai-search-visibility CITATION-SURFACES.md](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/ai-search-visibility/CITATION-SURFACES.md#other-languages-and-markets)). For proof in ads and creator content, "Translation alone is weaker than culturally native proof" [lightreel].

## Check against

When a spelling, loanword or grammar call is unclear, the references hebrew-content-writer names [skills-il, hebrew-content-writer]: the [Academy of the Hebrew Language](https://hebrew-academy.org.il) ("Official spelling rules, loanword decisions, new terms") and its [decisions](https://hebrew-academy.org.il/topic/hahlatot/); [Milog](https://milog.co.il) ("Modern Hebrew usage, register, common phrasing"); [Ravmilim](https://www.ravmilim.co.il) ("Synonyms, collocations, grammatical info"); and [he.wikipedia's style page](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/ויקיפדיה:לשון). That skill also has two grammar rules this file does not take: number polarity for "1-10" (the grammatical gender correction is in section 3) and "Adjectives agree with the LAST noun in the chain", which this repo's review flagged as wrong in general and left for a Hebrew editor to confirm.
