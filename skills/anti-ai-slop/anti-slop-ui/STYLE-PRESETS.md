# Optional visual-system presets

Choose a direction here only when it fits the brief and existing project. A preset coordinates layout, type, material, imagery and motion across a page; adding its accent color alone does not create that system. Keep [the existing alternatives](STYLE-ALTERNATIVES.md), truthful content, accessible controls and owner designs authoritative. ## Selection and boundaries

[MengTo, build-awwwards-quality-sites SKILL.md; style SKILL.md Scope/Tuning/Avoid sections]

Name the selected family, why it serves the content, and which project decisions it must preserve. Tune intensity, spacing, density, contrast and motion together. Use one material logic within a component rather than accumulating unrelated treatments. [Editorial page presets](EDITORIAL-PAGE-PRESETS.md) supply narrative/state models; the [SVG owner](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/web-ui/svg/SKILL.md) supplies six static drawing directions.

A complete reduced-motion/no-animation composition must still explain the page. Do not install GSAP, Lenis, Locomotive, Three.js or an icon package merely because a source preset names it. Preserve installed tools and native behavior where they satisfy the task; the source's mandatory GSAP/smooth-scroll stack is a separate house-style preference. Use the existing [animation lifecycle](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/web-ui/ui-animation/PERFORMANCE.md) and its canonical WebGL loop owner rather than copying raw demo loops.

## Light, paper and editorial systems

### Minimal agency grid

[MengTo, agency-grid-layout-minimal, SKILL.md]

Let tightly tracked oversized headings and deliberate line breaks carry a multi-column grid. Put compact uppercase labels, dates/section markers and small explanations in neighboring columns. Light neutrals, thin separators and quiet frames leave large open spans; architectural panoramas or facade-like media replace repetitive card galleries. Align asymmetric blocks to the same grid and vary type scale, image span, rail density and accent amount together. Reveals and hover crops support the typography instead of becoming the focal event.

### Cloudy blue

[MengTo, blue-cloudy-clean-modern, SKILL.md]

Use a saturated-to-pale blue atmospheric field with slow soft cloud light, white typography and translucent white details. Thin rails, tiny corner markers, a quiet nav and a few pills frame spacious hero copy. Keep supporting buttons dark-neutral or use a very small complementary accent. Adjust cloud softness, blue depth, white contrast and motion pace together; avoid turning the airy composition into a dense dashboard or adding heavy shadows. The raw demo's moving cards are reference artwork, not evidence of production lifecycle behavior.

### Serif book and index

[MengTo, book-serif-index, SKILL.md]

Put a warm folio/archive reading surface inside a darker shell. Pair serif body text and serif/gothic display moments with mono chapter/index metadata. Paper grain, creases, edge shade and light wear belong to the reading surface; sidebar chapters, catalog lists and restrained navigation belong to its frame. Tune page warmth, wear, spread proportions, type scale and index density; derive a small accent from the brand, or consider muted oxblood, bronze or antique gold. The associated navy/emerald demo differs from this authored paper direction; do not let that mismatch erase the book recipe.

### Minimal beige

[MengTo, clean-minimal-beige-light-mode, SKILL.md]

Layer beige, stone, cream and off-white inside a centered master frame. A calm centered hero leads to evenly divided process/information modules. Define hierarchy through spacing, hairline separators and small surface-value changes; use a sparse badge/progress/action accent instead of strong shadows or loud gradients. Adjust warmth, content density, container width, corner softness and accent strength together. This visual system does not prescribe a fixed number of process steps or invented mentorship statistics.

### Orange paper SaaS

[MengTo, orange-clean-paper-saas, SKILL.md]

Combine parchment/cream/stone surfaces with orange for steps, selected states, focus and primary action. A large rounded master container can pair an actual form/onboarding flow with an illustrative product stage. Use fine light-input borders, subtle gradient edges, soft orange-tinted depth and quiet sans typography; white/cream floating chips or rendered objects remain subordinate. Tune warmth, orange energy, radius and visual richness together. Keep tactile softness contemporary rather than adding distressed antique paper or ornamental objects that compete with the form.

### Light technical paper

[MengTo, light-mode-paper-technical, SKILL.md]

Frame warm light paper inside a darker exterior. Fine rules, L brackets, corners, diagonal texture and measured guides give the light interior technical structure. Rounded outer shells may coexist with crisp internal grids and one restrained status/action accent. Balance shell contrast, paper warmth, line density, texture and radius; avoid both a cold generic white dashboard and exaggerated antique distress. Large surfaces and content hierarchy must remain legible without the texture.

### Image-led grid

[MengTo, image-first-grid-layout, SKILL.md]

Let a licensed image or meaningful media field occupy most of the visual stage. Use directional tonal washes/vignettes for real text contrast, and align rails, center guides and corners over it. Place copy low or along a deliberate edge, leaving the image's subject unobstructed. Derive restrained neutral/white/smoke colors and a small accent from the image or brand. Tune crop, focal placement, overlay strength, rail weight and copy footprint responsively rather than assuming a fixed viewport-height hero.

### Editorial technical

[MengTo, editorial-tech, SKILL.md]

Combine asymmetrical columns, large expressive headings, small mono metadata and panoramic/inset media bands. Thin rules, grid traces and technical markers create rhythm without turning every section into a card. A controlled dark or light neutral base takes one brand accent. Adjust asymmetry, media span, type contrast and technical detail together; preserve narrative reading order and real scroll-container geometry when applying motion. The source demo's nested scrolling needs its actual scroller configured before ScrollTrigger measurements are meaningful.

### Nested agency shell

[MengTo, nested-container-clean-agency; nested-container-frames, SKILL.md]

Use a visible outer content boundary, then an inset contrasting section, then smaller modules only where they express hierarchy. The agency variant often nests a dark rounded feature region in a light editorial page, with clean headlines and a soft small accent. Distinguish outer frame, inner content frame and local media frame through decreasing line emphasis and deliberate insets. Tune shell contrast, depth, corner radius and frame density; stop nesting when content begins to feel boxed into cards inside cards. Frames are useful boundaries, not a substitute for grouping information.

### Technical split

[MengTo, split-layout-technical, SKILL.md]

Give two desktop panels different responsibilities: immersive media/visualization versus explanation/specs/navigation. Start near 50/50, but shift the ratio for content. Frame each zone with inset rails, small corners and measured metadata rows; pair quiet editorial headings with mono utility text. One embedded premium surface can create depth while the other panel stays informational. Stack in a deliberate reading order on narrow screens without dropping either zone. Tune ratio, frame density, type mood and depth; avoid equal clutter or a divider that has no functional meaning.

## Dark technical systems

### Blue laser glass

[MengTo, blue-laser-clean-glass-layout, SKILL.md]

Use near-black blue undertones behind a precise indigo/blue laser, with near-white narrow core, soft halo and light haze. Dark frosted cards, pills and gradient-edge wrappers sit above it; white display text, blue-gray support text and compact labels remain sharp. Keep the beam a spatial anchor rather than a thick neon spectacle. Tune beam direction/core/halo, haze, glass opacity and content contrast; the effect must not obscure state or create constant motion beside reading.

### Contrasting navy and cobalt

[MengTo, dark-blue-contrasting-clean, SKILL.md]

Build distinct blue-black/navy layers and reserve cobalt/royal-blue blocks for major hero, CTA or feature moments. White/pale-blue text and thin outer rails/corner markers keep the system crisp. Local blooms or tiny status lights supply restrained depth. Tune the size/frequency of blue blocks, navy separation, rail visibility and glow; do not flood every surface with saturated blue or flatten the whole page to pure black.

### Monochrome dark glass

[MengTo, dark-glass-clean-layout, SKILL.md]

Place a centered framed shell over soft atmospheric light or a blurred procedural field. Dark frosted navigation, sidebars, data nodes and cards support a quiet central stage. White, zinc, smoke and charcoal dominate; any brand accent remains small and purposeful. Tune glass opacity, blur, background activity, rail density and module spacing. Avoid competing translucent layers that muddy text or fill all available negative space.

### Dither and laser

[MengTo, dither-laser-dark-mode, SKILL.md]

Give near-black surfaces material through low-priority ordered dither, coarse pixels or soft grain. Add a narrow near-white-core beam with halo/haze tinted by the actual brand accent, then crisp muted-edge panels above it. Tune dither scale/strength, beam placement, haze and contrast separately; not every surface needs both texture and glow. The source Cedar Lodges demo is a separate composition from this authored dark-laser system.

### Lime instrument system

[MengTo, bright-green-tech-system-webgl, SKILL.md]

Pair a charcoal technical grid and highly visible rails/brackets/dividers with bright lime or acid-green signals. One panel carries content, instructions and metadata; the other hosts an integrated geometry/globe/particle instrument rather than unrelated wallpaper. Restrict vivid green to active indicators, progress, focal type and selected geometry. Tune line density, split ratio, signal coverage and visualization activity; keep a usable static/text equivalent when graphics are unavailable.

### Restrained emerald system

[MengTo, tech-green-dark-mode-modern, SKILL.md]

Use matte black/dark-glass hierarchy with mint/emerald selection, status and graph emphasis. Dashboard-like modules, mono IDs/timestamps, thin gradient shells, brackets/ticks and faint grids make the system analytical. Local underglow or a small inner highlight can distinguish one focal card. Tune tonal lift, green intensity, technical density and glow radius; avoid both radioactive green washes and an undifferentiated black grid.

### Silver framed modules

[MengTo, framed-tech-dark-border-gradient, SKILL.md]

Use an outer technical shell, edge rails, tiny corners and 1-pixel gradient borders across selected containers, buttons and rows. Quiet sans, mono labels and compact uppercase headings sit on separated charcoal layers. Keep black/smoke/white/silver dominant and brand accents rare. Tune edge highlight, contrast between page/panel/row, and metadata density. Border detail should express hierarchy rather than making every rectangle equally luminous.

### Purple creative network

[MengTo, funky-purple-container-tech, SKILL.md]

Large framed near-black containers organize purple/fuchsia/magenta search fields, inset modules, floating cards and side rails. Blue undertones can add depth. Preserve precise borders/corners/spacing while allowing a single more expressive orb, text ring or lighting bloom to identify a creative platform. Tune saturation, container scale, rounding and effect count together; the preset's allowed purple is a contextual direction, not a reason to override a project's established palette or add decorative AI claims.

### Glass dial and clock

[MengTo, glass-dark-mode-clock, SKILL.md]

A monochrome circular instrument anchors a near-black grid with faint beam guides and crosshairs. Rings, ticks, degrees or rotating text supply clock/calibration meaning; frosted nav and controls use restrained reflections and thin white edge gradients. Tune dial scale, annotation density, glass opacity and white-glow strength. A decorative timepiece must not impersonate a live measurement or require animation to communicate its label/value.

### Contrast and molded surfaces

[MengTo, high-contrast-skeuomorphic-clean, SKILL.md]

A light neutral exterior frames a deep charcoal application shell. Vertical/radial surface gradients, inset highlights, inner shadows and reflective edges give nested modules physical depth. One small brand signal drives status/progress; clean sans and occasional mono labels keep the presentation operational. Tune outer/inner contrast, depth, material and accent coverage. Real controls and accessible state cues still determine behavior; the source's decorative sliders are not functional implementations.

### Wireframe diagnostic

[MengTo, technical-wireframe-info-layout, SKILL.md]

Use near-black monochrome with an exploded/layered outlined object as the explanatory focus. Route thin connectors from meaningful regions to sparse labels; support with compact allocation/status/legend widgets. Distinguish parts through brightness, line weight and spacing rather than saturated color or filled glossy rendering. Tune object complexity, annotation count, contrast and slow staged motion. Keep diagram text equivalents, small-screen access and the actual scroll container; do not inherit the demo's clipped viewport or hidden mobile information.

## Framing and dimensional details

### Content rails and numbers

[MengTo, container-lines; number-details, SKILL.md]

Align rails to the real content container. The source baseline is max-width 1120 with gutter `clamp(20px,4vw,48px)`: content width `min(W-2*pad,1120)` and rail left `max(pad,(W-1120)/2)`. Keep the right boundary symmetric. Use the actual layout/scroll container rather than assuming 100vw matches its usable width. A 1-pixel low-alpha line and 6-pixel square marker are source starts; marker tuning 4 to 8. Put decoration in a noninteractive layer with `pointer-events:none`, and isolate stacking where needed.

Two-digit secondary numbers can align at card corners, gutters, rails or headings with consistent mono/narrow typography. Their low visual emphasis must not reduce contrast for necessary text. Decorative numbering should not duplicate spoken headings; semantic step numbers remain meaningful content.

### Framed grid

[MengTo, framed-grid-layout, SKILL.md]

The source uses 12 columns, 16 gap, padding `clamp(16px,2vw,28px)`, 18 radius and a 760 breakpoint for simpler stacking. Eight background-gradient layers can form 1-pixel brackets; diagonal texture repeats every 11 to 12 units at less than .05 alpha. Align those marks to component boundaries, not arbitrary viewport edges. Let real content determine responsive height and column collapse instead of inheriting demo 100vh/100vw constraints.

### Chamfered corners

[MengTo, corner-diagonals, SKILL.md]

Choose one polygon family: all four corners, or opposing top-left/bottom-right, or top-right/bottom-left. Source cuts 8/14/24 correspond to small/medium/large; tuning ranges 6 to 10 for controls, 12 to 18 for cards, 20 to 32 for large panels. A 1-pixel outer shell and inner fill share the polygon, with content padding `clamp(16px,2.4vw,28px)`. Source line alphas .18/.34, fill rgba(10, 14, 24, .82), accent `#8b5cf6` are optional tokens.

The source button uses 44 height, gap 8, horizontal padding 18, a 135 degree accent-to 28%-black gradient and 1.08 hover brightness. Treat 44 as its touch-size preference, not the universal WCAG minimum. The source 2-pixel focus outline with 3 offset can be clipped by the same polygon; put visible focus on an unclipped wrapper or another verified outline treatment. Keep shape stable during hover: brighten fill, border or overlay rather than changing hit geometry.

### Corner laser junction

[MengTo, corner-lasers, SKILL.md]

Converge 2 to 3 thin rays on one bright corner emitter. Separate near-white core thickness from the broader accent halo, and concentrate bloom at the junction. Atmospheric fog follows that same emitter/accent so the rays occupy one space. A line-distance shader can produce a sharp core plus a softer falloff; choose its palette from the project rather than hardcoding blue. Tune ray angles/core/halo/fog independently and keep content readability first.

### Dark glass panel

[MengTo, glass-dark-ui, SKILL.md]

Source tokens: backgrounds `#020617`/`#0b1220`, glass rgba(15, 23, 42, .45), strong fallback .62, text `#e2e8f0`, muted `#94a3b8`, accent `#60a5fa`. Panel radius 24, blur 18, saturation 140%, top-to-bottom white overlay .08 to .02, shadow 0/20/48 in rgba(2, 6, 23, .45), inset white top edge .12.

The source perimeter is 1 pixel wide with 1 pixel inset padding and a 145 degree gradient: slate .28, blue .36 at 45%, purple .3 at 70%, slate .18 at 100%. Keep it noninteractive with `pointer-events:none`. Use the canonical [masked border mechanism](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/web-ui/modern-css/GRADIENTS-AND-EFFECTS.md#masked-border), including support/fallback behavior, for its implementation. These are source-specific visual parameters rather than a second mechanism definition.

The source's text-color floor `#cbd5e1` does not prove contrast and even differs from its muted token. Check actual composited foreground/background; strengthen fill when blur is unavailable and keep focus visible above the decoration.

### Physical raised and pressed material

[MengTo, skeuomorphic-ui, SKILL.md]

Choose one physical material per component. Model light from above with a brighter-top/darker-base gradient, 1-pixel reflective edge, outer elevation and inner carved shadows. Use tinted darks rather than pure-black shadow. Source transitions 160 to 240 ms, radius 22, and pressed translation 1 pixel; pressed states reverse depth using inset shade. Heavy pressing belongs to selected/active states, not every surface. Texture, dots, seams and embossed text/icon edges must survive actual control size; do not mix this component's material with unrelated glass/neumorphic treatments.

Source base ramp `#f8fafc` / `#e9eef5` / `#cfd7e4`; top edge rgba(255, 255, 255, .82), bottom edge rgba(79, 93, 122, .34), shadows rgba(31, 41, 55, .18/.28), highlight white .72. Raised ramp changes near 48%, edge near 45%; shadows 0/18/34 and 0/5/12. Pressed ramp `#d5dce8`/`#eef2f7`/`#f8fafc` near 52%, with inset 0/4/10 at .22, inset lower white .72 and outer 0/4/10 at .1. Label `#334155` uses white .78 at 1 pixel below and dark .12 at 1 pixel above; the corresponding icon drop shadows use white .78 and dark .14. These are optional surface tokens, not replacements for protected control motion.

## Source implementation corrections

[MengTo, clean-minimal-beige-light-mode; company-logos; shared style demos; primary documentation]

Several raw shaders use the reversed-edge form `smoothstep(high,low,x)`. Khronos defines its result as undefined when edge0>=edge1. Use the corrected inverse ramp owned by [material studies](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/web-ui/webgl/MATERIAL-STUDIES.md#paper-one-field-torn-outline-and-thickness). See [Khronos smoothstep](https://registry.khronos.org/OpenGL-Refpages/es3.0/html/smoothstep.xhtml).

The beige demo uses `tanh` in a WebGL 1 shader. It is unavailable in GLSL ES 1.00; keep the shader compatible with its actual context or choose a deliberately supported context. See [Khronos tanh version support](https://registry.khronos.org/OpenGL-Refpages/es3.0/html/tanh.xhtml).

The logo/clock demo combines physical `gl_FragCoord` with CSS-sized resolution at DPR 2. Match uniforms to actual framebuffer dimensions, which can differ from requested canvas dimensions. Check [drawingBufferWidth](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGLRenderingContext/drawingBufferWidth), [shader COMPILE_STATUS](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGLRenderingContext/getShaderParameter) and [program LINK_STATUS](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGLRenderingContext/getProgramParameter). These corrections explain source defects; they are not a claim that a target shader was run.

## Prove it

[MengTo, style SKILL.md Validate/Quick Checks; anti-slop-ui integration validation]

- Record the selected direction and deliberate deviations, including preserved owner tokens, installed libraries and the source-specific preferences being declined.
- Check full-page hierarchy, actual media crop, small-screen reading order, real controls, focus, composited contrast, 200% zoom and a complete static/reduced-motion presentation.
- Measure decorative rendering only where it exists: actual framebuffer dimensions, compile/link results, elapsed-time motion and lifecycle cleanup. A recipe or raw demo is not runtime evidence.
- Report what was actually viewed or measured and what remains unrun. Do not import source customer metrics, ratings, security badges or production-readiness claims as facts about the target.
