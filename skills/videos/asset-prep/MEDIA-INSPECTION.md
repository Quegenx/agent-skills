# Inspecting supplied media

Use before transforming existing footage. These checks come from [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill](https://github.com/kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill), `scripts/_common/probe.py`, `scripts/color.py` and `references/gotchas.md`, with FFmpeg's official forms taking precedence over source generalizations.

## Record what the edit must preserve

- Record stored dimensions, sample aspect ratio and rotation-adjusted display dimensions separately; a portrait display does not establish the stored frame's shape. Name the coordinate basis for crop and layout: the source's geometry edits use a square-pixel displayed frame. Changing an aspect tag alone does not resample the image. Inventory audio/subtitle/data streams, channel layouts, language tags and the track actually selected for the edit. An audio stream's presence alone does not prove the requested track survived. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/probe.py; tests/test_picture.py; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-all.html#setdar_002c-setsar]
- Do not assume automatic selection means the first track: FFmpeg normally chooses the highest-resolution video and audio with the most channels, with the lowest index breaking a tie; data and attachment streams require explicit mapping. Preserve or explicitly report omitted tracks. [https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Automatic-stream-selection]
- An aspect-ratio crop and a caller-specified pixel rectangle are different jobs. FFmpeg crop defaults `exact` to 0, allowing subsampled width/height/x/y to round down; use the documented exact behavior when the promised coordinates require it. The old line said source `crop.py` omitted `exact=1`; supplied version 3.9.2 now requests it explicitly. [https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-all.html#crop; kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/crop.py; tests/test_editing.py]
- When preservation matters, also inventory chapters, cover artwork, attachments, extra playable video, creation time and the output's first-frame timecode. The source's `source_carry` report mostly compares counts or presence, with an expected-value comparison for timecode; it does not prove correct cue/chapter timing, tag values or retained content. Multiple media sources omit that report, and neither its absence nor `verified` establishes retention. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/probe.py; scripts/_common/emit.py; tests/test_delivery.py; tests/test_orchestration.py]
- Choose straight or premultiplied alpha and intentional flattening explicitly. An alpha plane can be entirely opaque. The source compares each decoded frame's mean alpha after reducing it to 64x36, pairing by index when counts match and using aggregate statistics otherwise; this cannot prove spatial coverage or edge preservation. A grade expects unchanged coverage, while composition can add coverage. Inspect transparent portions over the sequence using the [delivery checks](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/videos/frame-review/DELIVERY-CHECKS.md#evidence-limits). [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/alpha.py; scripts/export.py; tests/test_delivery.py]
- Record stream start offsets and individual stream lengths, alongside container duration. A file duration alone can hide shorter picture, longer sound or an initial gap. Final evidence belongs to [decoded-delivery checks](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/videos/frame-review/DELIVERY-CHECKS.md#evidence-limits). [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/emit.py; tests/test_editing.py]
- Keep unknown capability distinct from missing capability. A filter or hardware encoder listed in a build does not establish that the current fonts, GPU or driver can complete this job. Preserve actual requested, used and fallback paths rather than reporting a successful retry that was never measured. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_contract.py; tests/test_accel.py; references/ci-platform-pitfalls.md]

## Color descriptions and transformations

- Record bit depth, primaries, transfer, matrix and range separately. Ten-bit storage alone does not establish HDR; the source device corpus includes ten-bit SDR. The source distinguishes PQ/HLG/Dolby Vision from BT.2020 SDR; BT.2020 primaries alone are not its HDR classification. Its BT.2020 SDR conversion uses gamut conversion rather than the PQ tone-map path. Do not infer camera Log encoding from a flat, low-saturation sample alone: the source's Log detector is a heuristic. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/probe.py; scripts/color.py; references/gotchas.md; references/devices.md]
- Retagging and conversion are different operations. Official `setparams` changes properties used by filters/encoders without changing input image values. Select a conversion from the observed source properties and intended delivery, then inspect decoded output. The old supplied-footage table prescribed H.264 limited-range conversion generically for phone clips; source properties and the renderer contract decide the required conversion. [https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-all.html#setparams; kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/color.py]
- FFmpeg's CPU `tonemap` expects single-precision floating-point data in linear light; linearize before it and convert its output to the intended usable format. A color tag alone does not perform that transformation. Source toolkit help calls retagging a no-reencode operation, but its fallback can re-encode: report the path actually used. [https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-all.html#tonemap; kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/color.py]

For a requested broadcast code-range check, identify its standard and bit depth before measuring. The source counts out-of-range luma and chroma samples per frame and reports incomplete decoding as unmeasured; its Y'CbCr code-range check does not establish RGB gamut compliance. Re-measure after conversion, subsampling and encoding rather than treating a pre-encode clamp as the delivery result. Its thresholds remain source policy until checked against the requested standard. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, check.py and color.py]

## Keep evidence within its measured scope

- Black, frozen, silent, dropout and flash detectors flag measurements for review. A hold, deliberate silence, camera flash or strobe can be intentional; the source's local thresholds do not decide whether the content is wrong. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, check.py]
- Nominal/average frame-rate disagreement is suspicion, and packet timestamps sampled in windows establish only those windows. Record the sampling scope instead of claiming whole-file constant cadence. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/probe.py; scripts/cut.py; tests/test_cut_copy.py]
- Distinguish full content hashes from partial head/tail fingerprints and size/mtime checks. Bind the edit to the source actually inspected, but do not call a partial fingerprint whole-file content authentication. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/probe.py; scripts/batch.py]
- Do not silently trim, crop, drop motion samples or change ambience loudness to satisfy a preset. Keep platform size, duration, loudness and caption-safe presets labeled as local policies until checked against current official guidance for the actual upload route. A passing preset check is not evidence of platform acceptance. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_platforms.py; scripts/check.py; references/workflow.md; https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-tools-on-tiktok]

## Prove it

The source and selected streams are identified; stored/displayed geometry, sample aspect ratio, stream spans, preservation expectations, alpha intent and color properties are recorded; metadata relabeling is distinguished from image conversion; missing measurements, sampling limits and actual fallbacks are explicit. Inspect the [decoded delivery](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/videos/frame-review/DELIVERY-CHECKS.md) after the edit. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, scripts/_common/probe.py; scripts/_common/alpha.py; scripts/_common/emit.py; tests/test_analysis.py; tests/test_delivery.py]
