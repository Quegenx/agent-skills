# Restore and reframe supplied footage

Use when supplied footage needs deinterlacing, noise reduction, stabilization, known-region masking or a corrected viewport. These workflows come from [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill](https://github.com/kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill), `references/scripts.md` and the corresponding editing scripts, with the official FFmpeg filter manual qualifying each API. Inspect [MEDIA-INSPECTION.md](MEDIA-INSPECTION.md) first; timing, captions and final delivery stay in [FOOTAGE-EDITS.md](FOOTAGE-EDITS.md).

## Choose the measured repair

- Inspect moving frames for combing before deinterlacing. Choose one output frame per input frame or per field, record the resulting rate and field order, and inspect moving edges afterward. Yadif's automatic parity uses decoder information and assumes top-field-first when it is unknown; processing only marked interlaced frames trusts those markings. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, deinterlace.py; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#yadif]
- Picture denoising and audio cleanup are separate operations. The source's `hqdn3d` path controls spatial and temporal luma/chroma strength; stronger cleaning can soften fine detail. Compare the chosen treatment against the source before applying it throughout. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, denoise.py; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#hqdn3d]
- Stabilization needs motion analysis followed by a transform pass and the required filter build. Choose smoothing and border handling deliberately, then inspect actual motion and crop loss. Official `vidstabtransform` has separate manual `zoom` and `optzoom`; the supplied tool disables optimal zoom and leaves edges visible at its zero-zoom default. A completed encode does not prove reduced shake. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, stabilize.py; references/ci-platform-pitfalls.md, macOS; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#vidstabdetect; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#vidstabtransform]
- For a known redaction box, choose blur or pixelation and inspect its coverage throughout the intended interval. The supplied tool does not locate or track faces, plates or text. Its delogo mode interpolates from the surrounding border; it does not reconstruct hidden content and is meant for a fixed region. Respect the selected implementation's edge and pixel-format restrictions. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, redact.py; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#delogo]

## Choose the viewport

- For an aspect change, choose crop, padding or blurred fill deliberately and inspect which source edges or subjects remain visible. A centered crop does not establish that the intended subject is kept; the source's measured picture rectangle still leaves subject framing unverified. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/gotchas.md, Reframing, fps and duration; references/scripts.md, fit.py]
- Black-bar detection samples windows and proposes a rectangle; genuine letterboxing can produce the same result as unwanted bars. View the frames before cropping and report failed or unmeasured windows. A motion centroid is a motion measurement, not a subject identity or tracking decision. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, cropdetect.py]
- Horizon correction rotates by an explicit angle. Choose crop-to-fill, which loses a border, or padding that keeps the picture and exposes filled corners; the source does not measure the tilt for you. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, straighten.py; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#rotate]
- For a flat view from spherical footage, establish the source projection and stereo layout, then choose yaw, pitch, roll and field of view. A wrong projection can produce distorted pixels without an error; the source's fixed viewport does not identify or follow a subject. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md, sphere.py; https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#v360]

## Prove it

Compare the repair with the source over the relevant moving frames. Report actual output rate, geometry, crop/edge loss, measured capability and any uninspected interval; inspect sound, retained streams and captions through the existing delivery checks. A detected rectangle, declared filter or successful encode alone does not establish the intended repair. [kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill, references/scripts.md; references/ci-platform-pitfalls.md]
