# Screenshot art directions

Eighteen optional directions from the supplied `app-store-screenshots/style-prompts/` guides. These are the author's screenshot-art recipes, not platform rules, conversion evidence, product templates or instructions to redesign the actual app. Choose a direction for the user's own brand and keep [production and export review](SCREENSHOT-PRODUCTION.md) separate from the style choice. ## Resolve recipe conflicts before applying them

- The source SKILL calls plain white amateur, asks for mixed headline emphasis and adds a decorative accent on most slides. Existing [marketing-images](https://github.com/Quegenx/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/marketing/marketing-images/SKILL.md#blog-heroes-and-social-graphics) says brand direction comes from the brand. Keep that existing rule: these source preferences are options inside the chosen art direction, not universal corrections to an approved design. [app-store-screenshots, SKILL.md, Visual Design Principles; coreyhaines31/marketingskills, image]
- The source quality bar mandates one default phone bezel; the main guide offers frameless, tilted and full-device treatments, while the paper and quiet guides explore different visual restraint. Treat those as conflicting author recipes and select the approved treatment. Usage of Apple-provided device art still follows [VISUALS.md](VISUALS.md#rules-for-every-asset). [app-store-screenshots, style-prompts/_QUALITY_BAR.md, Phone treatment; SKILL.md, Phone framing; style-prompts/03-paper-sticker-skeuomorphic.md; style-prompts/17-quiet-japandi.md]
- The quality bar rejects flat fills and asks illustration to pass shading, texture and volume gates; those gates describe the author's preferred illustration polish. Existing brand direction remains authoritative when the approved style is deliberately flat or restrained. Do not make a shaded mascot compulsory across the other styles. [app-store-screenshots, style-prompts/_QUALITY_BAR.md, Illustration and SVG quality bar; coreyhaines31/marketingskills, image]
- Source headline proportions, decoration counts, font choices and color swatches are recipe choices. Store constraints, real app UI and [measured fit/contrast](SCREENSHOT-PRODUCTION.md#inspect-the-final-raster) take precedence; neither a palette label nor a reference thumbnail establishes accessibility or store acceptance. [app-store-screenshots, SKILL.md, Visual Design Principles; style-prompts/_QUALITY_BAR.md; https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en; https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html]

## Prove it

- A direction was selected for the actual brand, using its own assets and unaltered product captures; reference products, mascots, wordmarks and sample identities were not imported. [app-store-screenshots, SKILL.md, Migration Path and Visual Design Principles; https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata]
- Surface, typography, composition and decoration express that direction without defeating the headline or UI. The real standalone exports pass [the production checks](SCREENSHOT-PRODUCTION.md#prove-it). [app-store-screenshots, style-prompts/_QUALITY_BAR.md, Auto-reject checks and The thumbnail test]
- Conflicting source recipes were resolved as an explicit art choice; no universal author preference silently overrode the user's production design or official store policy. [app-store-screenshots, SKILL.md, user-instruction override; style-prompts/_QUALITY_BAR.md]
