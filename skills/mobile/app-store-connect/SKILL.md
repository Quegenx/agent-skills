# App Store Connect

App Store Connect is where an Apple-platform app gets its record, builds, testers, product page, products and review submissions. Three things decide most outcomes: whether the build meets Apple's current upload minimums and carries a valid privacy manifest (App Store Connect rejects it at upload otherwise), whether App Review can reach every feature and purchase with the demo account and notes you gave it, and whether the metadata, purchases and privacy answers match what the app really does. Apple's own documentation and the App Review Guidelines (current text dated October 16, 2026) are the authority here. The `asc` CLI (rorkai) and the preflight rules and `greenlight` scanner (truongduy2611, RevylAI) are third-party: their facts are tagged in each file, and where they differ from Apple, Apple wins. ## The rules

1. **Build to Apple's current upload minimums,** and check `https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/` before each release: Xcode 26 with a 26 SDK since April 28, 2026, a deployment target of iOS 13 or later since September 9, 2026, and Xcode 27 with the 27 SDKs and a target of iOS 15 or later from April 2027. Increment the build string before every upload.
2. **Ship a valid privacy manifest.** Every required reason API your code uses is declared in `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` (for Flutter, `ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`), each listed third-party SDK carries its own valid, signed manifest, and every tracking domain is listed when `NSPrivacyTracking` is true.
3. **Unlock anything digital with in-app purchase (3.1.1).** Auto-renewable subscriptions last at least seven days, give ongoing value and say what the user gets before asking (3.1.2). Outside the United States storefront, no button, link or call to action points to another purchase method unless the app holds a regional entitlement (3.1.1(a)).
4. **Give App Review full access (2.1).** Submit a final, on-device-tested build with the backend live, a demo account that must not expire (or an approved demo mode), every configured in-app purchase reachable, and review notes that describe new and non-obvious features specifically, not generically (2.3.1(a)).
5. **Keep metadata accurate and inside its limits (2.3).** Name and subtitle at most 30 characters, apply the qualified price/off-type rule and approved interactive-functionality exception rather than a blanket ban ([metadata policy](REVIEW-GUIDELINES.md#23-accurate-metadata), 2.3.7, 2.3.10), screenshots that show the app in use (2.3.3), and previews made only of screen captures of the app (2.3.4).
6. **Make the app's privacy match its answers.** Link the privacy policy in App Store Connect and inside the app (5.1.1(i)); offer account deletion in the app when it supports account creation (5.1.1(v)); disclose and get explicit permission before sharing personal data with third parties, including third-party AI (5.1.2(i)); ask through ATT before tracking; and pair a third-party or social login for the primary account with an equivalent private login option (4.8).
7. **Release forward only.** "It's not possible to revert to a previous version on the App Store": ship a fix as a new version, and use phased release for updates when you want automatic updates throttled over 7 days.
8. **Never commit or ship an App Store Connect API key,** and with `asc` (third-party) resolve IDs with `--paginate`, run the dry run, then `--confirm`, and read the state back rather than trusting a receipt.

## Prove it

Apply build, metadata, privacy and review checks to the selected job. `asc` validation applies when using that CLI; `greenlight` preflight applies when using that scanner. For the fastlane lane, use [FASTLANE.md](FASTLANE.md) and its checks. A metadata-only task does not require a new archive or release. [rorkai, asc; truongduy2611; RevylAI; FASTLANE.md]

The archive's Xcode, SDK and deployment target meet the current minimums row; App Store Connect sends no invalid-manifest email after upload; the strict `asc validate` for the version reports no blocking issues and the build shows `VALID`; a reviewer with only the demo account and notes reaches every feature and in-app purchase; `greenlight preflight .` reports GREENLIT; no `.p8` key is in the repository, client code or logs; and every file's own Prove it checks pass for the parts you touched.
